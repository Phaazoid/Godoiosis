## Weapons are template + instance, and the attack is its own content

**Weapons are template + instance, and the attack is its own content (#59 then #72, mirrors the jobs id-reference pattern + the rune/carving system):** `AttackData` (`extends Resource`) is the shared skeleton anything a unit can fire carries — `display_name`, `power`, `attack_pattern`, `can_counter`, `hits_allies`, `hits_self` (#123: the attacker is a legal victim of its own attack only when this is true — `RulesService.is_attack_victim` branches on `unit == attacker` and asks it instead of refusing unconditionally; `heal2.tres` is the first user. *(Corrected 2026-10-20: there is no `attack_pattern` field any more. Since #808 an attack's geometry is `min_range` / `max_range` / `max_and_a_half` plus `attack_shape: AttackShape`.)* **A player-AIMED heal keeps its enemy splash on purpose** — healing an enemy is a niche the player may want, dev call 2026-08-07. The `allies_only` opt-in #148 gave that predicate for its reactive heal went with the heal's repeal (#1135)), `targets` (**a MAP-only attack hits NO unit since [#1135](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1135), 2026-09-28** — `is_attack_victim` refuses every unit for it, its caster included, so nothing but its deposit lands; the one exception is a SHOCK's current, tag-blind by the dev's ruling), **`damage_kind`** ([#424](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/424), 2026-09-06: the delivery KIND armour answers — `AttackData.Kind`, blunt / slash / pierce / fire / shock / cold / corrosion, and NONE which is never authored: `deliver()` answers it for heals and utility attacks, the one home for that rule, and `AttackLint` BLOCKS a damaging attack stored as NONE. AUTHORED on every attack and carving, never derived from element or family — *"ice spear from just ice would be pierce, whereas sheer cold... would fall under energy"* — and SEPARATE from the element set: a fireball deals FIRE-kind damage and separately applies the fire effect. A fitted mod may REPLACE it through `WeaponInstance.effective_kind`; a second kind-changer is refused at the fit so fitting order can never decide what armour does to a swing. Armour lists the kinds its DEF covers (`ArmorData.covered_kinds`, empty = all) and `RulesService.def_against` layers that gate on `def_breakdown` — one composition for the panel and the resolver; Cover and brace are kind-blind.

**Insulation strips the EFFECT and nothing else since the same pass: the #90 never-arrives outcome is REPEALED**, and immunity to the damage is spelled as DEF covering that kind. **AMENDED for the GROUND by [#892](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/892), 2026-09-11:** that rule governs a DELIVERED hit -- one carrying a kind, resolved through `PlanResolver`, that `def_against` gets a look at. The end-of-turn tile burn is none of those (`TileHitAction.make` sets the damage directly, no kind, DEF never sees it), so there is no second half to offer and fire insulation stops the burn OUTRIGHT. The gate is `RulesService.occupant_damage_for`, the per-unit layer over `Terrain.occupant_damage`, asked by BOTH the queue forecast and the end-of-turn pass -- `can_traverse`'s home, for #115's reason: the burn is derived twice, so an immunity either derivation owned privately is one the other lies about. *(Since [#1331](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1331), 2026-10-22, the same holds for a gas's end-of-turn scald: `RulesService.gas_damage_for` zeroes it outright for a unit insulated against the gas's `damage_element`, which for steam is HEAT.)* First content: the Asbestos Shroud, which grants `Abilities.Id.INSULATED_FIRE` (and `INSULATED_HEAT` beside it since 2026-10-12, one ability per element) and is the first authored piece whose `covered_kinds` names a non-physical kind), **`heals`** (BUILT 2026-07-30: an attack is EITHER damage OR a heal, never both — the heal reinterprets the same base-damage number as HP restored, capped at max HP, short-circuiting `PlanResolver._resolve_one` before DEF/elemental/lethality/knockback ever run, since none of those are questions a heal raises; see [`docs/design/resolution-pipeline.md`](../design/resolution-pipeline.md)'s 2026-07-30 amendment), and **`deals_no_damage`** ([#126](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/126), 2026-08-08: a PURE-UTILITY attack — **SCALING is suppressed**, so both `TransmutationData.base_damage` and `WeaponInstance.base_damage` return 0 and neither aura nor the family's stat blend can sneak damage into a damageless effect. It exists because a damageless attack is otherwise barely authorable: channeling anchors on real aura in one of the carving's elements (anchor + wildcards, 2026-08-10; it required temper aura outright before that), so the alchemists who fire an AIR carving are mostly the ones whose aura would arm it.

**Read at the two `base_damage` implementations and NOWHERE else** — the resolver deliberately does not clamp, so an elemental reaction's `damage_bonus` still lands (dev: that bonus is not part of the attack, it is what the world did with the element it carried, and dodging it is the player's job). *(Corrected 2026-10-20: `deals_no_damage` is also read by `PlanResolver` (a Guard lets it pass), `AttackData.deliver`, `WeaponInstance.unwielded_damage` and `AudioDirector`. The zeroing itself still happens only in the damage functions.)* Mutually exclusive with `heals` — two booleans over one three-state question (damages / heals / neither), declared rather than collapsed into an enum, because `heals` has 8 production readers. First content: `Resources/TransmutationData/Gust.tres`) — with damage math deliberately OFF the base (carvings scale off aura, a weapon attack off its OWN blend + the firing weapon's mods — **the blend MOVED onto `WeaponAttackData` in [#485](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/485), 2026-08-25, and `WeaponData` no longer has the field at all**: per family, every attack a weapon owned scaled identically, so a mod-granted attack could not bring its own scaling and there was no reason to prefer one attack over another. A family's blend IS its main attack's — no fallback on the template, or that is a second answer to what an attack scales off. Weights are NORMALISED at read time, so any total is legal arithmetic and only `Stats.BLEND_TOTAL` makes the numbers mean what they say; `WeaponInstance.effective_blend` is the one composition, read by both `scaling_contribution` and the attack tooltip so the number shown and the number taken cannot disagree, and it clamps a weight at zero — a nudge authored against a family that uses STR, fitted to one that does not, otherwise subtracts the wielder's own stat from their damage). `WeaponAttackData extends AttackData` adds `elemental_damage_type` and `scaling_blend` (#485 -- moved off `WeaponData`, which no longer has it); `TransmutationData` (the rune carving) is its sibling subclass. `WeaponData` is the shared family TEMPLATE (family base like `ChainSword.tres`, or a named prototype) — `main_attack: WeaponAttackData` (the one REQUIRED attack, a curated resource the family points at — never a bare array slot — used by default aim and ALWAYS by counters) + `extra_attacks: Array[WeaponAttackData]` (no count cap; mod spaces gate access), `weapon_type`, `is_prototype`, **`mod_spaces: Array[int]`** (one capacity per mod space — AUTHORED per template since [#486](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/486), replacing a hardcoded `is_prototype` fork that forced every prototype to a single size-1 space, so "weaker but roomier" was unrepresentable; `SPACE_CAPACITIES` survives as the DEFAULT, and a template holding it leaves `mod_spaces` out of its file and follows a retune, prototypes included ([#1185](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1185), dev 2026-10-03).

**It is spelled as a typed copy, `Array(SPACE_CAPACITIES, TYPE_INT, &"", null)`, and both halves are measured:** the bare const is READ-ONLY and refuses every in-place edit, and a `.duplicate()` CALL gives the saver no default to compare against, so every save pinned the spaces. No count cap, which is what turned `WeaponInstance`'s three named fields into one array. Consequence: `is_prototype` now has ZERO mechanical readers and is identity + save folder only), and the `weight` it inherits from `Item` (was its own `base_weight` until 2026-07-27). It is NOT equippable (extends `Item`, not `EquippableData`) and is never duplicated — a unit that owns a template edits it live for every weapon built on it, main attack included. `WeaponInstance` (extends `EquippableData`, what a unit actually carries) holds a direct `template: WeaponData` ref plus its own `spaces` (one inner array of fitted `WeaponModData` per OCCUPIED mod space, the only true per-weapon state — **`spaces.size()` is one past the last space holding a mod and `[]` means nothing is fitted, deliberately NOT a mirror of `template.mod_spaces.size()`; `fit` is the one door that grows it and a READ never does ([#624](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/624)), or a weapon's saved form records what has looked at it rather than what is on it. `weapons.md` → *A weapon's saved form is a property of the weapon*.**) and computes the effective view (`base_damage`/`get_elements`/`hits_map`, all taking an optional `attack: WeaponAttackData` override that defaults to main when null) from template + active modules.

**A mod GRANTS as well as modifies since [#74](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/74) (2026-08-23)** — `granted_attacks` / `granted_abilities` / `stat_modifiers`, all additive; **since [#529](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/529) + [#530](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/530) (2026-08-26) a mod also REPLACES the main (`replaces_main`, composed by `WeaponInstance.effective_main` -- the three surfaces that read `template.main_attack` directly now route through it, and `secondary_attacks` was the sharpest, since filtering by the OLD main listed a replacement in the submenu as well as firing it from Attack) and EDITS the shove and ally splash of whatever `applies_to` names. `applies_to` is ONE selector governing every effect a mod carries, `EVERY_ATTACK` being both the storage's zero and the pre-#530 behaviour; GRANTS stay outside it, since which attacks a mod CHANGES and which it ADDS are different questions. An override may only touch what is read AFTER the attack is chosen -- anything changing how an attack is AIMED is a whole replacement, because `Reach._pattern_of` takes only the attack and `is_directional_attack` has no unit. *(Corrected 2026-10-20: `Reach._pattern_of` was removed by #808. Its successor is `Reach._place(attack, anchor, dir)`, which still takes only the attack, so the argument stands.)* A second replacer is refused at the fit; `hits_allies_override`'s OFF beats ON whatever space it sits in (law #1)** , all proficiency-gated through `active_modules` exactly as power is.

**Fitting became player-facing in [#732](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/732) (2026-09-06)** and brought two rules with it: a weapon takes ONE of any given mod (refused between family and the replacer clause, so a fitted replacer dragged to another space is not blamed for colliding with itself), and what a mod NEEDS is `lowest_space_for` -- the lowest space whose CAPACITY admits its size, ignoring fill, since proficiency N activates spaces 1..N and a requirement that moved as spaces filled would be a fact about the weapon wearing a fact about the mod. `unfit` is `fit`'s mirror for a caller holding the mod rather than an index; `active_space_count(null)` reads UNREDUCED, because nobody's proficiency applies to a weapon nobody holds and the card opens on stash gear.

**Its scaling half is `scaling_change` since #74 (2026-08-25, ex-`scaling_nudge`): a `+/-` percentage-point SHIFT against the family main attack's blend, composed in `WeaponInstance.effective_blend`. The dev authors ABSOLUTE percentages on the #485 sliders and the tool stores the difference -- a delta rather than an absolute is load-bearing once the blend is per-ATTACK, since an absolute would force every attack the weapon fires onto identical numbers. `WeaponModData.family` is what the shift is measured against, and its restriction is DERIVED (`requires_family`): a non-empty `scaling_change` REQUIRES a family, refused at the save by `save_block_reason`; a family set on any mod is a fitting lock either way. Fitting gained a reason channel with it -- **`WeaponInstance.fit_block_reason` is the one answer and `can_fit` is DERIVED from it** (#166's shape), FAMILY asked before capacity because a wrong family can never be fixed on this weapon while a full space can, which is why the picker HIDES what the family refuses and merely refuses, with the reason, what capacity does.** **Proficiency's default is `UnitInstance.UNREDUCED` (-1, an absent key) rather than the number 3 since #486** — the default has always MEANT "no reduction", and a fixed number can only spell that while three is the maximum any weapon has; with `mod_spaces` authored, a five-space prototype would have shipped with two spaces no unit could ever reach. `set_proficiency` erases on a negative rather than storing, so "back to unreduced" stays expressible, and its old upper clamp of 3 is GONE — `active_space_count` already floors against the template's own count, so a proficiency above what any weapon has is simply unreduced. The composition splits on WHOSE fact it is: an attack belongs to the weapon that fires it, so `available_attacks` reads only its OWN fitted mods; abilities and wielder stats describe the UNIT, so `Unit.get_live_abilities`/`_gear_modifier` union across **`Unit._mod_sources()`** — the one answer to which weapons contribute, being the equipped weapon plus every installed prosthetic, **deduped**, because `add_item` auto-equips the first weapon-shaped equippable and a `ProstheticWeaponInstance` qualifies, so one object can fill both roles and would otherwise pay out twice.

**Never `duplicate(true)` a `WeaponInstance`** — that deep-copies the shared template and severs the sync; use `copy_for_grant()` (the `EquippableData` virtual every equippable overrides as needed — `WeaponInstance`'s keeps `template` shared and copies spaces shallowly, the default falls back to `duplicate(true)` for `RuneData`). In battle, `Unit.get_fired_attack()` (live pick else first-channelable-carving/main) answers **"what would this unit fire if it declared right now"** — read at AIM time only (the targeting overlay, the hover preview, and `AttackAction.declare()`'s stamp). It is deliberately NOT what reach and ally-splash read: since #102 those take the `AttackData` as a parameter, so a stored order's geometry comes off its own stamp and a counter's off `get_counter_attack()`. `active_attack` is therefore a pure **aiming cursor**, cleared when targeting ends (`game._clear_aiming_pick`, from `exit_current_mode` while the state is still ATTACK_TARGETING) — a reset that was only safe to add *after* the parameterization, because until then the stale pick was the one thing making secondary AoE resolve correctly. `Unit.get_counter_attack()` is a deliberate exception — weapons always counter with main, ignoring any live pick (a rune counters with whatever it fires; overwatch-style alt-attack countering is future scope, #73) — and since #84 that main must also be FIREABLE (`Unit.attack_source_can_counter()` checks readiness), so an empty Carbine magazine or a sprung Springspear stops countering, and since #1135 a HEAL or a MAP-only attack never counters at all (`AttackData.can_ever_counter`, which `attack_source_can_counter` reads -- the plan cannot see that gate, since an empty counter volley simply vanishes, so a test asks `can_counter`), and since #810 an ARMED OVERWATCH stops countering (`Unit.is_standing_watch()`, read by `can_counter` -- the Overwatch was that unit's action; squad-system.md C11), and a BLOW BREAKS THE WATCH outright (any landed attack but a heal; `Watch.is_armed()` is the one predicate for "could this still fire" -- and since the #810 follow-up it ASKS the weapon, so a dry watch neither fires nor draws, at three lint doors onto effective_main, `ResolvedOutcome.cancels_watch` carries it to both executors, and it MARKS rather than lapsing so the reaction stays spent), and a counter that does land spends whatever its main consumes — and the chosen `AttackData` is stamped onto `AttackAction.fired_attack` at declare time so the resolver reads a frozen snapshot, never re-derives (Law #2). `AttackShape` resources stay pure **stamp geometry**, unaffected by any of this split -- and since #808 they are SHARED between attacks, so an editor copy that deep-duplicated one would silently fork it (`duplicate(true)` shares a path'd sub-resource, which is what makes that safe).

**The vertical aim gate is BUILT ([#258](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/258), 2026-08-20 incl. same-day review rework): `Reach.vertical_aim_ok` inside `can_hit_cell_from`, which takes the `BoardContext` as a required parameter (null = flat). Three authored pieces on `AttackData`: `vertical_rule` (`MELEE` — "same step, or a facing half step", judged by `RulesService.height_step_ok`, the height core extracted from `can_step`; a null attack/bare fists is MELEE; `RANGED` otherwise — renamed from STEP/TOLERANCE by [#473](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/473), members in place so the serialized ints are unchanged), `up_tolerance`/`down_tolerance` (-1 = unlimited — a lob's climb ceiling is its up-tolerance, a gun stays -1), and `arc_clearance` (0 = flat shot, no unlimited sentinel; renamed from the never-built `arcs_over_obstacles` bool by #218) — evaluated by `Reach.sight_trace`, ONE trajectory (endpoints at `EYE_HEIGHT` — 1.0 RULE unit since #427, i.e. 0.5 world, which #1059 measured as 80% of a body height rather than the "sprite centre" this line used to claim — parabolic arc, touch = blocked, `GridUtils.cells_crossed` supercover) that the gate checks and the in-game sight line draws, so the rule and what the player sees cannot disagree. Directional spreads were EXEMPT until [#756](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/756) (2026-09-04, dev: "truncate, and all 8"), which the dev found in play as an Overwatch bug -- a spread is now TRUNCATED at the first cell its shot cannot reach and every cell behind that one is cut, so `Reach.get_affected_cells_from` takes the board as a REQUIRED parameter and a spread advances as a FRONT (each lane traced from the shooter cell carried sideways onto that lane, never fanned diagonally out of it). A POINT aim's splash stopped being board-blind at [#805](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/805): it SPREADS from the impact cell, asking this same gate from there with the impact standing in for the shooter, so what #218 still defers is a blast covering a VOLUME rather than a heightmap's surface. Both propagations became OPTIONAL at [#1055](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1055): a true AoE covers its whole stamp through walls and still asks this gate per cell. The rulings and payoffs (counter denial via `can_counter`'s threaded board, `ResolvedOutcome.elevation_delta`) are in verticality.md → *Targeting* and *Line of sight*.** **Per-attack readiness BUILT 2026-07-22 (#73), completed by a third flag 2026-07-28 (#108):** `WeaponAttackData.requires_readiness`/`consumes_readiness`/**`builds_readiness`** (all default `false`) author a wind-up/recovery economy — gated / spends / banks; the actual readiness STATE lives on `WeaponInstance` itself, not `Unit` (a unit can carry more than one of the same weapon — they must track independently) and not the shared template (battle-scoped). `WeaponInstance.make()` dispatches on `weapon_type` to build the right concrete subclass — every family has one (`SpringspearWeaponInstance` plus six pure pass-throughs added by [#82](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/82), 2026-07-22), and the base class declares the readiness surface as no-op virtuals (`is_attack_fireable`/`consume_readiness_for`), so families with no signature mechanic yet pay nothing — **and as of 2026-07-27 the self-ability verbs (`can_reload`/`reload`/`reload_label`, `can_rev`/`rev`/`tick_rev`, `can_burrow`) sit one level higher still, on `EquippableData`**, so `Unit` delegates to whatever is equipped without casting. They were ARGUMENT-FREE until [#97](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/97) (2026-09-06): the reload trio now takes a `Unit`, since the Chemical Spitter's rearm reads that unit's inventory, and it promotes just as cleanly -- a `Unit` parameter needs no per-family widening the way a `WeaponAttackData` one would. Only the two `WeaponAttackData`-taking methods stayed on `WeaponInstance`: widening their parameter to `AttackData` for the base would break every family override's signature.

**The bool generalizes to a counter without touching the seam** — `CarbineWeaponInstance.shots_remaining` (#84) reads the authored flags as a magazine, and `KineticMaceWeaponInstance.charge` reads them as a bank. The mace only became true of that in #108: it had inferred its charge-spender from `knockback > 0`, a private predicate for a fact these flags already carried, which made authoring a readiness flag on that family silently inert and would have rewired the economy the moment any second mace attack gained knockback. `builds_readiness` is the state whose absence caused the shortcut — the seam could say "needs one and spends one" but not "banks one". Doctrine, now authored on Smash rather than emergent: **a counter banks charge** (it stamps main, main builds; the swing is what stores the energy). `Unit` only holds thin delegators reading whatever's equipped. An unmapped `weapon_type` is a loud failure (`push_error` + `null` return from `make()`), never a silent fallback.

**That battle state now SAVES without becoming persistent (#87, 2026-07-30):** `capture_battle_state()`/`apply_battle_state()` are a third family-agnostic virtual pair beside the readiness and status ones (base returns `{}`), and the fields themselves stay non-`@export` — so `make()`/`copy_for_grant()` still re-arm every weapon, and only an explicit scenario snapshot puts a magazine or a rev timer back. See the persistence-seam bullet above. **A SECOND economy joined it in [#97](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/97) (2026-09-06) — the SUPERCHARGE, and it is deliberately NOT the readiness seam.** The Chemical Spitter's tank banks shots an injected vial pays for, and `requires_readiness`/`consumes_readiness` express *gating*, which the one law of materia forbids: a dry spitter fires its ordinary Spray forever. So the tank is `WeaponInstance.is_supercharged()`/`tank_charges()`/`spend_charge()` beside readiness rather than through it, `is_attack_fireable` stays inert for the family, and what the charge BUYS is authored on the attack — **`WeaponAttackData.empowered_form`, what an attack BECOMES while its weapon is hot** (the weapon-side twin of the empowered form a carving authors at a source, [#695](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/695)). A whole attack rather than a delta, because the dev's ruling was that the supercharge changes RANGE and range is geometry.

**`WeaponInstance.effective_main` is the ONE substitution point** — applied after the mod replacement, so the menu, `Reach`, the resolver, the queue row and the AI all see the charged attack with no per-site knowledge of a tank — and it made that function's answer TIME-DEPENDENT for the first time, which is why `_mods_for` now asks `is_main_form` (both forms are the main) rather than comparing against whichever is live: a stamp of the other form would otherwise drop every MAIN_ATTACK mod silently. The SPEND is threaded on `PlanResolver._Hypo.charges` and stamped as `ResolvedOutcome.charge_spent` — the `burned_vial` shape — because counters and watch shots resolve before anything executes and a live read would let two of them claim one charge; **both execution twins read that stamp**, through the one spend step both call since #46 (`AttackAction.settle`). Only one level is ever read, and `AttackLint` refuses a chain. See [`docs/build-log.md`](../build-log.md) → *Weapons* for #82's full build note.

## The weapons/ folder

`weapons/` — the whole weapon domain (consolidated here 2026-07-23; this content previously sat in `items/`): `WeaponData` (family templates), `WeaponInstance`, `WeaponModData`, `WeaponAttackData`, the catalogs (`WeaponCatalog`, `WeaponAttackCatalog`, `WeaponModCatalog`); `weapons/instances/` = the 7 per-family `WeaponInstance` subclasses; `weapons/patterns/` = `AttackShape`, the shared named stamps (`Resources/AttackShapes/`, listed by `AttackShapeCatalog`); `Reach` = the static attack-geometry queries (ex-`CombatComponent`). The attack's OTHER shared library, `EffectLook` (#900), lives in `presentation/` with its catalog rather than here, on `LookPreset`'s rule: it is dumb presentation data a shipping resource points at, and a shipping resource pointing into `Classes/dev/` is the wrong direction.

## The items/ folder

`items/` — the base item vocabulary only (weapon content → `weapons/`, rune/transmutation content → `alchemy/`, both 2026-07-23): `Item` (base: `display_name`/`icon`/`description` — **renamed from `item_name` 2026-08-04**, so all four content roots off `Resource` — `Item`, `AttackData`, `JobData`, `WeaponModData` — agreed on one field name (**`WeaponModData` stopped being one of the four in [#732](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/732), 2026-09-06: it extends `Item` now and INHERITS `display_name` and `weight` rather than declaring them, which is why no authored `.tres` changed meaning. The base is not a claim that a mod is carryable — `WeaponData` extends it too and is a shared template; what it buys is riding the `Item`-typed drag widget. A mod answers `copy_for_grant()` with ITSELF, since it is a shared authored ref and `duplicate(true)` would fork its granted attacks into siblings a save writes inline**); that disagreement was the reason every catalog scan had to hardcode which field it read, and the migration was 34 `.tres` files + 44 code sites; **`copy_for_grant()` lives HERE since [#697](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/697)** -- it was `EquippableData.copy_equippable()` until the three authoring doors widened to `Item`, and a door typed `Item` cannot call a method named for equippables; **`shown_name()` joined it there in [#835](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/835) (2026-09-08)** -- `describe()`'s sibling for the NAME and there for its reason, the field being storage: a kind that reads its name from somewhere else says so once. `WeaponInstance` reads its template's THROUGH, which is what lets a DERIVED generic carry no name of its own and still list as "Chemical Spitter" rather than as its filename) → `EquippableData` (the equip-slot type + `copy_for_grant()` virtual + the **attack-source surface**: `selectable_attacks`/`default_attack`/`counter_attack`/`secondary_attacks`, split since [#590](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/590) into two DISJOINT views off one `repertoire()` — `fireable_only` filters every surface that offers something to FIRE, `watch_attacks` is what `Unit.overwatch_attacks` reads, and an attack authored `can_overwatch` is watch-ONLY so it can never be in both, plus the **weapon-verb surface** added 2026-07-27 on the same principle: `can_reload`/`reload`/`reload_label`, `can_rev`/`rev`/`tick_rev`, `can_burrow` — each defaulting to the inert answer so armour needs no override. Added 2026-07-26, **reversing** the older "EquippableData deliberately has no combat surface; combat sites cast as WeaponInstance and a rune yields null" rule — that held at one cast site, not at the four fork methods Unit had grown. Same outcome, declared once. The four `Unit` doors (`set_equipped_weapon`, `wear_armor`, `add_item`'s auto-equip and the preview) ask the slot, and so do `GearVerbs`' Equip and Wear, `ScenarioUnitEntry`'s restore, the inventory panel's rows, the Play API's kit view, and both editors' Equip/Wear checkboxes. ## The elemental/ folder and Conduction

`elemental/` — `Elemental`, `ElementalReaction` (a reaction answers to its primary `incoming_element` and to every element in `also_triggered_by`, `TerrainReaction` likewise, asked only through `fires_for` / `Elemental.triggered` and firing once per hit; HEAT, free heat, rides it since 2026-10-12 and no ignition lists it), `ReactionCatalog`, **`Conduction`** ([#884](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/884), 2026-09-10 — where a SHOCK hit's current TRAVELS. **The conductor set IS the victim set**: a water tile unless FROZEN, or a WET unit, and wet units RELAY, so a soaked line of bodies carries the current off the lake. Deliberately NOT folded into `Reach`, which answers pure geometry and has a null-board mode; this reads unit state. `Conduction.sweep` is THE one answer to "what does this aim reach", called by the three sites that build volleys — aims, counters, watch shots — and the four that must not contradict them (`HoverPresenter`, `AITactics`, and `play_session`'s two); `SquadPlanValidator` asks with its own projection, since #126's two falses are load-bearing there. Two things it deliberately does differently from `RulesService.is_attack_victim`: **it does not check tags** (allies and a shooter standing in the water are caught — dev, the reasoning #810 used for watch-breaking — nor `targets`, so a MAP-only shock that hits nobody itself still electrifies whoever is in its water, #1135), and it reads wetness **through the hypo**, so an empty one is the live board and the pass's own is what that pass has done. Reach is `SHOCK_ARC_RANGE`, a game CONSTANT rather than an authored field: arcing through water is a property of electricity, so no attack may disagree about it.

**The flood keeps its PARENT LINKS since [#887](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/887)** — `Conduction.flood` is the whole answer and `arc_cells` a projection of it, `Link` being one hop with its BFS depth, so the current is a TREE rather than a set of live tiles and a renderer lighting the hops in step order shows the spread the rule made. Stamped on the volley as `AttackAction.arc_links` at the same three sites the footprint is, and playback may never re-derive it: the conductor set depends on the PASS's own wetness, which lives in the resolver's hypo and is gone by execution).
