## Reach

`Reach` (`weapons/`, **static**) — weapon-aware attack geometry: `get_attack_cells_from` (facing-specific), `get_all_attack_cells_from` (union over 4 facings, for overlays), `get_affected_cells_from` (AoE -- and since #756 it takes the BOARD, because a directional spread is truncated by the terrain -- and whether it is truncated AT ALL is authored since #1055), `can_aim_at` (may this aim be declared at all -- the one gate the click, the hover and the headless twin share), `is_directional_attack()` (does this attack's pattern aim by facing?). **Every one of them TAKES the `AttackData` it is reasoning about (#102, 2026-07-28)** — they used to look it up themselves via `Unit.get_fired_attack()`, i.e. the live `active_attack` pick, while the queued order carried its own frozen `AttackAction.fired_attack` stamp; geometry answered from one source and damage from the other, and a leftover pick re-shaped stored blasts and granted counter reach the counter couldn't fire with. Each caller now names its source: **aiming/preview** → `unit.get_fired_attack()`, **a stored order** → `action.fired_attack`, **a counter** → `unit.get_counter_attack()`. Law #4 in practice — passing beats looking up. Null attack (or one with no pattern) = adjacency fallback (Manhattan 1 selectable, the aimed cell alone affected) — load-bearing in the tests, where a pattern-less weapon is how they get trivial geometry.

**Since [#894](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/894) (2026-10-30) the placement takes the SHAPE as a parameter** (`_set_down(origin, target, attack, shape, board, facing, walks)`, `_place(shape, …)`), so an attack's tile-effect layer is set down by the same code as its hit, never a copy of it. `get_affected_cells_from` hands it `attack.attack_shape`, and **`get_effect_cells_from`** hands it `attack.tile_effect_shape`: the same anchor, the same turn to the facing, the same Swing truncation or true-AoE height clause, the same surface trim. It never walks paths (`walks` false), because a path's stop belongs to its victim and an effect has none, so a PATH shape used as an effect layer is placed as its tiles. The aim sites never call it directly: `Conduction._sweep` stamps it as `Sweep.effect` (the struck tiles when the attack names no effect shape), `AttackAction.stamp_sweep` copies it onto every volley member as `effect_cells`, and a watch stores it when it arms (`Watch.effect_cells`), since a watch shot walks paths and never reaches `_sweep`. The hit footprints are byte-identical to before; the existing Reach and targeting suites are what showed it.

**Was `CombatComponent`, a Node on every Unit, until 2026-07-26.** It held no state: every method opened by fetching its owner back, `apply_damage` was a pass-through to `Unit.take_damage`, and `can_attack(attacker, target)` took the attacker as a parameter despite being an instance method on it (now `RulesService.can_target`). Its lone `@export can_counter` was **never authored on any unit**, so SquadManager's gate on it was permanently open and shadowed the real, authored `AttackData.can_counter` — the gate is gone and `Unit.attack_source_can_counter()` is the single check.

## Targeting modes

**Targeting modes (#25):** attacks are **directional** (`AttackData.is_directional()` true — a SELF-ANCHORED stamp, `max_range` 0, the old forward line/wide: the player points a cardinal direction and the whole stamp fires; the pointed cell need not be a member) or **point** (false — `max_range` 1 or more / no weapon: the hovered cell itself must be in range, and the stamp lands on it). ATTACK_TARGETING asks `Reach.can_aim_at` in both hover-preview (`HoverPresenter._hover_attack_targeting`) and click (`game._click_attack_targeting`) -- ONE predicate since #756, where the two (plus the headless `play_session.overwatch`) each carried an inline copy of the same disjunction, and the Play API's `queue_attack` and `legal_targets` ask it too since #1365, and where a directional aim stopped being unconditional: a facing whose spread the terrain truncates to nothing is a dud order and is refused -- and since [#1228](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1228) a footprint lists only cells with a SURFACE (`GridUtils.has_surface`: a tile, and not a hole; trimmed after propagation, so a shot still crosses a chasm), so a facing off the board's edge or a point aim into a hole is a dud too — both hoist `attacker.get_fired_attack()` into a local first, since aiming is the one context where the live pick genuinely IS the question; the red overlay still shows the full 4-facing reach (`get_all_attack_cells_from`).

**The reach layer is INVIOLABLE and the aim pulses on a separate channel (2026-08-01).** The full range is drawn once on entering the mode, never filtered or re-tiled — that invariant is about CELL MEMBERSHIP and is unaffected by the layer's fill color, which is red for a damaging attack and green for a healing one (`OverlayManager.attack_reach_color`, keyed off `AttackData.heals`, #123 follow-up, 2026-08-06) — nor by #258's blocked state: a reach cell the vertical gate refuses (rule or sight line) wears the hatched `BLOCKED_ATLAS_COORDS` tile instead of the plain fill, a per-cell state on the same layer (the `TARGET_ATLAS_COORDS` precedent), drawn once by `show_attack_reach(union, blocked)` and routed to its own `Layer.ATTACK_BLOCKED` in 3D by `OverlayMirror` (colour derived: reach modulate × `BLOCKED_REACH_DIM`). The hovered RANGED aim additionally shows its **sight line** — the laser trace `Reach.sight_trace` computed for the gate ("bead" as in *a bead on someone*, dev), stored once on `OverlayManager` (`show_sight_trace`/`clear_sight_trace` + `sight_trace_version` for the mirror's #308-style gate), drawn as a flat polyline by the code-built `SightTrace2D` and at true heights by `OverlayMirror` via `BoardOverlays.set_line` (`Layer.SIGHT_TRACE`, the sink's LINE kind); **the diorama draws those points as a camera-facing RIBBON since [#506](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/506)** -- an `ImmediateMesh` line strip is drawn by Godot at ONE SCREEN PIXEL whatever the distance, so the bead stayed a hairline at every #410 zoom level, and `add_beam_strip` (`set_line`'s recipe, hoisted at #887) emits each point twice with a side flag plus the joint-averaged tangent for `Classes/presentation/sight_beam.gdshader` to push apart in the VERTEX stage (a CPU ribbon would rebuild every frame the rig orbits, and `BoardOverlays` has no `_process`). `cull_disabled`, the joint average and `extra_cull_margin` are each load-bearing rather than caution, and **the cross-ribbon `abs()` belongs in the FRAGMENT stage** -- every vertex sits on a rim, so taking it per-vertex interpolates to a constant and ALPHA comes out ZERO, which shipped once and rendered nothing at all. A screen-pixel WIDTH FLOOR shipped beside it and was removed: it read `VIEWPORT_SIZE` in the vertex stage, dominated the width at every knob setting, and drew the beam as huge wedges FLARING with distance -- which is what identified it, since perspective narrows a constant-width ribbon instead. Width is one world-space multiply now, and a law refuses the builtin by name. See verticality.md -> *Line of sight*. The centreline STORE is untouched, which is why nothing upstream changed; the flat view keeps its thin line as a declared #292 asymmetry, the verdict COLOURS stay one answer on `SightTrace2D` (`static var` now, so `GameKnobs.CLASS_KNOBS` can write them) and width/softness/glow are 3D-only `BoardOverlays` exports. Those colours are deliberately NOT in #422's aim palette: widening that vocabulary is [#675](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/675)'s job, the same scope call the watch pair got. The rest of the look -- marching dashes, a terminal reticule, an impact mark, a lob's ground shadow -- is the declared deferral [#674](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/674), which [#1042](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1042) gave a CONSTRAINT: the repeating DASH pattern is the AIM's, because the enemy intent mark took the single travelling BEAD -- two motions with different signatures, so a beam that moves is still a distinction (dev ruling, 2026-09-19). `Reach.draws_sight_trace` is the one visibility policy — melee/STEP and bare fists draw none (visually obvious anytime — dev), directional spreads have no single line; `exit_current_mode` clears it.

**`ATTACK_MODULATE`/`HEAL_ATTACK_MODULATE` are `static var`, not `const`, since #212 (2026-08-15)** — the Moods tab tunes them, and `attack_reach_color` is itself static so no other form works. **`HOVER_MODULATE` joined them in [#422](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/422) (2026-09-01)** — the aim FOOTPRINT is the third channel a player's palette repaints, and a value a palette can move has to be one the dev can author, so it also gained its first `CLASS_KNOBS` row (*Aim footprint (2D+3D)*); the travel-order flash only whitens it, so it has no second colour to chase. **The three of them are the only knobs a player setting can override, and the fork lives inside the three accessors and nowhere else**: `OverlayManager.AIM_PALETTES` holds the ALTERNATIVES, and **DEFAULT is not a row in it** — it falls through to these statics, so a knob the dev drags still reaches the board and no copy can go stale (#308's shape). Consequences worth knowing before touching any of it: a colour knob moves the DEFAULT palette alone, which the Game tab says out loud in a polled notice rather than leaving three sliders silently inert; `_ready` paints both aim layers **through the accessors**, never the statics (reverting that left three suites 33 cases green — #103's shape, now pinned by `tests/ui/test_aim_palette_reaches_the_board.gd`); and `SettingsScreen` re-applies through `refresh_aim_colors` on close, because these layers are otherwise painted only on entering and leaving an aim while the footprint layer is shared with `PICKING_TARGET`. The WATCH pair is deliberately outside the palette. Canon: `docs/design/presentation-effects.md` → *The aim palette*. Tuning them moves BOTH stacks by construction: the 3D mirrors this layer's *modulate* rather than holding a colour of its own, so there is no 3D-only value to tune (dev call).

**Since [#591](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/591) that fill also forks on the VERB** -- `attack_reach_color(attack, watch)`, and the watch tint beats the heal fork outright, because while declaring you already know what you picked and cannot otherwise tell you are aiming a watch rather than firing. `set_aim_colors(attack, watch)` (ex-`set_attack_reach_color`) is the one door and paints BOTH layers of the aim -- reach and footprint -- remembering both so `refresh_aim_colors()` (ex-`refresh_attack_reach_color`) can re-derive them; without that a tuned colour would not appear until the player next entered targeting. `_aiming_watch` is THE store for the verb on this side, read by the two fills. (It used to be read by the footprint's breathing pulse too, whose running `Pulse` held the endpoints it was STARTED with and breathed a watch aim back to the shot's yellow twice a second; #1057 part 2 deleted that tween.) And the HOVER layer is shared with `PICKING_TARGET`, so `exit_current_mode` resets the fork or a watch aim tints the next rescue's tile pick. What the CURRENT aim would affect is **pulsed** (`Classes/core/Pulse.gd` — it takes the PROPERTY to tween since #313, because a 3D readout's colour is a material rather than a `modulate`, so every CanvasItem caller passes `&"modulate"`; `OverlayManager.set_target_pulse` diffs the set rather than restarting it — a restart per mouse-move strobes) -- whoever the sweep hits, the hover asking no `hits_units()` question of its own since #1135 (a map-only attack's own victims are none by the resolver's rule, and the units its current catches are hit for real) -- and **every footprint FLASHES in the order the attack travels** since [#1057](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1057) part 2 (`AimFlash2D`, the 2D clock the 3D copies per tile; see presentation-effects.md → *The aim FLASHES*) — which retired the rule that only an attack hitting the MAP moved its tiles.

**What the aim DROPS draws too** since [#1058](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1058) D2b: its payloads' tiles as INSET squares (`OverlayManager.payload_overlay`, `Layer.PAYLOAD` in 3D), each level flashing one step after the last, off a resolve (`SquadManager.preview_aim`) because a sticky bomb's tile exists nowhere else. **And what the aim would DO shows before the click since [#929](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/929)**: that same resolve is published as `game.aim_forecast`, which `game.previewed_plan` (battle3d's `_previewed_plan` until #1430) hands the health bars while an aim is open, and **F / Shift+F while aiming cycles every attack across every carried weapon** (`Unit.aim_options`). Another weapon's attack is previewed with it held only for the computation (the forecast holds it on the pass's copy of the actor, `PlanResolver.hold_in_hand`, since [#1480](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1480) stage 4; the hover's sweep still swaps the live hand for one synchronous call through `Unit.with_weapon_in_hand`; either way a save or report mid-aim records no swap), and the click equips it for real, recorded on `game.loadout_acted` — the one gear-act channel, which the dock forwards into too. See weapons.md → *Comparing attacks while aiming*.

**Never draw a marker onto the reach layer** — a `TileMapLayer` holds ONE tile per cell, so it replaces the fill; that mistake erased a whole ForwardWide lane per occupied lane. A live pulse OWNS `sprite.modulate` (`UnitVisuals.set_highlighted` yields to it, `reset_visuals` kills it first) and `exit_current_mode` must clear it. Tile overlays sit BELOW `Unit.BASE_SPRITE_INDEX`. The cursor no longer encodes validity — it went green on reach alone. Story + the two failed designs: [`docs/build-log.md`](../build-log.md). Pinned by `tests/ui/test_attack_targeting_visuals.gd`. **Geometry is a RANGE plus a SHARED SHAPE since [#808](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/808) (2026-09-06), and the split is the umbrella's opening ask -- *"two things about an attack: its range, and its pattern"*. The Manhattan RANGE (`min_range` / `max_range` / `max_and_a_half`, the no-float blended Manhattan/Chebyshev bevel → `GridUtils.cells_within_blended_range`) lives on **`AttackData`**; the STAMP of `Vector2i` offsets, grid-UP forward, lives in its own named **`AttackShape`** file under `Resources/AttackShapes/` (`AttackShapeCatalog`), turned to the aim's cardinal ONLY when the attack is self-anchored (see #818 below), and emitted near to far then left to right (a rule: victim order is volley order, and `Reach._truncate` needs a predecessor emitted first).** **`Reach` owns the three geometry queries**, because answering one needs both halves.

**A shape is SHARED BY REFERENCE** (dev, 2026-09-06 -- `main_attack`'s precedent, an attack being authored content rather than a save file): editing one changes every attack naming it, so the folder is an exact census and diverging means Save As. **A NULL shape covers the anchor cell alone** -- most authored attacks, deliberately not a file, since a "Single" entry says nothing about the variety the library exists to show -- while an EMPTY stamp covers nothing and is a lint BLOCKS. **The editor's grid edits a COPY of a named shape**: editing the shared object would break the commit-point ruling AND make Save As impossible, `take_over_path` moving the object every other attack still holds; Update writes both files under one confirm (FAMILY mode's precedent), and Delete is refused while anything references it -- read off a repo TEXT SCAN rather than a catalog, since an attack embedded in a mission or a rune is in no catalog.

**That whole flow is `Classes/dev/LibraryField.gd` since [#900](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/900)**, extracted when `EffectLook` became the second library and wanted every line of it: six non-obvious decisions in ONE place (the (none) row must be index zero, a held resource the library cannot name needs a row of its own, a named resource is edited as a COPY and an unnamed one in place, Save As forks THIS holder alone, a delete is refused while anything references it, and the commit point is Update) instead of two copies that agree until one is taught something. It binds to a FIELD through callables rather than a property name, because a shape is a property and a look is a slot in a dictionary. `users_of` went with it to `ResourceCatalog` -- the question is about a path and a `.tres`, not about shapes.

**The anchor is DERIVED from the range, never a flag (dev, 2026-09-06), AND SO IS WHETHER THE SHAPE TURNS ([#818](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/818), dev 2026-09-07 -- *"the direction input model doesn't make sense for attacks placed at a range"*): `max_range == 0` sits the stamp on the ATTACKER, the aim IS a facing, and the shape rotates to it; anything else sits it on the AIMED CELL and it NEVER turns -- it lands as drawn, grid-up reading as board north, so the editor's grid is WYSIWYG for a placed shape.** That repeals half of #802's ruling 2 and RETIRES a special case rather than adding one (the old min_range-0 self-aim clause is simply what anchored means now). Consequence worth knowing: an anchored footprint emits in BOARD order (south row first, then northward, west to east) rather than nearest-the-attacker-first -- still one deterministic rule, but no longer origin-relative, and ordering a placed blast outward from where it LANDS is #805's question.

**The caret over a stamp grid is `AttackData.grid_up_label`, read by BOTH surfaces that draw one** (the Attack Editor and `ShapePlate`) and riding the caption's live `changed` sync -- spelled twice, or read at build time, it says FORWARD over a shape that no longer turns. Cleave, Line and every point attack are authored stamps now, and the three classes (`ManhattanRangePattern` / `ForwardLinePattern` / `ForwardWidePattern`, with #20's keep-both ruling) are gone; `AttackLint` gained the twin rule *lands on at least one cell* for an empty stamp under a live ring. **The stamp is authored on a CLICKABLE GRID since [#804](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/804) (2026-09-06)** — an odd-sized board of toggle cells around a marked centre, grid-UP forward, with a stepper that grows it and greys rather than dropping an authored cell, and the coordinate row surviving underneath as a second RENDER of the one store (so a shape can be copied between attacks).

**Which fields get one is DECLARED by the resource** (`AttackShape.grid_fields`, `property_tips`' shape), never inferred from `Array[Vector2i]` — `ScenarioUnitEntry.watch_cells` is that type and holds ABSOLUTE cells, which a centred grid would lie about — and the caption naming the anchor is the ATTACK's answer (`AttackData.grid_caption`, reading `is_directional()` so it cannot drift from the rule; the shape holds no range and structurally cannot answer, which is why `add_cell_grid` takes a caption SOURCE). It follows `max_range` live off the `changed` that the reflective rows' writer, the public `DevWidgets.write()` / `write_many()`, emits, and it was the first listener `_adopt`'s long-declared hook ever got; a rebuild driven from the spinbox's own signal would free the emitting node (#741).

**Splash at range stopped being board-blind at [#805](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/805) (2026-09-19): a placed blast SPREADS from where it lands** — `Reach._spread` floods outward from the impact cell, a cell covered only when the blast could propagate to it through the cells between, and each cell asks **`vertical_aim_ok` with the IMPACT standing in for the shooter**. **It added NO authored field, and that is the design**: a blast's vertical reach and what blocks it are the attack's own `vertical_rule` / `up_tolerance` / `down_tolerance` / `arc_clearance`, read from where it landed — one rule at two anchors, and which anchor applies is the question `max_range` already answers. A blast-only tolerance looked necessary only while the delivery and the blast were one attack (a lob authors its tolerances unlimited so it can be lobbed anywhere, which would make its burst unlimited too); **once a blast is its own attack fired from the impact, it authors its own reach and that conflict dissolves** — which is what made this the right home for a PAYLOAD attack, **BUILT by [#1058](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1058) (2026-09-23)**: `AttackData.payload` names another authored attack that a hit DROPS, resolved in full from where it went off with the thrower still the actor (`PlanResolver.drop_payloads` is the one derivation, called at the aim, counter and watch-shot sites, each payload joining its parent's own list right behind it; `Conduction.sweep_payload` fires it with a facing, through `Reach.placement_dir`). A chain runs to any depth and NEVER loops, because Godot cannot store a reference cycle (ruling 46). See weapons.md → *Payloads*.

**A swing travels in parallel lanes and a blast propagates, so the CONNECTIVITY halves stay two rules** — two physical claims, not one question asked from two cells. **`_truncate` could not simply be re-pointed, for two compounding reasons worth knowing before anyone tries**: its predecessor is `cell - dir` along ONE facing and an anchored aim's `dir` is the attacker-to-target cardinal, so a placed cross's centre would be nobody's predecessor and the blast would change shape with where the attacker stood — the spin #818 removed from placement; and a predecessor pass needs cells decided toward the origin first, which `AttackShape.place`'s sort gives only near-to-far along a facing, while an anchored shape emits in BOARD order (southern row first) and hands out the south arm before the centre it must propagate through. Walking outward by distance from the impact needs no sort and is order-independent. Survivors are filtered in emission order, so victim order stays volley order.

**WHETHER EITHER PROPAGATION RUNS AT ALL IS AUTHORED SINCE [#1055](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1055) (2026-09-20) -- `AttackData.swing`, the MODIFIER half of the dev's own vocabulary** (*"2 kinds of attacks - Single target, AoE, and then we have a modifier - swings"*). Both were unconditional until then, which left a **TRUE AoE unauthorable** -- the whole stamp at once, through walls. Swing OFF takes a THIRD path that drops connectivity and the trace and **keeps the per-cell vertical rule** (dev ruling, over a fully terrain-blind AoE: a melee cleave must not reach a unit three levels up), so a true AoE ignores what STANDS BETWEEN and never how high a cell sits. The flag reads nothing without a shape -- one cell has nothing to stop at -- so the editor hides its row there; it defaults FALSE, and all sixteen shaped files were authored `true` in the same diff.

**A shape is painted tiles OR ordered PATHS since [#1079](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1079)** (paths arrived in [#1056](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1056)) — a path is how a single-target swing is authored, drawn as arrows in the grid's Paths mode; a path shape's tiles are the ones its paths visit (`AttackShape.tiles()`, the one answer to which tiles a shape covers), its `path_cells` + `path_lengths` pair is judged by `AttackShape.path_fault`, and since [#1057](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1057) Swing on walks it as a single-target swing (`Reach.get_paths_from` + `RulesService.gather_path_victims`, both reached through `Conduction.sweep`, which takes the AIM so no site can hand in tiles alone), and every overwatch shot is single-target; see weapons.md and standing-reactions.md. The attacker's chosen facing is now explicit — the intended seam for a future **backstrike** (facing-vs-facing damage).
