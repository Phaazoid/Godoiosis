## Scenes/LookDev/ is load-bearing

**`Scenes/LookDev/` is LOAD-BEARING, not scratch ([#393](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/393), 2026-08-19).** The name and its own header said scratch; eight presentation suites fixture on `LookDev.tscn` (`test_board_overlays`, `test_board_picker`, `test_camera_flourish`, `test_camera_rig`, `test_camera_scale`, `test_lethal_direction`, `test_look_dev`, `test_walk_demo`; `test_sight_beam` deliberately builds a bare `BoardOverlays` instead, to skip the scene's 5 MB mesh library), `tests/law/test_heavy_scenes_are_preloaded.gd` names its path, `Battle3D.tscn` loads its `lookdev_meshlib.tres`, and `BoardMirror`/`BoardOverlays` read textures out of `Art/LookDev/` — so it is edited and deleted with the care shipping code gets. Two things left it in #393. Its **moods** were a hardcoded `const PRESETS` seeded from the same numbers four of the twelve `LookPreset` files carry — a second answer to *what is Day?* — and are now key BINDINGS only, resolved by name through the shipping `LookKnobs.resolve` + `apply`; the cost is #253's own whole-table ruling arriving in the diorama, so a mood key restores an ingredient the F/G/C/V toggles turned off, and `.|opening_view_cells` is silently skipped because the free-roaming rig never frames here. Its **camera** was `look_dev_camera.gd`, i.e. the SHIPPING rig (Battle3D loads the same script) wearing the scratch scene's name, in the scratch scene's folder, with no `class_name` — now `Classes/presentation/CameraRig3D.gd`, beside the `CameraPose` that exists only to feed its `pose()`. The scene's remaining job is what it always was: the permanent lighting/grading playground, and the free camera + per-ingredient toggles that make one.

## ArcLightning

**`ArcLightning` (`presentation/`) is what a SHOCK looks like ([#887](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/887), 2026-09-10) — `StagingDust`'s shape one effect along**: a resident node owned by `battle3d`, its own clock, handed `volley_struck` events, every look value a `static var` with a Game-tab row on the Elemental page. **ONE MESH FOR THE WHOLE STORM, twice** — every bolt is a SURFACE on a shared `ImmediateMesh` rebuilt each frame, so a twenty-cell current is two draws and there is no pool to grow or exhaust; the pair is a narrow blooming CORE and a wide dim CORONA, two instances because the width is a shader uniform. The per-bolt AGE rides in the **vertex colour**, the only channel that varies inside one draw, which is why `sight_beam.gdshader` now multiplies by `COLOR` (a mesh with no colour array reads white, so the aim's sight bead is untouched — the shader has two tenants and the file name is the first one's). `BoardOverlays.add_beam_strip` + `beam_tangents` are the hoisted statics, and `FLAME_RENDER_PRIORITY` is `EFFECT_RENDER_PRIORITY` now, its own comment having asked for the rename on a third tenant.

**The STRIKE is the attack's own trajectory** — `Reach.sight_trace`, so an attack authored to clear any height descends near-vertically and a flat one draws a horizontal rod, with `BoardSpace.trace_point` the one conversion out of a trace's space; computed at PLAYBACK rather than stamped, which is deliberate because a trace is geometry rather than an OUTCOME. #217's second reader after fire, and only the flicker is gated. The sprite blowout is deliberately absent (`UnitMirror` rewrites every sprite's `modulate` per frame; a sprite effect is a tenant of #358's status material, never a second writer to `modulate`).

**The host hands it two answers, both as callables**, because only `battle3d` knows the mirrors: `point_of(cell)` is where a cell's surface is DRAWN (`BoardSpace.drawn_offset`: the tear-out's offset and the water basin's drop), which places the bolts; `feet_of(unit, cell)` is that point less `UnitMirror.wade_at`, where a body standing there is drawn, which places its sparks ([#1363](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1363)). Unset, `feet_of` falls back to `point_of`: a host with no unit mirror knows of no wading. The tear-out's landing dust takes the same `_surface_of`.

**SLICE 2 added three channels that each cross a system the ribbons do not**, all on the arc's own clock so a hitstop freezes the lot: **`ShockSparks`** (one emitter on #656's rules, a burst per caught BODY -- the half the bolts cannot say -- firing on the hop that reaches it, the delay READ BACK off the stamped tree so there is no second schedule); **`ParticleFan`** (#656's scatter with every number lifted into parameters, shared with the slam dust rather than copied, beside `BoardSpace.effect_volume` for the cull box -- where a stale copy draws NOTHING and no test can see it); **the WHITE-OUT's second driver** (each holds its own LEVEL, one function composes them with `max` -- a sum would pass #217's cap -- and the tear-out's idle branch clears only its own, which a single-writer channel could not survive: the shock inherits that cap by driving the existing channel rather than a rect of its own); and **the CRAWL** (the water shader draws the current as filaments, its own caustic net at a tighter scale, from a mask of one texel per cell holding that cell's hop count, decoded through the board mask's EXISTING rect and sampled NEAREST because a hop is a category). Its five shader globals are a NAMED CLOSED CATEGORY on the water law rather than an exception to it, and the `project.godot` edit round-trips through `ProjectSettings.save()` with zero churn (measured, via a throwaway tool scene).

**EVERY ONE OF THOSE VALUES IS NOW PER-ATTACK OVERRIDABLE ([#900](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/900), 2026-09-10)**: `strike` adopts the attack's `EffectLook` for SHOCK -- REPLACED per strike, never merged, or a Zap's overrides play on the next Shock Rod -- and every read is that look with the static as its fallback. The node owns the accessors `battle3d` used to read the statics for (`draws_crawl`/`crawl_life_now`/`crawl_step_now`), since the host has no attack in hand on a frame path; `GameKnobs`' spark sweep goes through `ArcLightning.reapply_sparks()` for the same reason, a re-apply from the bare statics being how a slider drag strips an authored look.

**The six MATERIAL rows are last-strike-wins while two shocks overlap** -- they are shader uniforms on one shared pair of meshes, which is the whole reason there are two instances -- declared rather than solved.

## Gas

**Gas is drawn by `GasMirror` (`presentation/`, [#508](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/508), 2026-10-01; mixes 2026-10-02)** — eight kinds at most, because the cells texture packs a cell's kinds in one byte and the shaders' per-kind uniform arrays are sized to that (widened from six for #1324's Essence; `tests/law/test_gas_look_coverage.gd` reds a short one, which no headless run could otherwise see). All eight are spent since #1427's Heat Haze, and the same law reds a ninth kind until the byte is widened — the store is `GasField` (terrain.md), the drawing the realistic volume mixed with pixel puffs in four switchable mixes behind Experiments' **Gas style**, plus a pixel floor shown only while Alt (`show_gas_floor`) is held (with next round's outline beside it since the round tick, built from `GasField.next_round` so it cannot disagree with the round: `GasSpread` is the one rule); since PR 3 medium-or-thick steam soaks a unit (WET) at the end of its own turn, and since #1331 scalds it on the same row, a row in the burn's END OF TURN list built by `TileHitAction.gas_hits` from the gas's `GasRules` file, and its volume is the project's first `CompositorEffect` (`GasVolumeEffect` + compute `gas_volume.glsl`; `get_cam_projection()` there is already reversed-Z, so never add `create_depth_correction`). Read `presentation-effects.md` → *Gas* before touching any of it. **Gas lies on the ground as drawn, never its rules height** ([#1363](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1363)): over water the floor, puffs and strike glows take `BoardSpace.drawn_offset`, the volume's ground textures and region boxes drop by `basin_drop` together, and `BoardSpace.basin_version` is in the rebuild key, since a depth move changes no store the mirror otherwise polls.

**The round runs the GAS before the TILES since #1335 (2026-10-22).** A spreading fire gives off its ground's gases on the cells it takes (`GasSpread.next` asks `TerrainStateManager.cells_fire_takes(board.tiles)`), and those cells can only be named before the tile tick lights them. So `game._on_round_completed` and the Play API's twin tick `gas_field` first, and the outline and the round read the same frontier. The tile tick changes nothing the gas round reads (FROZEN, the one state that moves walkability, has no clock). `tests/terrain/test_gas_in_play.gd`'s spreading-fire case is the ordering pin, falsified by swapping the two lines back. A smoke status (`GasRules.while_standing`, Smoked) is read off the gas field at the unit's cell each frame (`UnitMirror._states_shown`), never stored on the unit.

**The outline folds the previewed plan's deposits since #1332 (2026-10-22).** `GasField.next_round(board)` rules on the store's gas overlaid with `board.tiles` (the planned tiles, #1498) when the board carries any, and `GasMirror.board_source` is `game.next_round_board()` (battle3d's `_gas_outline_board()` until #1430 moved it, with `_previewed_plan()`, into game.gd as `previewed_plan()`, so the 2D red tether rules on the same board): `game._board()` over the planned tiles of the previewed plan (the queued plan, or the open aim's forecast; `board.planned(plan.tiles)`), so a queued push, a douse's steam or a hit's ignition smoke outlines from where the pass will leave it. **Mid-pass too, since #1498 stage 2 (2026-10-28):** the planned tiles are each touched cell's END record, not a delta, so reading them while playback has landed only some volleys still gives the end of the pass, the round to come. (Until stage 1 the board folded the deposits onto the live stores, which a landing partway through would have counted twice, so the outline read the live board while `executing_plan` was set.) Once the pass ends, `_end_squad_turn`'s cancels re-resolve the emptied queue synchronously, so no frame reads a played plan. The tick's board carries no planned tiles, so the round is unchanged. **A fire a queued hit lights spreads in the outline since #1430 (2026-10-23):** `GasSpread` asks `cells_fire_takes(board.tiles)`, which reads each cell through `PlannedTiles.states_at`, so the frontier of a tile the plan lights outlines its smoke and a fire a queued douse puts out outlines none (`test_gas_in_play`'s queued-fire case, falsified by passing no deposits).

## Weather

**Weather is a BOARD fact, and rain soaks ([#1260](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1260), 2026-10-08)** — `ScenarioData.weather` (`Weather.Kind`, append-only) names it, `ScenarioManager.current_weather` is the live store (`clear_board` drops it), and `BoardContext.weather` carries it into the rules (set after construction by the builders that have one, so every fixture reads CLEAR). `WeatherRules`/`WeatherLook` are one file per kind, GasRules/GasLook's shape. Its one rule rides steam's seam: **`TileHitAction.soaks` is the ONE composition of every end-of-turn soak**, gas then sky, and since #1331 it is the first half of `TileHitAction.end_of_turn` (soaks, then the burn), which both the forecast and the pass call. `WeatherMirror` (`presentation/`) draws it off that same store — the project's first particles shader for the rain, `WeatherMask` for where a drop lands, two `WetGround` decals, `StormBolts` off the board and the white-out's third driver — tuned on Project → Weather.

**A board may OFFER a set of weathers, one drawn when the mission starts ([#1509](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1509), 2026-10-27)** — the dev's ruling, general to board authoring. Two fields, each the one answer to its own question (Law #4):
- **`ScenarioData.weather` / `ScenarioManager.current_weather` stay THE answer to "what weather is it."** Neither changed type, and every reader still reads the store.
- **`ScenarioData.weather_odds: Dictionary[Weather.Kind, int]`** (kind → relative weight) is THE answer to "what may a mission start draw." Its live store is `ScenarioManager.current_weather_odds`, in `current_atmosphere`'s shape: copied on apply and capture, emptied by `clear_board`.
- **Empty odds is a locked board**, which is every board saved before #1509, so none needed migrating. **One spelling per meaning:** the Properties page writes one weather as a locked board (odds empty, `weather` set), never as odds of one.

**`WeatherDraw` (`flow/`, static, pure) is the rule, and the first roll on the rule side.** Law #1 is about combat: this draw happens before any order exists and is shown on the pre-mission screen's CONDITIONS box (the weather row's tooltip names the set) before the player commits. It walks the offered kinds in enum order, so one seed means one weather however the set was saved.
- **It draws ONLY at the fresh starts.** `MissionController._draw_weather` is called from `begin_mission` and `restart_mission`, after the load and before `_open_deployment` and `_begin_turn`, beside `deploy_roster` and for its reason. The Play API's twin is `PlaySession.start_weather`, called from the bridge's `load` (not on `resume`) and `restart`.
- **Never in `apply_scenario`**, which a save slot, a report, a replay, F2 and the dev header's Load all cross: each replays the RESULT, which a full capture keeps in `weather`. Nothing re-derives a roll.
- **A restart keeps the draw** (ruling 2: Retry, Restart and Reset Loadout alike). `restart_mission` reads the weather before its reload and passes it as `kept`; `WeatherDraw.at_start` keeps it unless the reloaded board no longer offers it.
- **The draw writes `current_weather` and nothing else**, never the `ScenarioData`, which is Godot's resource cache. **An AUTHORED capture of a drawing board writes `weather` as CLEAR** (#1483's `terrain_state_turns` shape), so Update never bakes the last draw into the file; a dev Load or F2 of such a board stands under a clear sky until a mission starts, as F2 stands up no roster.
- **The headless board has a store of its own, `board["weather"]`** (`BoardBuilder.apply_scenario` stands it up; `weather_of` reads it). Before #1509 the Play API read `scenario.weather` straight off the loaded resource, so a draw there would have written into the cache.
- **The roll is `MissionController.weather_rng`**, randomized once; a suite swaps in a seeded one. **The look does not follow the draw:** the board keeps its one look preset until #278's override layer keys on the weather.

**Snow since [#1269](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1269)** (Light Snow, Snow, Blizzard -- LOOKS with no rules file): a look's `Fall` picks what is drawn, and snow adds flakes, ground drift, a `SnowGround` cover decal (thinner on slopes and standing up off the ground through a normal map, [#1278](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1278)), caps on props and buried tall grass (`BoardMirror.set_snow`, the one door) and units (`UnitSprite3D.show_cap`), breath, and the weather's own grade (`WeatherGrade`, a canvas layer under the HUD). **Fog since [#1285](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1285)** (Mist, Fog, Thick Fog -- looks only): drifting pixel cards over a thin full-screen fog pass, both reading `fog_field.gdshaderinc` (the one answer to how thick fog is at a point) over the rain's mask and `FogGround` (where fog thins at an edge, and how deep it pools over its OWN ground, so it never stands as a box), both sorted under every piece of markup.

**Wind since [#1286](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1286)** (Calm, Breeze, Strong Wind, Gale, plus a direction -- a SECOND board axis beside the weather, looks only): `ScenarioData.wind`/`wind_direction`, `ScenarioManager.current_wind`, and `WindLook.vector` is the ONE composition, which battle3d hands every mirror as `wind_source`, so no weather or gas look carries a wind of its own. `WindMirror` draws blown specks and cloud shadows, both under a clear sky only; plants sway through `prop_sway`, which shares the engine-sprite copy with the status material. **Auroras since [#1298](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1298)** (Faint Aurora, Aurora, Aetheric Storm -- looks only): `AuroraLights`, a child of `WeatherMirror`, draws glow curtains on the ground (a full-screen pass, `BoardOverlays.make_screen_pass` and `screen_ground.gdshaderinc` being the one mount and ground lookup every such pass uses), an aurora light and rising motes, all on one colour cycle; the storm's strike corona is the look's `bolt_element`.

**Sand and ash since [#1302](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1302)** (Dust, Sandstorm, Dust Wall; Light Ashfall, Ashfall, Ash Storm -- looks only): **a fall draws PARTS**, and `WeatherLook`'s part lists (`FLAKES`, `COVER`, `CAPS`, `DRIFT`, `HAZE`, `GRIT`, `MOTES` and the rest) are the one answer that both `WeatherMirror._draws` and the page's rows read, so a row shows exactly where its part is drawn; sand adds airborne grit (`sand_grit.gdshader`, born over the board), ash embers (`RisingMotes`, the aurora's motes hoisted), and `water_haze` (`FogGround`'s B channel) thins the fog and its cards over water.

**Every system born in the camera's box is HELD at its cap** (`WeatherMirror.hold_emitter`, [#1304](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1304)): setting `amount` restarts a system and replays its whole lifetime in one frame, so re-sizing slow flakes on each zoom froze frames for up to 1.4 s; only `lifetime` (which restarts nothing, measured) and `keep` follow the view. A falling flake is born no higher than its look's `flake_ceiling` above the ground in view and lives only to land, fading by its age ([#1305](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1305)), so a zoomed-out view keeps its flakes around the board instead of up by the camera. Read `presentation-effects.md` → *Weather* before touching any of it, and re-run `tools/weather_probe/` after a shader change.

**The AIR is a third board value since [#1324](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1324)** (a rule input like the weather): `ScenarioData.atmosphere` → `ScenarioManager.current_atmosphere` (the AUTHORED reading) → `ScenarioManager.air_reading()` → `BoardContext.atmosphere`, a percentage per `Atmosphere.SUBSTANCES` (the one ambient list), and a carving moves a step per substance it draws from the air (`CarvingRecipe.drawn_from`). **What the board plays under is `Atmosphere.effective(authored, weather)`**, the authored reading moved by the weather's `WeatherRules.air` (#1324 slice 4: rain and fog add vapor, snow lowers heat, the auroras add aether), and every reader takes that one composition. A moved row names its weather. A player reads it on the pre-mission screen's CONDITIONS and, through the battle, in the corner HUD's off-Normal rows (`Atmosphere.off_normal`, so `game.refresh_mission_status`'s hide gate asks about the air too) — `deep-alchemy.md` → *What is built*. **A gas moves ONE cell's air since #1324 slice 5**: `GasRules.air` (points per level, `WeatherRules.air`'s shape) nudges that reading through the same `Atmosphere.nudged`, and `BoardContext.air_at(cell)` is the one answer for a cell, read by the resolver at the caster's position in the pass, the AI's seek key, the tile card's Air section and the Play API's board view; `atmosphere` stays the board's, for the board-wide readouts.

## A unit wears its element state

**A unit WEARS its element state ([#358](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/358) slice 1, 2026-09-22): `UnitSprite3D.show_status` is the one door, `UnitMirror` pushes it every frame (fade levels on the SCALED delta, one status clock, a per-unit seed), and the material exists ONLY while a state shows, the tint is brighter than the art, or the weather's snow cap shows ([#1269](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1269), `UnitSprite3D.show_cap`) -- since [#1251](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1251) `UnitSprite3D.set_tint` is the one tint door, because the engine sprite clamps its vertex colour at 1.0 and no brightening cue reached 3D before it.** `unit_status.gdshaderinc` is a **HAND COPY of the engine's own Sprite3D material** — declared, since 4.7.1 exposes no generated shader to read; the copy itself lives in `sprite_material.gdshaderinc` since #1286, shared with the plants' sway — plus the state branch; `tools/sprite_parity/` measures it pixel-identical (real sprite, ghost, atlas frame) and **must be re-run after any engine upgrade**. Where a mark may go is `StatusArt`'s pure scan (an effect map + the OVERHANG list the drips fall from), never shader logic. The ghost variant carries `render_priority` itself, because the engine applies it to its own material only (#317). Ghosts wear the unit they STAND IN FOR, through a `unit_id` `OverlayMirror` puts inside each ghost entry. Knobs are `StatusLook` on the Elemental page; hues are `ElementPalette.color_for_state`.

**Slice 2 (2026-09-23) throws the state into the WORLD**: `StatusWorld`, a child of `UnitMirror`, schedules drips + splash, mist and breath per unit on the mirror's scaled clock into four resident `StatusParticles` emitters (#656's rules), placed through `UnitSprite3D.texel_to_world`; a drip lands exactly by falling at the constant speed that ends its life on the surface. `tools/status_world_probe/` measured that GPU particles FREEZE at `Engine.time_scale = 0`. **The damp blot is a `Decal` whose `cull_mask` is the ground ALONE — and that is why render layer 1 is `BoardOverlays.GROUND_RENDER_LAYER` (the GridMaps, which cannot be re-layered, plus the hole lips) while `WORLD_RENDER_LAYER` is its own bit.** Any new 3D visual on the board must set `layers` or the blot darkens it, and since #1269 `PROP_RENDER_LAYER` (8) is the block props' alone, for the snow's cap decal; `tests/law/test_only_the_ground_takes_a_decal.gd` walks the live scene for exactly that. It lies at the unit's BOARD POINT: anything belonging to the ground must never be placed through `texel_to_world`, whose answer turns with the camera (the dev's playtest found the patch sliding across the tiles). A drip landing in it RIPPLES it, baked into the patch's own texture rather than drawn as a second decal, since Godot orders overlapping decals by camera distance.

**A decal's texture must be REASSIGNED to change, never `ImageTexture.update()`d** -- the renderer's decal atlas copies it once, on assignment (measured, 4.7.1). Canon: `presentation-effects.md` → *A unit WEARS its element state*.

## A unit sprite can play frames

**A unit sprite can play FRAMES ([#629](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/629), 2026-08-28) — the project's first frame animation of any kind.** Before it a unit's visual was ONE still `Texture2D` swapped between three authored stills (map/move/downed); there was no `AnimatedSprite2D`/`SpriteFrames`/`AnimationPlayer`/`AnimationTree` anywhere, and three tickets named that gap without owning it (#603, #358, and #414's deferral in shipped code at `AttackAction.gd:89-92`). **A frame is a RECT into one sheet, never a cut-out file** — `Sprite3D` honours an `AtlasTexture` region (only `StandardMaterial3D` quads do not, `BoardOverlays.gd:516`), so a wrong frame is a number to fix rather than a file to re-cut.

**`SpriteAnimator` (`presentation/`) is the clock and the frame choice and knows nothing about the node it drives**, which is what lets `frame_at` be static and pure — a test states an elapsed time and an expected frame instead of waiting on real ones. **TIME ARRIVES AS A PARAMETER**, the caller's own `_process` delta, already scaled by `Engine.time_scale` — so #520's hitstop freezes a swing with the world by construction rather than by being told. **`UnitSprite3D.texture` is ONE door now, `_apply_state_texture()`**: five places spelled that decision before, which is what made an animation a sixth writer with nothing arbitrating; an animation BORROWS the property against it (`CameraRig3D.stash_view()`'s shape — the gate, not the assignment, is what makes a borrow safe), and going down interrupts a swing.

**THERE IS NO ZOOM ART AND NO SHEET READER ([#139](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/139), 2026-09-14).** `Art/Units/ZoomAnimations/`, `Resources/ZoomAnimations/`, `CardSheet` and `tools/zoomanim/` are all DELETED — the four GBA sheets were the last Fire Emblem rips after [#937](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/937) took the map sprites, and the reader could only ever parse the one of them that was card-format (durations OCR'd off digits printed above each frame). They went together with no replacement: **the battle zoom has no art until [#603](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/603) resumes**, which is paused on [#635](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/635) building a timing-authoring tool — the dev's own next step, and one that supersedes reading timings off pixels. The format's rules are recorded in `docs/design/presentation-effects.md` as history; do not re-derive a reader from them without asking.

**What SURVIVED is the machinery, and the distinction is the point**: `SpriteAnimator` and `UnitSprite3D.play_animation` drive ANY `SpriteFrames`, so they are UNFED rather than dead and are the seam #603 returns to — `DevController`'s K hotkey still reaches for `Resources/ZoomAnimations/<family>.tres`, finds nothing, and says so. **A frame also has to be told WHERE IT HANGS FROM ([#634](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/634)): the pivot was `Vector2(0, 16)` — half of THIRTY-TWO, cut for map art — so a 66x42 card sat four texels into its own tile and, worse, on the card's horizontal centre, which is not where the character stands (the idle Sage stands at column 18.5 of 66; the rest is lunge room). The stand point is measured ONCE off the idle pose and stored on the `SpriteFrames` as metadata; per-frame ink is never touched, because that IS the registration.** Canon + the open half: `docs/design/presentation-effects.md`.
