## SquadManager

`SquadManager` — **the only owner of squad lifecycle**: create/destroy/join/leave. Member removal funnels through `Squad._erase_member()` (the sole `members.erase` caller, mirroring `_add_member`), reached only through `_erase_from()`, which emits `squad_member_left` with a `LeaveCause` ([#367](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/367)); its callers are `_detach_from_current_squad()` (single-unit) and `disband_squad()` (bulk). **The settle after a pass is FORECAST since #367 part 2A**: `SplitForecast` (`squads/`) replays it at plan time into `ResolvedOutcome.splits`, which the queue shows as a "Split" chip on the blow that causes it (and which the AI's score reads since [#761](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/761), so `resolve_hypothetical` stamps it as well as `resolve_plan`), sharing `successor_among`/`capacity_overflow` with `check_reassign_leader` rather than copying them; `tests/squad/test_split_forecast.gd` runs real passes to keep the two agreeing (Law #2).

**Part 2B has it name the LINKS too (`ResolvedOutcome.relinks`), and `OrderExecutor.after_the_blow` hands each landed outcome to `SquadTetherPresenter.foretell`, so the tether BREAK plays at the blow, inside the zoom; the settle's synchronous flush consumes that ledger rather than playing it twice** (`docs/design/visual-clarity.md` → *Part 2B*). **2A's leftovers closed after it**: the forecast also settles the END OF TURN burn and judges the settle on the ground the pass leaves (`BoardContext.with_deposits`), and a leader killed MID-WALK names its successor at once while its reach waits for the walk to end (`SquadManager.hold_handovers`), so animation no longer decides who stays (`visual-clarity.md` → *Part 2A's leftovers*). Also home of order queueing (the Law #3 `queue_action` chokepoint), activation state, plan resolution, and counter-attack resolution. **Every position the resolve reads is the THREADED one ([#1349](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1349), 2026-10-21):** `PlanResolver.projected_position(unit, hypo)`, never `get_projected_destination`. That covers a later aim's origin, a watch's anchor, counter reach and aim (`can_counter`, `choose_counter_target` and `calculate_reactions_for_squad` take the hypo; the AI, asking outside any pass, hands in an empty one), the rescue's reach and landings, and Burrow's cell. The projection is what `_publish_shoves` leaves for everyone OUTSIDE the pass: the attack prefix, meaning aim, payload and walk- or attack-phase watch-shot shoves, never a counter's or a tail shot's.

**Slimmed 770 → 516 lines 2026-07-26**: it had become the second dumping ground after game.gd — two of its sections were literally labelled *"migrated from game.gd (#22)"* — so three tenants moved out to **`SquadPlanValidator`** (plan-context validity, stamped onto each action), **`GroupMoveSolver`** (the pure formation solver; `queue_group_move` is the committing half), and **`ActionQueueDisplayEntry.build_for()`** (queue-panel layout, now beside the view model). All three are fully static — they read a plan and return a result, touching no squad state. **Queueing is STRICT and validity is one-way (2026-07-31): you cannot AUTHOR an invalid order, but a queued one can BECOME invalid.** `queue_action`'s third gate runs `validate_squad_plan_preview` and reads **the candidate's own `is_valid`, never `validate`'s return value** (that is whole-plan validity — one already-broken row would refuse everything queued after it). A break caused by a LATER re-plan leaves the order queued **in red, not deleted** — and for an *attack* that is the whole answer: a move is never refused for breaking a queued aim (dev fork, 2026-08-02, fork ii), the aim falls into invalid instead, because a planned heal does not lock its ally in place. The gate has a **second half** since then, `_candidate_aim_connects` — the whiff question, which only a candidate may ever be asked (see `SquadPlanValidator` below) — **and a third since #124 (2026-08-09), `_candidate_rescue_target_ok`**: a candidate rescue may target a unit that is down OR one the squad's last resolve predicts will be down by the side channel. That resolve is stored (`_last_resolved_plan`, read via `resolved_plan_for(squad)`; `ResolvedPlan.hypo` now survives the pass for exactly this) and is the right witness for a candidate the same way published knockback is — it covers the already-queued prefix, kept fresh by the synchronous queue→repaint→resolve chain, which the Play API rides too since #46 (`PlaySession._refresh_plan` on the same queue signals), so only a caller driving `SquadManager` bare, queueing twice with no resolve between, hits the same known coupling aims do. The lifecycle rule itself is ONE seam, `RulesService.is_rescueable(target, plan)` — menu candidate list, this gate clause, and the validator's plan-armed `_revalidate_rescue_targets` (outside the fixed-point loop beside the attack whiff clause; adjacency stays inside) all call it; plan-less callers (the AI builder) keep the live `is_downed` rule. (An interim clause excluding Crisis-eligible player allies lived here for the hours the live prompt outlasted #124; #158 deleted both — a Crisis-armed ally now predicts CRISIS and falls out of candidacy through the ordinary DOWNED filter.) A same-pass revive is still ejected and SPENT — `SquadManager.settle_downed` (both hosts' pass-end sweep since #46) marks the fresh solo squad, because at `RescueAction.execute` time the solo squad doesn't exist yet — and execute's `is_downed()` guard firing is now a `push_error` (post-#155, a wrong lethality prediction is a bug, never a fizzle). This collapsed three hand-synced answers to "may this be ordered" (menu gate, click handlers, AI builders); `_click_choosing_move`'s private leader-range copy went with it. The hypothetical list displaces via **`Squad.displaces()`**, the same predicate `_queue_action` uses.

**Batched orders skip the gate** — a formation is one decision, judged whole by `queue_group_move`. Refusals are silent (dev call) -- at QUEUE time, in the game; the chokepoint does name its reason since #662, for the Play API and the replay viewer; a refused EXECUTE answers back since [#1121](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1121) (`visual-clarity.md` → *A refused Execute says why*). Rationale, forks and the three traps (leaf ordering, rollback scope, the knockback/resolve dependency): [`docs/build-log.md`](../build-log.md). **Order batching (`SquadManager.batching`, 2026-07-26):** Group Move queues N orders as ONE player action, so the expensive squad-level repaint runs once at the end rather than per order (a 5-member click went 232 ms → 65 ms). Every order still passes `queue_action`'s gates (Law #3), and `action_queued` still fires per order so cheap per-UNIT listeners don't miss anyone.

**It is safe only while a batch stays synchronous** — if one ever spans a frame, the queue panel goes stale and Law #2 breaks. Invariants + measurements: [`docs/performance.md`](../performance.md).

## Squad

`Squad` — member list + `action_queue` (player-authored orders only). Enforces one order per action-type per unit (volley siblings exempt — see `_is_volley_sibling`). *(Corrected 2026-10-20: `Squad.displaces()` also allows only one MAIN action per unit.)*

## The squads/ folder: SquadPlanValidator, SquadCohesion, GroupMoveSolver

`squads/` — `Squad`, `SquadManager`, `Team`; `SquadPlanValidator` (fixed-point plan validity: leader range, destination conflicts, rescue adjacency re-checks — **and those project BOTH sides since [#126](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/126) (2026-08-08), asymmetrically and on purpose**: the ACTOR is inside the plan being validated so it keeps `projected_cell_for`'s two load-bearing falses, while the TARGET is not — a rescue target is a body in its own solo squad — so it reads `get_projected_destination()`, the same board fact `BoardContext.projected_unit_at_cell` and the board preview read. `RulesService.adjacent_downed_allies` had the identical half-projected bug (rescuer projected, body's cell live) and now asks `projected_unit_at_cell`; **both were correct only by accident, because a downed unit could not move** — a damageless shove can now reposition one, and half-projecting gets it exactly backwards, offering the body on the cell it is about to vacate while refusing the rescue aimed where it lands. Not a fixed-point hazard: the target's own squad's validity is settled and the published knockback is constant across the loop, so rescue validity stays the leaf it always was. `RescueAction.execute` carries a live-cell adjacency backstop for the same reason `_revalidate_unit_attacks` exists — the validator's stamp is computed before the pass runs, and a mid-pass shove is exactly what separates rescuer from body between the stamp and the act. Since [#1349](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1349) the resolve asks that same reach question of its own cells (`RescueAction.resolved_within_reach`, one rule in `RescueAction.within_reach`), and the plan-armed `_revalidate_rescue_targets` reds the order on it, so a counter that shoves the rescuer away turns the row red instead of fizzling at execute — **cohesion is STRICT for every move whatever authored it, and Group Move is refused at the OVERLAY rather than at the queue (2026-08-04)**: the leash is applied identically to an individually ordered move, a solver placement, and the hold-position filler — so a leader can never walk out of range of its own squadmates.

**The rule itself is `SquadCohesion` (`squads/`) since 2026-08-06, and the metric is PATH DISTANCE over terrain the member can traverse (#151), not Manhattan** — walls block cohesion (a Manhattan-close member behind a castle wall is not in the squad; that pretence froze a Rushdown squad for a whole battle, #127). One seam, two forms that cannot drift (`in_range` decides, `cells`/`field` draw — a bounded `path_hops` from the leader's cell): the validator, the solver, the squad-up gates, `compute_move_range`'s clamp, the three overlay draws and the ejection sweep all route through it, which is what made the metric swap a one-function edit — eleven sites used to hand-roll the Manhattan compare. Per-member (a Waterwalker's bubble crosses water; flyers later), occupancy-blind always (an enemy body never severs a squad — the pin test calls `path_hops`' default form).

**The leash LENGTH is read where the leader stands, since #1335 (2026-10-22).** Smoke blinds whoever stands in it, and a blind leader holds its squad closer: `SquadCohesion.reach_at(leader, center, board)` is COH plus the COH change of every state the gas at `center` gives while standing (`GasRules.states_while_standing`), floored at 0 because a negative bound is `path_hops`' UNBOUNDED. `field_of` reads it at its `center`, so every caller that centres on the leader's cell got smoke with no wiring of its own: the validator, `compute_move_range`'s clamp, the three overlay draws, the ejection sweep, the squad-up gates and `SplitForecast` (whose landed board folds a pass's own smoke in). **The one caller that does not centre on the leader is `GroupMoveSolver.stranding`**: it walks from each MEMBER cell, path distance being symmetric, so reading `reach_at` there read the smoke under the member. Found by the leader-into-smoke case going red after the plan had assumed every caller centred on the leader. It now works out the leash at each destination, walks to the longest of them (`field_within`) and accepts a destination only if the distance is within that destination's own leash. That is exact, costs the same as before, and changes nothing on a smokeless board. Walking from each destination instead was the rejected alternative: same answer, about 3.5 times the walks per member on every move-mode entry. **A new cohesion caller that walks from anywhere but the leader's cell must do the same comparison.**

**The board it walks arrives via `SquadManager.board_source`, a Callable returning a FRESH `BoardContext` per query** — terrain state changes mid-battle (FROZEN melts), so caching a board would validate against a world that no longer exists; game/play/test fixtures each wire their own source once at bootstrap, and `SquadPlanValidator.validate` takes the board as an explicit parameter. **`SquadManager.enforce_contact()` is #151's backstop**: movement can no longer author a split (the validator refuses it), but a shove or melting ice still can — an out-of-contact member ejects to a solo squad at the THREE settle points (end of `execute_orders`, turn start after ticks, and since #772 a pre-mission REPOSITION -- which is the one the player performs deliberately, so its ejection lands while they are still looking at the board that caused it rather than a turn later), mirroring downed ejection, and since #367 part 2A a pass's ejections are FORECAST by `SplitForecast` (the turn-start and pre-mission ones are not); also the self-heal path for pre-#151 saves. *(Corrected 2026-10-20: true for a MEMBER's move only. `SquadPlanValidator._check_leader_range` skips the leader, so a leader move that strands members is refused only at the player UI (#1069). The AI and the Play API can still author one, and `enforce_contact` ejects the members afterwards.)* COH default went 3 → 4 with the swap (path ≥ Manhattan, so the old number was a strictly tighter leash).

**It was briefly relaxed to let an outrun member trail, and put back the same day**: the relaxation reached every squadmate move, not just group-move placements, and a member could then be individually ordered *and executed* far outside its leader's range (12 of 40 tiles the red INVALIDMOVE overlay was painting had started accepting orders). **The generalizable lesson — a rule read by more than one surface must be justified at every surface, not at the one that motivated the change.** Group Move now splits into two visible cases: **Case 1**, the member falls behind but stays in range — legal, and `GroupMoveSolver` sets `MoveAction.is_trailing` (it ends *further* from the leader than it started) so `OverlayManager._arrow_modulate` draws its arrow GREEN; **Case 2**, the member cannot reach the range at all — refused, and `GroupMoveSolver.followable_destinations` marks those destinations so `enter_group_move_mode` paints them the same GREY GRID a squadmate's own out-of-range tiles get (a mauve wash until [#1070](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1070)), and a click on one SHAKES the tether it would break and keeps the pick open, exactly as there. That sweep is one `compute_move_range` per member plus a dilation, NOT a `plan()` per destination — the real solver is 4–6 ms a cell, i.e. 176 ms to filter a 44-cell range, and the sweep costs the SAME for 1 destination as for 38 (it is per-squad work).

**Placement is most-constrained-first, and that is load-bearing, not a tidy-up**: assignment is first-come-first-served with no backtracking, so a member with two options must pick before one with twelve — member order silently dropped **6 of 16** green destinations for one Castle Assault squad, i.e. a third of that squad's range clicked and did nothing, on a 952/952 green suite. `_candidate_cells` therefore has no `taken` filter (options for every member are computed before any placement, because knowing who is constrained requires it), and moves are **emitted in member order** so queue rows don't reshuffle. `followable_destinations` also requires *distinct reachable cells ≥ member count* — the genuinely impossible case (a one-wide corridor collapsing the squad onto one tile), which no ordering can fix; it is necessary rather than sufficient and wrongly refused nothing on either authored board. Three consequences to know: `RulesService.compute_move_range` filters occupancy BEFORE cohesion, because `reachable + squad_unreachable` became the standable footprint that sweep unions; the leash-as-PREFERENCE retry `_candidate_cells` used to carry (drop the leash when it leaves nothing) is **deleted as of #151** — with cohesion itself path-based, the solver's `leader_field` IS `SquadCohesion.field`, the same field the validator refuses against, so the two can no longer disagree and a member with no candidate genuinely cannot follow; and `queue_group_move`'s rollback calls `revert_if_only_hold`, since a refused batch that leaves a hold-only queue used to strand the squad `active` behind a panel with no X and no Execute — the reported symptom, *"a ghost order that did nothing"*. Residual: the sweep cannot see two members contending for one bubble cell (`taken`), so a green tile can still be refused, rarely, and now reverts cleanly. Story + the measured 40-of-44 reproduction: [`docs/build-log.md`](../build-log.md) → *Group Move's two cases*. Pinned by `tests/squad/test_squad_cohesion.gd` — plus **`_revalidate_unit_attacks`, which runs OUTSIDE the fixed point** because attack validity is a *leaf* (nothing reads it, so it can read SETTLED move validity instead of the loop's half-computed output) **and only when handed a `ResolvedPlan` (2026-08-02)**. *"Does this aim still hit anyone" is the RESOLVE's answer, not a re-derivation:* a shove puts its target on a **sequence** of cells across one plan, and only `resolve_plan`'s loop walks that sequence in order, expanding and resolving each aim before the next. Judged instead against the settled end state — which is what shipped 2026-08-01 — every aim that knocked its target back went hunting for its victim on the cell it had just cleared, so a **single Blowback dulled Execute**, and the same read made an aim's validity depend on shoves ordered *after* it. `resolve_plan` now stamps `AttackAction.source_aim` on every action it derives, and the clause asks the plan whether that aim found anybody; a validate with **no plan leaves attacks untouched** rather than guessing. The one exception is a CANDIDATE the resolver has never seen — `aim_finds_a_target` (the ex-`_projected_victim_for`, now public, still the only reader of `Unit.projected_cell(…, true, true)` rather than `projected_cell_for`'s two falses) is asked by `SquadManager`'s queue-time gate **alone**, and is correct there precisely because the published knockback is the ALREADY-QUEUED aims' shoves — exactly the prefix a new aim lands after. Both sources feed one policy, `aim_whiffs`; that split is a *declared* second representation per Law #4, and asking the predictor about a stored aim is the bug it replaced) and `GroupMoveSolver` (the Group Move formation solver — pure and static, which is what lets one call serve both the hover preview and the committed order; its `_path_hops` BFS takes two optional stopping rules, `max_depth` and `until`, because neither caller ever needed the whole field. *(Corrected 2026-10-20: the BFS is now `RulesService.path_hops`, which `GroupMoveSolver` calls; its stopping rules are unchanged.)*

**Both only skip work whose answer was already going to be discarded**, so the plan is unchanged — see [`docs/performance.md`](../performance.md) before widening either).
