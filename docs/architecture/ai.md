## Archetype AI

**Archetype AI (`ai/`, #29, built 2026-07-01):** `AIController` (child of Game) runs AI-controlled factions — per squad (the FIRST one planned inside the turn hand-off beat, after the faction claims the board, since [#1220](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1220) ruling 7: the cost hides behind the banner): archetype plans → the same `OrderExecutor.execute_orders` the player's Execute button calls, which paces its own sequential phases off the same `is_ai_faction` read the concede below makes (`Pacing.AI_ACTION` vs `PLAYER_ACTION`, so a hotseat faction with AI off paces like the human it is). **The camera is not `AIController`'s since #987**: an AI squad's opening shot is its walk's own framing in `OrderExecutor`'s move phase (`Pacing.AI_SQUAD_PAN`), followed there by **a beat while the drawn plan is readable** (`Pacing.AI_PLAN_READ`, #118 — skipped when `only_hold_actions`, since a squad with nothing to do would just add dead air). Orders go through `SquadManager.queue_action`/`queue_group_move` ONLY (Law #3).

**An AI squad's turn must ALWAYS terminate (#103, 2026-07-29):** `execute_orders`' invalid-plan guard is a *player affordance* — flash the refused units, shake their rows and say why (reachable by a click only since #1121, when Execute stopped being truly disabled over red orders), leave the plan queued, hand control back — and its early `return` sits above everything that ends a turn. An AI squad refused there has nobody to hand control back to, so it left the board dressed for a plan that never ran (ghosts, path arrows, `get_projected_destination()` pointing at an unreached cell — which is what enemy AI then swung at) with `has_acted` false and `active_squad` still set; nothing in the cycle mutated the state that produced the plan, so the identical plan was refused **every turn, forever**. Two fixes, both needed: the guard now routes an AI squad through `_end_squad_turn` (keyed on `is_ai_faction`, i.e. the ENABLED toggle — a hotseat faction with AI off is a *human* and keeps its plan), and the group move gives up the **advance** rather than the turn when the plan doesn't validate, by cancelling every member's move back to the all-hold plan the squad started with (legal by construction).

**That rollback lives in `SquadManager.queue_group_move` as of 2026-07-31** (was `AITactics.queue_group_move_if_coherent`, deleted — strict queueing made all-or-nothing every caller's rule). Its scope must be **every MOVE in the queue, not just the batch's own**: a member the solver could place nowhere has no move in the batch, it keeps the hold order it already had, and that is the order cohesion refuses. **A batch fails two ways as of [#443](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/443) (2026-08-21), and the rollback serves both**: INVALID (above) plus REFUSED — an order `queue_action` turns down never reaches the queue, so scanning the queue for invalid moves is structurally blind to it, and the count of orders that landed versus orders authored is what sees it. Move-before-main is the refusal that fires: a leader holding a main action had its own move dropped while every follower's was queued, so the squad walked off and left it, and `queue_group_move` returned true. `MainActionMenu._can_move` is now the single gate both movement rows read (Group Move's hand-copy had drifted and lost the main-action clause), but the menu is only one door — the Play API and the AI call `queue_group_move` directly.

**[#417](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/417) (2026-08-21) then took the already-queued-move clause OUT of `_can_move` and put it on the Group Move row alone**: picking Move over a queued move CANCELS it (`cancel_move_for_unit` + `revert_if_only_hold`, the pairing both its other callers already use) and re-enters planning, so backing out leaves NO move; re-planning a whole formation followed in [#461](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/461) the same day, which also closed the half #443 could not see: `_can_move` answers only about the unit whose MENU is open, so a SQUADMATE holding a main action rolled the whole formation back from a row the gate still offered. `_can_group_move` asks `_can_move` of every member, at the Group Move caller -- Move stays a per-unit question. That clause is SCOPE at the caller, not a survivor of the hand-copy — see `docs/design/visual-clarity.md`.

**Round 2 the same day put that gesture behind RIGHT-CLICK too**: `game.begin_move_planning` is the one door both the menu row and `_pop_last_gesture`'s new lone-move branch call, so the press cycles `move queued -> planning -> nothing`; `game.enter_move_mode` deliberately stays the BARE mode entry (thirteen `tests/presentation/` call sites use it only to paint the overlay), and `game.select_unit` is the extracted select write point (#107) the re-entry needs. Root cause of the illegal plan: `GroupMoveSolver` places a member nowhere when no cell inside the leader's NEW cohesion range is free, and it then keeps the hold-position order `setup_hold_move_actions` gave it — which `SquadPlanValidator._check_leader_range` refuses.

**That root cause is UNCHANGED, but the player no longer meets it (2026-08-04 — see the cohesion rule under `squads/` below).** `GroupMoveSolver.followable_destinations` paints such destinations red before the click, so a human is refused at the overlay; the AI does not read that overlay, authors the refusal anyway, and everything in this paragraph still describes its path exactly. **That is NOT a group-move bug** — a single individual leader move produces the identical refusal (measured), because the filler is queued by `SquadManager` itself the moment a squad's plan opens, regardless of who authored the move.

**It is SquadManager's since #46, for every host**: it used to be a `game.gd` signal handler, so `play/board_builder.gd` boards never grew it and the Play API and the game disagreed about whether a plan was legal; the Play API also refuses a leader destination that would strand its squad, as the game's move modes do (`GroupMoveSolver.stranding`), and has a `group_move` verb. Per-unit AI movement is [#117](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/117), the evergreen AI catch-up tracker. Two independent layers, and **both are board content as of [#150](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/150) (2026-08-06)**: **ENABLED** is per-faction, carried by `ScenarioData.ai_factions` and applied on every load — the Scenario-tab checkboxes are the live override, no longer the only source. The set is **REPLACED, never merged** (`AIController.set_ai_factions` clears first, so an unlisted faction falls back to `DEFAULT_ENABLED`); `clear_board` zeroes it beside `mission_controller.reset()`, which is what keeps `spawn_sandbox()` manual. It was session-only until then, and the cost was **not** only the dev re-ticking checkboxes every launch: commanding is gated on `unit.get_faction() == turn_manager.active_faction()` — i.e. hotseat — so a mission that declared nobody did not stall on the enemy turn, it **handed the player its own enemies**, which is what `Scenarios/missions/Prolog.tres` shipped doing. That rule is `BoardLint._check_ai_factions` as of [#390](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/390) — it lived inline in `tests/flow/test_scenario_load_integrity.gd` until the Check board button gave it a second reader, and a rule stated in two places is two rules. `tests/dev/test_board_lint.gd` WARNS about any board holding ENEMY units that doesn't list ENEMY, and deliberately does **not** refuse one (2026-08-26 — see the DEGRADES ruling below); note the SCOPE stayed at the caller rather than travelling with the rule (the test asks it of `missions/` only, because root and `fixtures/` saves are deliberately manual, while the button has no path to scope by and asks unconditionally).

**An AI-less board SHIPS, because the dev authors them: "I want to be able to have levels with no AI. controlling both sides is important to testing. If the tests and lint are redding that out, they should be warnings, not hard stops."** So the finding sits at DEGRADES, which is the tier `test_every_shipped_mission_is_playable` does not collect — and it cannot go silent instead, since `ai_factions` defaults to empty and a board he chose is indistinguishable from one he never thought about. Scoped to ENEMY on purpose — NEUTRAL/ALLY AI stances are undecided. Watch the flag's *other* reader when touching it: it also decides whether an invalid plan concedes or hands control back (`OrderExecutor:43`, #103) — a leaked flag reads as a squad silently conceding turns, not as "the AI moved". (It used to gate the Crisis prompt too, until #158 deleted the prompt.) **ARCHETYPE** is per-squad and persists with the scenario (`Squad.archetype`, an `AIArchetype.Type` — saved with `squad_name`/`squad_zone` on the leader's `ScenarioUnitEntry`; the sentry post = the leader's saved `cell`). A unit that LEAVES a squad and stays on the board keeps the old squad's archetype and zone in its solo squad, never its name or post ([#1196](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1196), dev 2026-10-03), so a Hold squad's rescued member still holds.

**HOW WELL a unit plays is its AI PROFILE since [#1230](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1230)** (2026-10-05): a few fixed bands (Easy / Medium / Hard in `Resources/AIProfiles/`), per UNIT by file name (`Unit.ai_profile`, authored on `ScenarioUnitEntry`, unassigned = Hard), the LEADER's deciding where a squad decides once; one `AIProfile` knob per behaviour, read at the rule it governs, with notches rather than dice; and loose enemies REGROUP into squads (`AIController.regroup`). Canon: ai-tactics.md → *Difficulty bands* and *Regrouping*. Archetypes (`AIArchetype` enum+registry, `AITactics` shared queries): RUSHDOWN (best exchange among anyone attackable this turn, else nearest → path → attack), HOLD (attack in reach, never move), SENTRY (its painted zone limits where it walks, never what it hits, since [#1377](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1377): it engages anyone it can attack this turn from zone∪post, wherever they stand, with every destination clamped to zone∪post — `GroupMoveSolver.plan(…, allowed_cells)` leashes members too (NB: the allow-list clamps MEMBERS only; the archetype picks the leader's destination itself); nobody in reach → pursue an intruder inside the zone; nobody there either → walk back to post, and at the post attack whatever is in reach; zone missing → Hold; ai-tactics.md → *The Sentry's leash is footing, not reach*), BALANCED ([#1220](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1220): best exchange with anyone in reach, else the nearest enemy that could not answer it, re-picked every turn; the one archetype that weighs SAFETY, how many enemies could reach a cell, and it carries Hold's whole action list -- ai-tactics.md → *Balanced*).

**WHERE A FIGHTING SQUAD STANDS SEEKS A REMOVAL OR A SQUAD BREAK since [#760](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/760)** (dev, 2026-10-03: only those two pull a unit off its usual cell, and the squad is one unit -- the leader first, then the closest member that can do it): `AITactics.seek_positions` runs inside `engage` before the group move, scores each cell by STANDING the unit on it (the only way, since the resolve re-expands every aim from its actor's projected cell, #15), and pins the winners through `GroupMoveSolver.plan(…, pinned)`. Damage alone moves nobody -- **except a MEMBER since #1220** (ruling 9): one with nobody to hit from its formation cell steps to a cell inside the leash it can hit from, one with nobody to hit anywhere walks beside a body if its archetype rescues, and one with NOTHING TO FIRE falls back out of reach; Hold never moves; and no cell is ended on a hazard (end-of-turn damage, a hostile watch, and for a leader the round's smoke drifting onto it, #1514) while a safe one offers the same, within a tier. ai-tactics.md → *Seeking a removal or a squad break* and *Members who find nothing*.

**A DOWNED ENEMY IS AN ORDINARY TARGET THAT LOSES HEAD TO HEAD (#57 fork 3, rewritten by [#720](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/720), dev 2026-09-03).** Every selector used to answer about the STANDING first and consult bodies only if that found nobody, which is absolute precedence rather than preference — and in the layer that decides where to STAND it meant a body one step away lost to an enemy on the far side of a wall, so five attackers spent thirteen rounds revving beside a downed general. One ranking each now: pursuit `hops > standing > distance`, engagement `standing > safe > hops`, attack the score alone. What made it safe to delete is that the score already said it — a body clings at 1 HP, so the clamp prices finishing one at +1 and it earns no removal. Zones: `ZoneManager` (name→cells, game child), painted via the Tile Brush tab's Zone mode, persisted in `ScenarioData.zones`, drawn on `ZoneOverlay` ONLY while the Tile Brush tab is up. During AI turns `GameState.AI_TURN` locks all board/queue-panel input and the camera.

**Crisis is an EQUIPPED ABILITY, not an AI stance ([#158](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/158), 2026-08-09 — replacing #57's per-archetype `CRISIS_STANCES` table, which is deleted along with `CrisisPrompt`, the offer poll, and `Unit.is_crisis_eligible`):** a unit holding `Abilities.Id.CRISIS` (the Berserker job's pool; any kit source works) that takes a would-be-down while NOT WOUNDED (it was FULL Will until #1174, which left the gambit unreachable below WIL 20) enters Crisis **directly inside `Unit.take_damage`** — never DOWNED, no `went_downed`, no ejection, no prompt, any faction. `LethalityRules.crisis_armed_for(body)` is the one arming read, asked of the body (the pass's planned copy, or the live unit) on a would-be-down, so `ResolvedOutcome.Lethality.CRISIS` previews for anyone armed — the player included. Enemy Crisis access is authored content (put the ability on the unit), which is also the whole balance lever now.

**The rung ladder itself is `LethalityRules.predict()` (`actions/resolution/`, static) as of 2026-07-27** — preview and execution used to be two hand-synced implementations (`PlanResolver._predict_lethality` and `Unit._select_lethal_rung`), which is exactly the drift Law #2 forbids; now `Unit.take_damage` and the resolver call the same function, the ladder takes the BODY, a `UnitState` (the pass's planned copy of it, or the live one; until #1480 stage 3 `PlanResolver._Hypo` extended a `LethalityRules.Situation`), and `OVERKILL_CEILING` moved off `Unit` to sit with the decision that reads it (`CRISIS_WILL_GATE` went with Will, #1174, and the limb thresholds `LIMB_LOSS_DAMAGE`/`_WOUNDED` joined it: `LethalityRules.severs` answers whether a BLOW takes a limb, beside the rung, for both callers) (`Unit` keeps `CRISIS_REVIVE_HP`/`CRISIS_SURGE*` — those are the gambit's effects, and Unit still owns carrying a named rung out).

**#78 tactics catch-up BUILT 2026-07-22, CLOSED 2026-07-23 (design ratified same day; canon `docs/design/ai-tactics.md`):** the AI now walks the player's WHOLE declare flow — **pick** via `Unit.active_attack` (probing every `get_selectable_attacks()` candidate, `is_attack_fireable`-filtered; extras + rune carvings finally visible), **declare** via `AttackAction.declare()` (the one factory that stamps `fired_attack` — game.gd's click handler and `play_session.queue_attack` use it too; the rune-fists bug was exactly the AI skipping this stamp, leaving the resolver's null-fallback to punch with bare STR), **queue** via `queue_action` unchanged. Candidate scoring is JOINT and MARGINAL as of [#117](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/117) (2026-09-02): `AITactics._queue_attacks_jointly` re-resolves the squad's REAL plan each round, scores every remaining member's every candidate as `score(plan + candidate) − score(plan)` through `SquadManager.resolve_hypothetical`, and queues the single best — so the second member decides knowing what the first committed to. It replaced a throwaway ONE-VOLLEY plan against the LIVE board, which is why members double-spent on a target the first already downed, could not see a finishing blow, and **could not open a combo at all** (a damageless set-up scores `(0,0)` and the bar is *beat* `(0,0)`); one step of lookahead prices a set-up by its best squadmate follow-up. Three rules make the ledger honest and each was found the hard way: a REMOVAL is per VICTIM (a change of standing across the plan), never per hit — the ladder answers `KILLED` for any damaging hit on a downed body, so per-hit counting re-paid the overkill; OVERKILL IS WORTH NOTHING (damage capped at the victim's starting HP), without which full damage on a downed body ties with the same number on a fresh enemy and focus fire falls to board order; and **a reaction's DAMAGE is not scored, only its removals** (dev, 2026-09-02) — at par a counter cancels exactly, so every even trade scores `(0,0)` and a squad facing mirror-statted enemies declines every attack.

**The pass is NOT pure and restores the real plan BEFORE it queues as well as after**: `queue_action`'s whiff gate reads published knockback and is correct only because that knockback is the already-queued aims', so a leftover candidate shove made every knockback attack refuse itself as a whiff. **It is NOT the only reader, and that claim stood here until [#709](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/709) (2026-09-04):** `gather_attack_victims` resolves occupants through PROJECTED cells (#105), so the candidate BUILDER reads it too -- which is why the restore-before-queue was not enough on its own and every member's candidate list is now built against the base plan BEFORE the first hypothetical. Fallback verbs stay the ratified per-unit priority walk with ATTACK removed from the list.

**FELLING SOMEONE STILL STANDING BEATS FINISHING A BODY — a RANKING since [#720](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/720) (2026-09-03), a hard precedence before it** (dev, 2026-09-02 — the reason was the player's rescue window). #57's two passes were the gate; one pass over every target replaced them, because the arithmetic already said the same thing and said it more precisely (a body at 1 HP is worth +1 through the clamp and earns no removal). **The one comparison a body wins is a ruling of its own**: beside a standing target whose counter would FELL the attacker, the score decides — a free finish at (0,+1,0) over a suicidal swing at (-1,d,-x).

**A TARGET THE PLAN HAS ALREADY KILLED IS NOT ONE** ([#719](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/719)): pass 2 admitted anyone `is_downed()` on the LIVE board and never asked the hypo pass 1 had asked since #117, which is how a leader queued her `hits_allies` line down her own party at a corpse for a marginal of (0,-20,0); hypo-DOWNED is still a target, hypo-DEAD is not. REFUSAL survives from the old two-pass bookkeeping — a candidate `queue_action` turned down is skipped before it can count. **The LOOKAHEAD's floor moved in the same ticket**, and it is the one thing that would have shipped broken: it started at `Vector3i.ZERO` and REPLACED the solo score, which launders a negative into a zero once nothing has to beat zero — a set-up worth (-1,0,-5) came back (0,0,0) and outranked an honest swing at (-1,8,-4), so a member facing a lethal counter soaked instead of hitting and died dealing nothing. It floors at the candidate's own solo score now.

**And a candidate is judged against the PLAN, never the live board** — planning does not execute, so a unit a squadmate felled THIS round is still `is_active()`; `_attack_candidates` asks `base_plan.hypo`, which is why `_best_candidate_for`/`_lookahead` take the base PLAN rather than its score. **WHO the squad fights is TWO SYSTEMS as of 2026-09-02 (dev ruling, from playtest), forked on "is anybody attackable this turn?"** — somebody is → the best EXCHANGE, distance only the tie-break; nobody is → pursue the NEAREST, unchanged. `AITactics.choose_engagement_target` is the fork and both moving archetypes call it; `nearest_enemy` survives as the pursuit half.

**Pursuit stays nearest for DESIGN reasons**: it is Rushdown's identity (a rusher hunting the softest target across the board is BALANCED wearing the wrong name — which is now that archetype's written definition), it rewards screening a mage behind a frontline, and it is legible enough to bait and funnel. **Sentry passes two leashes, and since #1377 each half of the fork reads one** — `allowed` (zone + post) is the cell it would fight FROM and the only leash engagement asks, so an enemy outside the zone that a zone cell can reach is fought; `within` (the zone) tests the enemy's own cell, and only pursuit reads it. The exchange term is a BOOLEAN (can they answer me from the cell I would attack from), asked through `SquadManager.can_counter`, which **takes the attacker's cell as a parameter** rather than AITactics re-deriving reach — Law #4's own "pass it in" clause, since a hypothetical move moves nobody while `_resolve_actions` reads positions off the LIVE queue (a cell CAN be scored by standing the unit on it, which #760's seek does for a removal or a squad break only).

**One BFS, legality filtered BEFORE it**: asking for the globally nearest firing cell and testing THAT excludes an enemy whose nearest cell is outside the leash when another inside it would serve, and costs a BFS per enemy (+21% measured). The score gained a THIRD term for the same ruling, `-damage taken from reactions`, strictly below damage dealt so it cannot revive the parked squad — and it is what stops the ATTACK pick undoing the TARGET pick by tying on damage from the settled cell. **The two layers judge an exchange differently on purpose** (boolean here, graded damage there) — declared in ai-tactics.md, because this layer cannot resolve and the other can. It compares an `AIScore` lexicographically since #1220 -- `(mission, removals, splits, saves, limbs, net damage, damage taken)`, a mission-ending kill of a protected unit above everything, a heal on an ally priced like damage, a heal stabilising a body nobody can rescue as a save, and a limb taken off someone standing above damage, both ways (#1230) -- and before that `(net removals, net squad breaks, net damage dealt, -damage taken)` — the squad-break term since [#761](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/761) (dev, 2026-10-03: above damage, below a removal), counted per unit off `SplitForecast.leavers`, skipping a unit the plan also removes: active enemies count +, ANY ally counts − (net-damage doctrine), downed enemies only in a second pass (#57 preserved), predicted CRISIS is priced by the acting unit's profile since #1230 -- one that sees it, at the HP the hit really takes and no removal; one blind to it, as the kill it would have been if the gambit did not exist (#708, sighted to the counter it draws); a candidate is queued WHATEVER ITS SIGN since #711 deleted the bar, save one that FELLS ITS OWN SIDE (#1220 ruling 1: its own volley or a drowning its deposits cause; a counter it draws is not its work) -- the score ORDERS, it never gates (dev, 2026-09-02: "the AI should ALWAYS attack if there is an option to, and if all the options are weighed bad, it has to pick its least bad option"), which also deleted the freeze where no opener could net positive against a whole defending party's counters; and a unit with no selectable attacks (unarmed / aura-dry rune) has NO candidate since [#1215](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1215) (dev ruling, 2026-10-04: the AI loses the punch) -- the player's ring offers it no attack row, which #102 had made true as a side effect while the AI went on punching, and the danger field, Guard's exposure count and the Play API's unnamed pick now agree that nothing to fire threatens nothing. Per-archetype policy is declared on `AIArchetype`: `MAIN_ACTION_PRIORITY` (ordered try-list — Rushdown [ATTACK, RELOAD, REV]; Hold/Sentry/Balanced [ATTACK, RESCUE, OVERWATCH, RELOAD, REV, BURROW, GUARD] since [#751](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/751), 2026-09-04); the OVERWATCH and GUARD builders run after the squad's attacks are queued, so since [#1209](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1209) they decide on the board that plan leaves (`AIController.stand_on_projected`, lifecycle through the plan's hypo), never the live one + `MAIN_ACTION_NEVER` (BURROW on Rushdown, because a rusher does not entrench; Rally and Intimidate were retired by [#1174](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1174)). `AITactics.queue_main_action` walks the list. Its ATTACK arm runs `_queue_attacks_jointly` with the unit as its only chooser, so a direct call chooses exactly as the squad's pass does ([#1445](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1445) deleted the arm's own chooser, `_try_best_attack`); production never takes that arm, because the one production caller of `queue_main_action` erases ATTACK from its list once the squad's attacks are queued jointly. Every other verb has a `_try_*` builder (`_try_rescue`/`_try_reload`/`_try_rev`/`_try_burrow`, plus `_try_overwatch`/`_try_guard`); the first four each mirror `MainActionMenu`'s gate, with deterministic tie-breaks (rescue = lowest downed clock); an unmatched type is a `push_error`, never a silent skip.

**WHEN a weapon verb is worth doing is the FAMILY's call (`Classes/ai/weapons/AIWeaponRoutine.gd`, #726)** — one routine per `WeaponData.WeaponType`, every family declared (the inert base is a legal declaration; `tests/law/test_ai_weapon_routine_coverage.gd` pins it, a rune or empty hand resolves to the base), and **a routine only ever says NO**: it refuses a weapon verb (`RELOAD`/`REV`/`BURROW` only, never rescue) before its builder runs — the Drill will not re-dig a covered cell, read at the PROJECTED destination where the deposit lands — or DEFERS one of its own attack candidates inside `_best_candidate_for` — the Springspear saves its disarming Spring for a line or a kill. Deferral is member-local and never a delete: a deferred candidate is still taken when the member has nothing else, so #711 stays literal, and it never rides `_Scored` into the joint loop (that would be #720's cross-member tier back). A preparation is a RULE, never a score term (the predictability contract prefers the legible sentence); REV stays an unconditional fallback with no ATTACK preemption, and the mace needs no routine because the score already prices a shove that denies the counter (dev, 2026-09-03). A Sentry AT ITS POST takes the whole of `queue_main_actions_for_squad` since #1377, attacking whatever is in reach, with the fallback verbs only when nothing is (from #726 until then it took the fallback walk alone, `queue_fallback_actions_for_squad`, and never an attack). Canon: `ai-tactics.md`, *Weapon routines*.

**`tests/law/test_ai_action_coverage.gd` pins the partition — every `MAIN_ACTION_TYPES` member must land in exactly one table per archetype, so a new verb cannot ship without declaring its AI stance (NEVER is legal, absence is red).** `AIController` resets each member's `active_attack` before planning — **hygiene, not correctness, since #102**: the scoring loop no longer writes candidates into that slot at all (it passes each one straight to `Reach`/`gather_attack_victims`; the candidate builder sets it only for `declare()`'s stamp and clears it again), and the one assignment that stands is the joint pass's, set on the winner just before it is queued, mirroring a player pick. `attack_if_possible` is gone. Known v1 approximations (accepted at ratification, listed in ai-tactics.md): destination planning reads the default pick (except for a removal or a squad break since #760, and a member's damage step since #1220); counters and squad-level coordination closed with #117. Integration contract: content-shaped changes (new families/carvings/mods/readiness/#76 strain) reach the AI with zero AI-side wiring — if the AI can't see a feature, the feature bypassed the player surface; fix the feature. ## The ai/ folder: the threat field and enemy ranges

`ai/` — archetype AI (#29): `AIController`, `AIArchetype` (enum + registry), `AITactics` (shared board queries), `ThreatField`/`ThreatIntent` ([#710](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/710); the PLAN tier is `AIController.preview_turn`, which stands every unit on its `get_projected_destination()` via `MovementComponent.set_cell`, runs the REAL archetypes against that board, harvests what they intend and rolls every order back -- so the AI sees the player pending plan with no rules code edited. `is_squad_previewable` is `is_squad_actable` minus the `has_acted` clause, because that flag is about the turn that finished.

**ONE KEY SINCE [#1069](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1069), and the other channel rides the move GESTURE.** `V` toggles the enemy RANGES -- what they COULD do. What the enemy WILL do had `T` and a three-state cycle until the dev looked at 3D FE again ("they aren't conveying enemy intent at all, just who can reach who. **What one build costs ([#1344](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1344), 2026-10-21).** The Gorge's opening (19 enemies) cost 335–480 ms per `for_viewer` on a loaded machine; the issue first quoted ~235 ms. Profiled, the waste was all inside one build. A sentry's origins are 20–39 cells, not its 80–164-cell zone, because the move range clips the zone. Each origin then traced the line of sight to every cell in range, so the same cell was traced again from every origin that reached it. 73% of the opening pass's traces and 94% of the counter rim's re-checked a cell already reached. The other costs were the watch lanes (75 ms, four Marksmen), the origins themselves (42 ms of `compute_move_range`), carving fizzle checks (24 ms, re-asked by the rim) and `is_attack_fireable` asked once per origin (13 ms). The fix is exact and per build:
- a cell an attack already reaches is never traced again;
- fireable is asked once per attack;
- a fizzle verdict is asked once per attack and origin within a unit's walk;
- every trace verdict lives in `ThreatField.TraceMemo`, keyed attack → origin → cell, and is shared by every unit in the build, because `Reach.vertical_aim_ok` reads no unit.

The field came out identical cell for cell, key order included, on five shipped boards. The build cost 0.47× on The Gorge and 0.46–0.62× elsewhere. `ThreatField.traces` is the operation count the two cost cases in `tests/ai/test_threat_field.gd` assert on. **Nothing is carried between builds (dev's pick, 2026-10-21).** A cache keyed on #1360's store versions measured about 4× on a plan-only change, but those versions don't cover an attack's range or shape, or a tile's rule height, edited in the dev tools. Today the field heals at the next plan change; such a cache would keep the old answer, and that can under-warn. Watch lanes are not shared across units either: they would have saved about 10 ms, and the sharing would rest on `watch_lanes` ignoring the unit it is handed. What remains (origins, watch lanes, carving fizzle) lives in `RulesService`, `AITactics` and `CellSubstances`.
