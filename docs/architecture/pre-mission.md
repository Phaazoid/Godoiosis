## The pre-mission phase

**[#738](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/738) (2026-09-05) then made the WHOLE roster spawn**, deployed or not, into **`game.reserve_root`** (`$Reserve`, `visible = false`) — real `Unit` nodes, because every readout the pre-mission screen needs takes a wielder and `Unit._ready` guards only its grid setup. **Not under `units_root`, and inside `clear_board`'s teardown**, and both halves are load-bearing: nine readers take that node to mean *on the board*, so undeployed units there are living player units and the #96 defeat floor never fires (note it is the FLOOR, not rout — rout asks about hostile factions); while a holding parent outside the teardown survives a board swap pointing into a freed board. `deploy_unit`/`undeploy_unit` are the board-entry/exit pair, and undeploy goes through **`SquadManager.release`** — the detach WITHOUT `leave_squad`'s re-solo, the one exception to "every unit is in exactly one squad", scoped exactly to *is it on the board*. It also forced `Squad._erase_member` to clear `unit.squad`, symmetric with `_add_member`: every prior caller re-added immediately, so a unit naming a squad it had left was unobservable — and `destroy_empty_squad` then freed the object it still named, which a null check cannot see (#149).

**[#739](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/739) (2026-09-05) then made it a PHASE THE PLAYER STANDS IN**: `begin_mission` holds the board after the draw instead of playing on, and `commit_deployment` is the one exit (refused with nobody on the board). **`MissionController._deploying` is the intent behind the new `GameState.PRE_MISSION`, and it has to be a flag of its own for the reason `dev_mode_enabled` is one** -- `game._base_state()` decides what the board RESTS at and `clear_selection` WRITES `game_state` from it, so a state derived from `game_state` would answer PICKING_TARGET in the middle of a squad pick and rest the board on IDLE afterwards. Its one entry `_open_deployment` sets the flag AND rests the board (the flag alone leaves `game_state` wherever the arrival left it); its one exit clears the flag BEFORE `_begin_turn`, since `start_faction_turn` rests on `_base_state()` too.

**The HUD stand-down rides that flag's SETTER, never the commit** -- `abandon_mission` and `resume_from_slot` both leave the phase without passing through it, so a hide written at commit strands the NEXT mission with no End Turn button. The phase's ring is `MainActionMenu`'s own, early-returning into `_pre_mission_options` before any gate that reads `unit.squad` (a just-undeployed unit has none): squad verbs unchanged, plus Undeploy -- gated on `drawn_from_roster`, since authored units and enemies are `units_root` children too -- and Inspect, and no turn verb at all. An empty deployment cell opens the reserve as synthetic ring leaves, so the dropdown needed no widget.

**A SAVE is refused while deploying**, in `save_to_slot` rather than at the pause-menu row: `capture_scenario` walks `units_root`, so a slot taken here records only what is placed and the resume drops the rest of the roster with no way to finish choosing. **[#740](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/740) + [#743](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/743) (2026-09-05) gave that phase its SCREEN** -- `PreMissionScreen`, a `ModalCard` that is not a card (unframed, opaque, no `ModalLock`) holding the roster's cards, the stash, the deployed force and the contract (and since #1324 the board's conditions above the stash: its weather and the air's reading), with Tab swapping to the board underneath.

**It locks the board by joining `game.menu_is_up()` rather than by eating clicks**: one predicate covers both click doors plus the camera rig, whose WASD poll no Control can catch (#740 also claimed a click a Control swallows still reaches the 3D view; a real click measured it FALSE at #929's follow-up, see the HUD sharp edge), and it reads VISIBILITY not existence -- hiding the screen IS the board preview, which is why it is hidden rather than freed. `MissionController` owns the lifecycle and closes it at commit, at `reset()` and at **`abandon_mission`, which needs its own call** since that path never reaches `clear_board`. Two extractions came with it, each because a second reader appeared: **`MissionStatusPanel.briefing`** is now the ONE builder for the objective and FAIL-IF rows (`briefing_rows` its plain-label projection since #955 part 3) (the briefing before the battle and the status during it cannot word a condition differently), and **`UnitInstance.LIMB_SHORT`/`LIMB_FULL`** moved beside the enum they name, `info_panel` being a scene script with no `class_name`.

**The equip block reason lives on the CARD and never in the stash** (dev): `can_equip` takes a WIELDER, so the stash has nobody to validate against -- #744 widened it to a sentence, through one helper the card already routed through, and the stash shows `requirement_text` instead (what a piece DEMANDS, the one question a list of loose gear can answer). **[#741](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/741) (2026-09-05) then made the gear MOVE**, and its first finding was that there was no live stash at all: `PreMissionScreen` read `roster.stash` off whatever `RosterCatalog.resolve()` returned, which is Godot's resource CACHE, so the first item moved out would have depleted the authored `Roster` for the rest of the session (#731 ruling 3, broken by one drag).

**`Classes/flow/Loadout.gd` is the phase's live gear plus the ONE judge**: `copy_for_grant()` copies built in `deploy_roster` and dropped in `reset()` (the phase's own pair of edges -- `PreMissionPhase`, shared with the headless Play API since #46), and `move(item, from, to)` where **null is the stash at either end**, so all four directions are one function. **Clicking and dragging both ask `move_block_reason` and both act through `move()`** -- a drag that judged for itself is a second answer to "may this move". `Unit` gained `add_block_reason`/`remove_block_reason` with `add_item`'s bare false and `remove_item`'s SILENT prosthetic guard derived from them (an installed prosthetic lives IN the inventory; a mover that could not read that guard would trade away someone's arm). `add_item` is derived since [#1412](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1412) (2026-10-23); until then it ran its own free-slot loop and took a loose mod. The two seeding doors, `UnitData.starting_inventory` and `ScenarioUnitEntry.inventory`, drop an authored mod with a warning that quotes the reason. Two bugs the suite found rather than review: **a redraw run inside a row's own signal tries to free the emitting node** ("Attempted to free a locked object" -- the player's FIRST click), so `_redraw` defers, which is the rule for any surface that rebuilds itself from its own child's signal; and **`data as Dictionary` is a HARD cast that throws** rather than degrading to null, so a drag payload that is not ours took the frame with it -- type-check `_can_drop_data`'s data, never cast it.

**A THIRD was found in PLAY afterwards ([#789](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/789), 2026-09-06): acting on the mouse PRESS makes dragging structurally impossible** -- the redraw frees the drag source one idle frame later, while Godot asks `_get_drag_data` only after ~10px of travel WHILE HELD, so it never ran once in the shipped build and clicking worked throughout. A click is the RELEASE, and travelled distance separates the two at Godot's own threshold. **The suite could not have caught it and its own header said so** -- every case drives the three callbacks directly; what a headless suite CAN see is the row surviving its own pres The selection is DATA, never a row, because a successful move frees every row on screen (#107).

**[#745](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/745) (2026-09-05) then made the NUMBERS reach the decision**, and found most of the derived half already built -- `ArmorData.mechanical_text` (#44), `TransmutationData.mechanical_text` (#166) and `WeaponInstance.attack_detail` (#485) read their numbers at display time and NOTHING pinned that retuning one re-words the readout. **PREVIEW-AT-DECISION is the new mechanism**: `_gear_modifier` split into a walk taking the gear, so `Unit.previewed_stat` and the live read share ONE function, and **DEF got its own substitution because it is a SECOND walk** reading both the worn piece and the CON that piece's modifiers move (judging a candidate against the OLD plate's CON is the bug that shape prevents; the case equips it for real and compares rather than hand-computing). WEIGHT forks on provenance -- incoming adds mass, equipping in place moves nothing. It annotates the card's own stats grid IN PLACE and **rebuilds nothing**, #741's law from the other side, **pinned by NODE IDENTITY: a rebuild restoring the same values is invisible to a text compare, which a mutant proved.** **The FLAVOUR wire was broken outright** -- `make()` has never copied its template's `description` and `copy_for_grant` only copies instance-to-instance, so authoring a family's line reached nothing a player could hover; `Item.describe()` is the one door and `WeaponInstance` reads THROUGH, which covers saved scenarios with no migration. Flavour never states a NUMBER (it goes stale while the data stays right), enforced over every authored weapon file. And **Begin carries its own refusal**, because `commit_deployment`'s banner is a `TurnBanner` -- a plain child of Game, under this screen's `CanvasLayer` and therefore invisible.

**[#774](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/774) (2026-09-05) then gave the phase a BOARD-SIDE surface**, reported as *"while inspecting the board, there's no way back to the loadout menu"* -- and the mechanism was not missing, the affordance was: Tab was bound, registered in `Controls.gd` and named in the swap button's own tooltip, but #739's stand-down meant the preview came up with no chrome to say the phase was still running. `PreMissionBar` fills the slot End Turn vacates (`MissionStatusPanel` reserves it either way, so nothing reflows) with Loadout and Begin Mission. **Neither greys, which is the OPPOSITE of the screen's answer to the same act and for the same reason**: `commit_deployment`'s refusal speaks through `TurnBanner`, a plain child of Game, so under the screen's `CanvasLayer` it cannot be heard and the screen greys Begin instead -- out on the board it can, so the button stays live.

**The commit ASKS FIRST since the dev's 2026-09-05 tweak**, through `confirm_and_commit` on the CONTROLLER rather than on a button, because the commit has three doors and a confirm on one is one the other two walk around; the empty-force refusal is deliberately not asked about first, and `_deploying` is re-read after the await since F2 is exempt from the modal freeze. **A palette-aware GROUND with a theme-default INK is how a label vanishes**: Loadout took its box from `row_box()` and its font colour from the theme, so it disappeared under Parchment -- `HEADER_TEXT` is the role that INVERTS with its ground (`TITLE_TEXT` and `EXECUTE_TEXT` stay light in both by design), and all four font states need it because a Button falls back to the theme's on hover. `test_queue_palette.gd` pins the PAIRING, not the button.

**[#814](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/814) (2026-09-07) then found that rule live at TWELVE more sites and named what was actually missing: a text role is a WEIGHT and a GROUND, and under slate every ground is dark so one value answered both.** Parchment splits them, which is why callers kept guessing -- there was no role saying *body text on paper*. Four roles fill the grid (`BODY_TEXT`/`NAME_TEXT` on paper, `FRAME_TEXT`/`FRAME_REFUSED_TEXT` on the frame), each taking its SLATE value from what its own sites already rendered, so slate is unchanged BY CONSTRUCTION and only the parchment column is new authorship -- pinned by provenance cases, `BODY_TEXT`'s against `ThemeDB` rather than a retyped number.

**The law had to be a TREE WALKER (`tests/ui/test_pre_mission_contrast.gd`), because a colour-PAIR test cannot see a label with NO override -- there is no colour to look up, and that was five of the twelve.** It reads each control's effective `get_theme_color` against a ground COMPOSITED down the ancestor chain (a stylebox may be translucent), and found three sites the ticket had not enumerated, one of them on slate. **A mutant then found the door the walker could not see**: #745's hover puts the stat grid back by REMOVING the override, which is the resting colour only while the ground is dark in both palettes -- restoring it leaves all three suites green at 32/32, because a sweep over a surface's RESTING state is blind to every state it can be put into. its lifecycle joins the screen's in `_close_deployment_menu`, so reset, commit and abandon take it for free.

**The blind spot underneath was a WIRE**: every case drove `toggle_deployment_menu()` directly, so a bound-and-dead key would have passed a green suite -- `test_the_tab_key_reaches_the_swap` builds the event on `physical_keycode` the way `project.godot` binds it. **[#763](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/763) (2026-09-07) then made a RESTART keep it** -- `PreMissionSnapshot` (`RefCounted`, `BoardSnapshot`'s never-saved shape, rows of `ScenarioUnitEntry` so `capture_unit_state`/`apply_unit_state` carry gear, jobs and fitted mods with no field added to a resource 13 `.tres` embed), taken at the commit and replayed by `deploy_roster`'s SECOND ending. It is the one phase field `reset()` must not clear -- the others hold references into the board being freed, this holds data -- and its life has two edges, neither a teardown: **overwritten by the next commit anywhere** (a `mission_path` key is why no door has to remember to clear one) and **dropped by a restart taken inside the phase**, which is the player's way back to the authored draw and renames the pause row to *Reset Loadout*. That second ruling is why `restart_mission` reads `_deploying` FIRST: `reload_current` -> `clear_board` -> `reset()` clears the flag, so the same question one line lower answers no every time and the ruling silently inverts. Placement replays BEFORE anything else and **nobody standing falls through to the authored walk** -- both doors gate the phase on a non-zero draw (and, since #46, on the board's authored *Pre-mission screen* box, `ScenarioData.offers_pre_mission`, through the one rule `PreMissionPhase.opens`), so returning 0 opens a battle with the whole roster in reserve.

**[#812](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/812) (2026-09-07) then gave the ROSTER an editor** -- `Project > Rosters`, the tab #735 deferred (*"hand-authored in the inspector until #731's deferred dev tab"*) and `RosterLint`'s first caller outside CI. FOUR COLUMNS (three until #964), each the WHOLE catalogue with the chosen floated above a rule, so ticking an unchosen row IS the add and there is no picker, no Add button and no second pane (dev: *"I kind of want to see both what's available and what's already there at the same time"*). **Entries are DIRECT REFS** -- a copy serializes INLINE as a sub_resource instead of an ext_resource -- and the tool only ever authors a REFERENCE entry.

**The stash COUNTS where the others tick**, being a multiset a tick would silently halve on the next Update. **EMPTY MEANS NONE with a stored `offers_every_*` flag for everything**, three states rather than the `ai_factions` ambiguity, resolved in ONE accessor per list (`offered_entries`/`offered_stash`/`offered_mods`/`offered_jobs`) so no reader asks about a flag -- and `offered_entries()` SYNTHESIZES under its flag, which makes resolving it ONCE a correctness rule, since `deploy_roster`'s second walk keys `unit_of_entry` BY THE ENTRY. The mod pool rides **`Loadout`**, the one phase object outliving the draw, and `offerable_for` takes it as a parameter.

**[#964](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/964) (2026-09-17) added the FOURTH list, the jobs the pre-mission picker offers** -- filed as *hide the picker for the demo* (two of the four jobs are unfinished content) and answered by authoring the gate instead (dev: *"we don't have to hide anything… we just only populate what jobs we want seen"*), which costs no demo-only flag anybody has to remember to remove. **IDS, not `JobData` refs** -- `UnitData.starting_jobs`' precedent, the key `UnitInstance.jobs` persists, and it keeps a roster out of the #596 dangling-`ext_resource` blast radius; the price is a silent typo, which is `RosterLint`'s one roster-level finding and the ONLY surface that can show one, since the tool draws a row per CATALOGUED job and an unmatched id draws nothing at all. The picker unions the offer (`Loadout.offered_jobs_for` since #46, which the headless Play API's `job` verb refuses against too) with **whatever the unit already holds** (a `starting_jobs` character or a `state_saved` entry can arrive holding a job the mission does not offer, and it must stay re-pickable) and **builds no picker at all** when that set is empty.

**`offers_every_job` defaults TRUE against its three siblings' false**, and the asymmetry is forced: their lists were authored BEFORE the flags existed so false preserved what the files said, while no roster names a job -- false would have been the plumbing diff itself switching every picker off. Empty means none at every layer here, and since [#1222](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1222) on the mod path too: `offerable_for` used to read an empty pool as EVERY mod, so every shipped mission offered every mod on disk; the pool is required now and `WeaponModCatalog.all_mods()` is the one spelling of *every mod*. `ItemCatalog` (the union of the item folders, promoted out of `UnitEditorTool`: seven of them since conduits joined at #1312, #835 having put the two weapon TEMPLATE folders in it) and `ResourceCatalog.by_file` are the two seams it needed; scarcity is [#828](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/828). Since [#1414](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1414) both editors' item pickers read `ItemCatalog.by_name()` (they had kept a walk of their own), and all three projections refuse what a kind's own catalog refuses, so `offers_every_item` no longer offers an illegal rune.
