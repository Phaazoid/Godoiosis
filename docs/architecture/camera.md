## The camera's vocabulary has one home

**THE CAMERA'S VOCABULARY HAS ONE HOME: [`docs/design/camera-language.md`](docs/design/camera-language.md) ([#671](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/671), 2026-09-02) — read it before naming anything camera-shaped.** It NAMES and POINTS, never re-tells: the channels and the door(s) that write each (`_apply_position`'s sum is the #602 diagnosis in one line), `When { LIVE, SETTLED }`, the shot words as they exist TODAY (ease / cut / snap / hold, and the six implicit shots), and **the FIVE DOORS that may move the camera** — `_mirror_camera` (the per-frame `cam.*` poll), `_on_impact`, `_center_rig_on`, `fit_camera`, and the player's own hand — which is the table [#672](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/672) formalizes, so a sixth door is a decision to state out loud. Two traps it exists to stop you re-deriving: `Camera3D.is_position_in_frustum()` already answers on-camera for the LIVE pose (only SETTLED was ever missing), and `widen_to_fit`/`_fit_distance` RE-AIMS and is a different question from a frame edge. Also flags that **`flourish` means two unrelated things** — the camera addend and `class_name Flourish` in alchemy. `visual-clarity.md` keeps the round-by-round history.

## CameraTrace: what the camera just did

**A report also carries WHAT THE CAMERA JUST DID ([#669](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/669), 2026-09-02) — a `## Camera trace` section, on a SECOND pushed Callable beside `view_source`.** Two questions, two answers: the View line (which grew the live HOLDS — playback lock, death show, followed unit — and the frame floor as a live/settled PAIR) says what the camera is doing; the trace says what it just did, which a final-frame reading structurally cannot. **`CameraTrace` (`presentation/`) records rig-side, and the HEARTBEAT rather than the rig's mutator doors is the load-bearing choice** — `hold_at`/`drop_to`/`lift_to` are re-driven every frame under playback, so door hooks write ~60 rows a second and a 120-entry ring covers two seconds, evicting the edges it exists to show; and `stash_view`/`restore_view`/`drop_stashed_view` write `_target_drop` and `_target_yaw_degrees` by DIRECT assignment past every door, with `_process` clamping `_target_aim` besides. Reading the channels where they ENDED UP, last in `_process`, change-gated AND floored at 200 ms, catches both. Discrete doors still `note_event` themselves (that is what names a CAUSE), each guarded so a no-op cannot write a row saying `11.0 -> 11.0` where a cause should be. It is **pure on time** — every verb takes `now_msec`, `recovered()`'s idiom — so the rate limit is asserted rather than slept through. The View line prints the frame floor as a live/settled PAIR; the two functions behind it were collapsed at [#670](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/670) into **`CameraRig3D.frame_floor(when)`**, `When { LIVE, SETTLED }` choosing the terms.

**Two corrections from that ticket are worth knowing before building any frame query:** `Camera3D.is_position_in_frustum()` already answers "is X on camera" exactly for the LIVE pose (so only the SETTLED half was ever missing), and `_fit_distance`/`widen_to_fit` is a DIFFERENT question that re-aims (how far back to contain a box), not a frame-edge read.

## ShotDirector: the camera's shots are a priority table

**THE CAMERA'S SHOTS ARE A PRIORITY TABLE ([#672](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/672), 2026-09-02) — `Classes/presentation/ShotDirector.gd`, and `battle3d._apply_shot` is the ONE place a distance or a framing is written under playback.** `enum Shot { NONE, WIDE, SPAN, STAGE, TRAINED, DEATH_SHOW }` ascending, and **the enum's ORDER is the mechanism** — `solve()` is a rank compare over `liveness()`, one clause per row, so moving a row changes the answer, where an if-cascade would read the same and mean nothing (a mutant swapping two rows PASSES under a cascade; that is how this was found). It replaced five edge-driven writers of the distance channel whose precedence was TEMPORAL, and five `battle3d` latches with it (`_playback_owned_camera`, `_framed_span`, `_stage_cells_solved`, `_trained_seen_id`, `_release_deferred`). Four things to know before touching it.

**The playback lock is the table's GATE, not a row** — a `LOCKED` row would be outranked from above and a death show still playing as the lock let go would hold the camera past the pass's end. **What `DEATH_SHOW` outranks is the FALLBACK, not the close-up** — its condition ("show live AND nobody followed") and TRAINED's are mutually exclusive so those two never compete; it stands in TRAINED's place once the body is gone, and outranking STAGE/SPAN/WIDE is what makes round 8's release a DEFERRAL rather than a skip, now derived instead of maintained. **The apply is EDGE-driven** — `update()` reports a change on a changed CAUSE, not just a changed shot (a stage re-published over different cells, a new trained subject), and reports false otherwise because `widen_to_fit` solves against the live basis and the stage's height LATCH stops a thrown body walking the shot down. And **a release falls to the next LIVE shot** (dev ruling, 2026-09-02): a staged fight used to read `stage fit -> 7 -> 11 -> 7 -> 11` because the release hardcoded `playback_distance`, so the stage's framing survived until the first beat and never came back; it now holds the stage between beats.

**Testing a pass headlessly: you cannot.** `Pacing` collapses every beat to zero, so the move phase returns before its own `process_frame` await and a real queued move records ZERO trace rows — and even with frames, `pan_to`'s null-follow window never contains one, since headless `pan_to_position` does not suspend. Script the causes and drive `_mirror_camera()` by hand, as `tests/presentation/test_camera_follow.gd`'s shot transcript does. Vocabulary in `camera-language.md`, history in `visual-clarity.md`. **What stands in a shot's way is `ShotClearance`'s since [#1132](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1132)** — on a battle-zoom beat the camera turns to a clear side first and HIDES only what no angle clears (a column into `BoardMirror.hidden_board`, a prop or unit made invisible, through the two mirrors' own gates); `camera-language.md` → *Clearance words*.

**On the battle zoom the APPROACH is the shot** (same day, from his play-check): `pan_to` publishes `pan_subject`, which `battle3d._shot_subject` lets stand in for the follow so the close-up and the clearance's angle start on the pan's first frame, judged from where it lands — so between battle-zoom beats there is no stage gap any more — and the beat waits on `CameraController.view_arriving` (`CameraRig3D.is_arriving`, the second rig → playback fact) before its hold. **Round 3 made the camera STILL while anything plays** (dev: *"if the camera is still moving to a new position, nothing should be playing"*): the angle turns ONLY while a playback pan travels (`can_turn = cam.is_panning()`) and afterwards the clearance only hides, which repeals #972's spin; and every playback pan, in both profiles, is followed by `OrderExecutor._settle_then` (arrival, then at least `Pacing.CAMERA_SETTLE`, a knob). That wait is headless-invisible, so its evidence is the probe table in `visual-clarity.md`.

**Round 4 made it SEE units** (dev: *"battles hiding behind units"*): a unit blocks as its art AND its health readout (`ShotClearance.Body.blocks`, sized by `UnitHealthBar.half_extents`), because from the zoom's pitch the readout over a head is the occluder; on a close-up the action is the aim line rather than everyone on stage (whose ground stays protected); a close-up watches both ends of its line; and on a walk every walker is the action. A probe that measures a feature must show it ACTING, not only behaving: round 3's logged `hidden 0/0/0` for a whole pass and was read as success. **The dev can PAUSE a pass and take the camera since [#705](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/705)**: P (a dev key) is one more reason in `Pacing._apply_time_scale`, never a direct write; under it the rig eases on a capped wall clock, manual input opens, the director's per-frame writes stand down, and resume CUTS back to the frame `CameraRig3D.snapshot_view` held, separate from `stash_view` (the player's view). `PlaybackControl` releases the pause whenever playback lets go of the board, so nothing strands the game at time scale 0; `camera-language.md` → *the doors*.

**While paused, N records a KEY POSE** (Shift+N clears, a board swap clears): `CameraRecording` keeps the framing beside the director's held frame, the pass time, the shot and the aim line, and the bug report carries it as a table, the same numbers as JSON to replay, and `camera.png`. A recording is in-memory data rendered into the report, never a saved resource (`CameraPose` has no pitch, and a saved type would drag in the embedded-content sweep). **Session → Camera in the dev window shows it all live** (`CameraTool`, a projection with no store): the table's lit rows from `ShotDirector.liveness`, which `solve()` ranks over, so the page cannot disagree with the camera; the View line and trace through the sources BugReporter reads; and the key poses with Jump to (paused only), Delete and Clear all.
