## The persistence seam

**Persistence seam (locked, #8):** transient `Unit` node holds battle-scoped state that resets each mission (element states, projected position, lifecycle ACTIVE/DOWNED/DEAD); persistent `UnitInstance` resource holds identity that survives missions (HP, fixed stats, limb loss, proficiency, and `permadead`; Will lived here until [#1174](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1174) retired it, and its replacement, `Unit.wounded`, is battle-scoped). **`permadead` (added 2026-07-27) is declared but deliberately UNWIRED** — nothing sets or reads it until there are missions and a persistent roster, at which point `Unit.die()` is the setter and roster assembly the reader. It is the *third* distinct "dead" on this seam and the only non-battle-scoped one: `UnitInstance.is_dead()` is `current_hp <= 0`, `Unit.is_dead()` is "went down the permanent path this mission", `permadead` is "never fields again". Don't add a fourth, and don't rename any of them to plain `dead`. New per-unit state picks its side by one test — *does it survive a mission?* No third store.

**The seam now has NO exceptions** — the one that used to be documented here, `UnitInstance.stat_modifiers`, was **deleted by #112 (2026-07-28)**. It was a battle-scoped add/subtract bag (the Crisis surge) parked on the persistent instance purely because it was a stage of `get_effective_stat`'s chain, and it had already gone permanent once by having its reset missed. Temporary stats are now `StatEffect`s on the transient `Unit`, which is where the seam's own test ("does it survive a mission?") always said they belonged. **The effective-stat chain is base → limb → jobs → temporary effects → gear**, and only its first three stages live on `UnitInstance`: `UnitState.get_body_stat()` adds effects, `UnitState.get_effective_stat()` adds gear (both forwarded by `Unit`).

**Because the chain's top stages live on the transient side, every DERIVATION off a stat must live there too (on `UnitState`), or take the finished value as a parameter** (#106: `UnitInstance.get_max_hp(effective_con)` and `get_mov(effective_dex, weight)` are passed it; `get_effective_ldr`/`get_effective_def` sit on `UnitState`; `UnitState.set_current_hp(value)` is the one door for writing HP, since the clamp needs a ceiling `UnitInstance` can't derive). **Everything that follows a stat change runs in `Unit._settle_stat_change`** — `state.settle()` enforces wear gates (stripping gear you no longer qualify for) → re-clamps HP, then `Unit` emits `stats_changed` — in that order, because stripping armour moves CON which moves max HP. Wear gates read `get_body_stat`, i.e. **never gear** — and the gate step's second clause since #157 (2026-08-10), the equipped rune's channel gate (`EquippableData.can_equip`, aura+affinity), also never reads gear — which is what keeps the strip to a single pass with no cascade.

**The gear stage grew a SECOND source in [#74](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/74) (2026-08-23)** — a fitted `WeaponModData`'s `stat_modifiers`, summed over `UnitState._mod_sources()` alongside `worn_armor` — so the "no gate reads gear" property is now what keeps *two* clauses non-cascading rather than one, and is pinned rather than merely written down (`tests/weapons/test_weapon_mod_grants.gd`). `stats_changed` is for READOUTS only; "may this be queued?" is a question about the *projected* stat and belongs to `SquadPlanValidator` ([#113](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/113)). Doctrine + the save/load bug #106 caused: [`docs/design/stats.md`](../design/stats.md) → *One chain, one layer* and *Temporary stats*. Pinned by `tests/stats/test_stat_effects.gd` + `test_derived_stat_chain.gd`. (Cross-mission save/load is the future campaign layer; boundary is locked now so features land on the right side. See `docs/design/resolution-pipeline.md` → "The persistence seam".)

**The transient side is an object of its own since [#1480](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1480) stage 1 (2026-10-24).** The battle-scoped fields (`inventory`, `equipped_weapon`, `worn_armor`, `attunement`, `element_states`, `stat_effects`, the lifecycle and Crisis flags, the downed clock) and the persistent `instance` moved off the `Unit` node into `UnitState`, a RefCounted the node owns as `state`, together with the rules that read them: the stat chain and the gear walk, HP, MOV, weight, LDR, DEF, live abilities and immunity, aura and affinity, the previews, and the gear-gate judgement and strip. `Unit` keeps forwarding properties and methods, so its several hundred readers did not change, and every mutation door with a side effect (a signal, a sprite, a squad) stayed on `Unit`. `UnitState.settle()` is the gates then the HP re-clamp and never emits. `state.unit` is a back-reference for identity only. **Since stage 2 (2026-10-24) content asks a `UnitState`, never a `Unit`:** every wielder parameter in `items/`, `weapons/` and `alchemy/` (78 methods) takes the state, `UnitState`'s own rules pass `self`, and callers pass `unit.state`, or `UnitState.of(unit)` where nobody may hold the piece (a stash weapon on the fitting card). Two content reads were not body facts. A rune's counter needs the live PICK, which stays on `Unit`, so `counter_attack(wielder, live_pick)` takes it as a parameter (`Unit.get_counter_attack` passes `active_attack`; a weapon ignores it). A vial's refusal names its holder, which is identity, so it reads `user.unit.get_unit_name()`. `Reach` took a `Unit` it never read until stage 4 removed it: its queries take only the geometry they reason about. It is not a third store, it IS the transient side, and the reason to make it an object is stage 3 (2026-10-24): a resolve pass threads a planned COPY of it (`UnitState.copy()`, made on a target's first write and read through `PlanResolver.state_of(unit, hypo)`) instead of `PlanResolver._Hypo`'s field-by-field mirror, so every read of a hit TARGET sees what the pass already did to it. The copy twins every carried weapon and shares the rest of the gear; `tests/law/test_unit_state_copy.gd` refuses a field nobody has classified. **Since stage 4 (2026-10-25) the ACTOR reads its copy too** (its damage, kind, elements, shove, counter, fireability and a carving's fizzle; the attack-source rules moved onto `UnitState` for it, with `Unit` forwarding), the supercharge spend draws on the copied weapon, and an alternate-source preview makes the copy hold the source (`PlanResolver.hold_in_hand` through `UnitState.equip`, the one equip gate). **The seal:** `Unit.state` is a getter that counts a read on `Unit.seal_breaks` (and logs it with its backtrace) while `SquadManager._resolve_actions` runs, and `PlanResolver._live_state` reads past it as the one declared door. It never changes an answer. `tests/law/test_a_pass_reads_no_live_body.gd` and `test_a_pass_writes_no_live_body.gd` walk every saved board's real passes and refuse a live read or write; the second fingerprints a body through the copy law's own tables (`tests/support/unit_state_fields.gd`). The forwarding itself is pinned by `tests/unit/test_unit_state.gd`.

**Scenario save/load round-trips the whole `UnitInstance` side since #83 (2026-07-22):** `ScenarioUnitEntry.capture_unit_state`/`apply_unit_state` own the unit-state snapshot (stats, current HP, full inventory via `copy_for_grant` + `equipped_index` — the old standalone `equipped_weapon` copy is retired — `worn_armor_index` (#89 — armor is carried in the SAME inventory as weapons but fills a DIFFERENT slot, so it saves as an index exactly like `equipped_index`, never as a second copy beside the carried one; **load assigns directly and never re-runs the wear gate** — a save is authoritative, and re-gating would strip armor off a unit whose stats drifted after saving), limb states with prosthetic re-link-**by carried instance**, not by template — two prosthetics built on one shared family template are different items and matching by template links both slots to whichever copy is found first (the TorvArm/TorvLeg bug, latent since #83 and unreachable until `install_prosthetic` got its first production caller -- and SHIPPED STATE since [#835](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/835), 2026-09-08, which retired the hand-saved `TorvArm.tres`: Torv's arm is now the DERIVED generic and his leg the authored `TorvLeg.tres`, two instances on one shared Prosthetic template, so the rule guards a real pairing rather than a hypothetical one) — proficiency, aura, **affinity** (`affinity_saved` is a DECLARED sentinel: an empty affinity is a legal saved value, the Rebecca rule, so emptiness cannot double as "unsaved" the way every other default in that block does; without it, aura was restored while affinity was not, leaving a loaded alchemist with a full pool, an empty affinity, and every carving silently unfireable), jobs), applied AFTER `initialize()`'s reset on load; `ScenarioManager` and the Play API's `board_builder` both call the same pair, and since #46 the rest of a load through shared doors too: `ScenarioUnitEntry.apply_placement` (the escort flag and a leader's squad name, archetype, zone and post, outside #177's fork below), `ScenarioManager.valid_entries` and the spawn gate with `ScenarioUnitEntry.spawns_as_body()`, and `ScenarioManager.relink_guards` for armed Guards.

**The entry has TWO declared kinds since [#177](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/177) (2026-08-10), forked per unit by `ScenarioUnitEntry.state_saved`** (default `true` = the snapshot every pre-#177 save is; the `affinity_saved` pattern widened to the whole block): an **authored** save (`capture_scenario(name, authored = true)`, the Scenario tab checkbox) writes a **cast** unit — spawned from a standalone character file under `Resources/Units/`, provenance carried by `Unit.unit_data_source`, which `UnitFactory` stamps only for a `::`-free real `resource_path` — as a **reference** (`entry.unit_data` = the file itself, `state_saved = false`, nothing captured, and the loader must NOT call `apply_unit_state`; both loaders gate on it). Every load re-reads the character as authored then — *references for the cast; snapshots return with mission-to-mission carryover* (dev, 2026-08-10).

**A DEV EDIT breaks the reference (dev-ratified 2026-08-20):** transient `Unit.dev_edited` (set by the Unit Editor's Save, and by a cast spawn placed on a non-file faction) makes the authored capture snapshot that unit instead — a re-reference would silently discard the edit, which is how inventory/team edits to the verticality fixture could never be saved; sticky across loads, since the snapshot entry respawns without `unit_data_source`. `BugReporter` and every other caller keep the default full snapshot. `UnitData` grew the **starting-kit block** (`starting_jobs`/`starting_inventory`/equip+wear picks/`starting_proficiency`/`starting_prosthetics`), seeded by `Unit._seed_starting_kit()` right after `initialize()` through the gated equip doors (refusals `push_warning` — bad authoring surfaces at spawn); `apply_unit_state` **clears the seeded kit before restoring** so a snapshot stays authoritative instead of doubling inventory. The loader passes `entry.unit_data` to `spawn_unit` UN-duplicated (the factory copies; the old outer `duplicate(true)` severed provenance so a load-then-resave silently embedded). Pinned by `tests/flow/test_cast_references.gd` + `tests/unit/test_starting_kit.gd`, falsified against all three of those bugs.

## A save is a mid-battle snapshot

**A save is a true MID-BATTLE SNAPSHOT since [#87](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/87) (2026-07-30) — this REVERSES, for the save-resume axis only, the "battle-scoped `Unit` state deliberately stays out" line that used to end the paragraph above.** That line was right for a *mission boundary* and wrong for *pause/resume*: a different axis the seam had never separated. Weapon-state-only was explicitly rejected as a lopsided snapshot (mace charge kept, but downed units standing up and fires going out). Now round-tripping alongside the instance side: per-carried-weapon battle state (spring readiness, carbine magazine, mace charge, chainsword rev — **keyed by inventory index**, because a unit can carry two of a family and they must track independently, the same identity rule the prosthetic re-link learned), `element_states`, `lifecycle_state` + the downed clock, the two Crisis flags (`in_crisis` + `crisis_surge_pending` — `crisis_offered_pending` died with the offer, #158), live `StatEffect`s **with their remaining countdowns**, `Squad.has_acted` (saved on the leader's `ScenarioUnitEntry`, applied after the squad rebuild), and the mission's captured zones, `contested` latch and `rounds_elapsed` clock (#101; `MissionState` since #46) (on `ScenarioData` — `outcome` is deliberately NOT saved, since it is derived from those plus the board and the next `check()` re-reaches it).

**The seam is unweakened, and that is the whole mechanism:** nothing became `@export` where it lives — weapon state is still plain vars on `WeaponInstance` subclasses so `make()`/`copy_for_grant()` re-arm every weapon, and lifecycle/states/Crisis are still plain vars on the transient `Unit`. The snapshot captures them EXPLICITLY, exactly the way limb fittings have always been captured rather than serialized, so a mission boundary still resets for free. `WeaponInstance.capture_battle_state()`/`apply_battle_state()` are the family-agnostic seam (base returns `{}`); a **Dictionary** rather than one int even though every family tracks exactly one number today, because #108's lesson is that a seam which can't say what content needs grows a second one. Two structural rules, each pinned by a test falsified against its own bug: **(1) a restore replays the RESULT, never the EVENT** — `Unit.restore_lifecycle`/`restore_stat_effects` exist because `_go_downed()` would emit `went_downed` (ejecting the unit from a squad the loader hasn't rebuilt), and `apply_stat_effect()` would reseed a countdown from its `duration`; **(2) effects settle BEFORE gear and BEFORE HP** in `apply_unit_state` — settling enforces the wear gates, so a restored debuff applied after the armour strips gear the save says is worn, and a `+CON` effect moves the max HP the saved `current_hp` was measured against. `ScenarioManager.capture_scenario`/`apply_scenario` were split out of `save_scenario`/`load_scenario` (the shape `play/board_builder.gd` already had) so a whole board round-trips in memory — writing a test `.tres` into `Scenarios/` would be picked up by Mission Select and by #9's folder scan.

**The mission-start RESET half is REAL AND EXERCISED as of [#737](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/737)** (2026-09-04, replacing this line's "the path that doesn't exist yet"): `MissionController.deploy_roster` draws a mission's Roster onto its DEPLOYMENT zone at the two FRESH-START doors — `begin_mission` and `restart_mission`, **before** the director arms (a solo squad per spawn emits `squad_created`, which an armed director answers by firing `SQUAD_FORMED` beats) — and deliberately NOT inside `apply_scenario`, since a save slot carries `roster` and would re-draw on `resume_from_slot` on top of the units it just restored. Units arriving off a roster carry no battle state by construction, which is the reset this line always described.

**`Unit.drawn_from_roster` is the one flag it added**, read only by `capture_scenario`'s `authored` branch: an authored save SKIPS the drawn units, or an Update after a play session bakes them into `unit_entries` and the next boot draws a second force on top.

## Carry-over between missions, and player save slots

What is still missing is carry-over BETWEEN missions, which is [#70](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/70)'s debt. Still deliberately out: the queued action plan (player intent, re-authorable, and serializing `BaseAction` graphs with live `Unit` refs is its own feature), `active_attack` (an aiming cursor), and projected-knockback cells (re-derived each resolve pass). **The snapshot has PLAYER doors since [#144](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/144) (2026-08-11)**: three `SaveGame` slots under `user://saves/` (never `Scenarios/` — folder scan + #9 + read-only-when-packed), Save/Load on the pause menu, Load Game on the title, `MissionController.resume_from_slot` aiming `last_loaded_path` at the ORIGIN mission so Restart/F2 reload the mission not the slot. The rule it forced: **`reset_faction_actions` fires from the turn HANDOFF (`game._on_turn_started`), never from `start_faction_turn`** — a menu arrival trusts the file, or every resume hands acted squads their actions back.

**Since [#709](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/709) (2026-09-04) that same call also SHEDS every other faction's queued orders** (`SquadManager.shed_orders`, through the signalling door so the arrow and ghost go with them): an order nobody executed used to survive the enemy turn, where the resolver placed the unit at its queued destination while the whiff gate placed it where it stands. Canon: `docs/design/missions.md` → *Player save slots*.

## The units/ folder

`units/` — the `Unit` node + its parts: `UnitData`/`UnitInstance`/`UnitFactory`/`UnitVisuals` (**`UnitFactory.create_unit` deep-copies the `UnitData` it is handed, since 2026-07-27** — `faction` is the one field on it that mutates at runtime, so two units sharing one resource would flip factions together; the copy is cheap because Godot skips sub-resources that have a `resource_path`, leaving sprite textures shared. Consequence: editing a `UnitData` `.tres` no longer live-updates already-spawned units — **still true, but it has a DOOR since [#589](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/589) (2026-08-27): `Unit.reseed_kit()`**, the dev re-grant, which reads `unit_data_source` (the FILE) precisely *because* `unit_data` is this copy. `_seed_starting_kit(source)` takes its source as a parameter for that reason — a spawn seeds from the copy, a re-seed from the file, one rule and two callers. If faction ever needs to vary per battle, the right fix is instead to move `faction` onto `Unit` as battle-scoped state + give `ScenarioUnitEntry` its own field — see the note at the call site), `MovementComponent` (the one real component left — it owns `cell`, the authoritative board position read ~148 times, plus the walk animation; `set_cell` teleports, `move_along_path` animates).

**"Where will this unit END UP?" is one derivation since #105: `Unit.projected_cell(unit, actions, require_valid, use_published)`,** with its inverse `Unit.projected_unit_at(units, cell)`. It had grown three independent answers that disagreed on two axes, so those axes are now arguments each caller states out loud: `Unit.get_projected_destination()` passes `(true, true)` — the live reading; `SquadPlanValidator.projected_cell_for()` passes `(false, false)` and **both falses are load-bearing** (it runs inside the fixed-point loop that COMPUTES `is_valid`, and a shove is derived by a resolve pass that reads validity, so either would close a loop). The second axis was `use_knockback` until #116 gave the resolve a SECOND thing to publish — a rescue HAUL — and it governs both: the axis is *may I read a displacement a resolve pass published*, and its name was one displacement narrower than its reason. The inverse takes the unit set for the same reason the forward one takes the action list — `BoardContext` scans its own `units`, `SquadManager` scans squad membership. A hold-position move deliberately loses to a shove (it means "not moving under my own power"); a real move wins only over a STALE shove, one published before the walk was queued. Inside one pass a shove always lands after the walk, so since [#1349](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1349) the resolve stamps a walk it displaced (`MoveAction.resolved_displaced`) and that shove wins. **Nothing inside a pass reads this projection**: a resolve-time reader asks `PlanResolver.projected_position(unit, hypo)`, which also holds the counter and tail shoves the projection never publishes. The projection stands for the attack prefix, the moment a newly queued order lands at.

**The inverse is also the POINTER answer (#107):** `game.unit_at_pointer(cell)` — what a click selects, what the info card shows, what `hovered_unit_changed` names, what the dev editor opens — is a one-line delegation to it, because the board draws exactly one sprite per unit *at its projected cell* (`redraw_projected_units` and `show_knockback_preview` both hide the real sprite when they put a ghost elsewhere). That the pointer's answer and the plan's answer are identical is **Law #2 working, not a coincidence** — if they diverged the board would be previewing something the plan does not do, so do not "fix" one side alone. Pinned by `tests/squad/test_projected_position.gd`.
