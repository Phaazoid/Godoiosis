## Actions are a small in-code registry

**Actions are a small in-code registry, not a framework** (`BaseAction` → `MoveAction`, `AttackAction` (→ `CounterAttackAction`), `RescueAction` (#33), `ReloadAction`/`RevAction`/`BurrowAction` (the weapon-signature verbs, #73/#84), `GuardAction` (#414 — the bodyguard verb; substitution itself is entirely PlanResolver's, this order only arms the ward), `OverwatchAction` (#413 — the standing-watch verb; the trigger and the shot are entirely PlanResolver's, this order only arms the watch), `CaptureAction` (#96 — stamps its cell, zone name and the `MissionState` at queue time, so the resolver reads a frozen snapshot and a re-planned move can't quietly capture elsewhere; the state both hosts own since #46, so the game's reactions to a claim -- the telemetry row, the zone redraw, the HUD -- hang off `MissionState.zone_captured` rather than riding the action); action-registry refactor 2026-07-20/21): the `ActionType` enum plus two declared `const` lists on `BaseAction` ARE the registry. `MAIN_ACTION_TYPES` (ATTACK/RESCUE/RELOAD/REV/BURROW/CAPTURE/**GUARD**/**OVERWATCH**) drives `is_main_action()` — mutually exclusive, one per unit per turn, must come *after* any move (locking one removes the move option). `SIDE_CHANNEL_ORDER` (RESCUE/RELOAD/REV/BURROW/CAPTURE/**OVERWATCH**/**GUARD** — the two standing reactions are LAST on purpose and in that order: a Guard or a watch armed this pass must arm after every hit it was resolved against has played back — stored orders that bypass `PlanResolver`) is iterated by THREE places at once: `OrderExecutor.execute_orders`'s phase loop, `ActionQueueDisplayEntry.build_for`'s queue-panel sections (header = the enum's own key string), and the headless Play API's `_describe_plan`/`execute()` — a new type registered there gets a phase, a queue section, and headless execution and preview for free, with no per-type mirror to add.

**ORDERING it headlessly is not free**: that is a `PlaySession` verb plus a bridge arm, and the parity ledger (#1236, below) is what refuses to let it go undeclared. Per-action **queueing requirements** live on the action itself via the `actor_block_reason()` virtual (move-before-main on `MoveAction`, verb locks on `AttackAction`/`RescueAction`) — `SquadManager.try_queue_action` asks it once as the sole Law #3 chokepoint instead of a per-type match arm, and **answers WHY it refused since [#662](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/662)**: `queue_action` and `actor_can_perform()` are both DERIVED from the reasons, and no action overrides the bool (a law case refuses one that does). The turn-flow rules — whose turn, has the squad acted, is another squad mid-plan — stay the CALLERS', because the AI's preview queues enemy orders on the player's turn (`squad-system.md` → *A refusal says WHY*). `game.gd`'s `GameState.PICKING_TARGET` is the one generic "pick one of these highlighted CELLS" flow.

**`enter_cell_pick_mode(cells, on_pick, mark, flash)` is the mode and `enter_target_pick_mode(candidates, on_pick)` is a declared SPECIALIZATION over it (#116)** — the click guard was always cell-shaped underneath (`target_pick_cells.has(cell)`), and what makes the unit flavour a flavour is the two lines in the wrapper: map the clicked cell back through `unit_at_pointer`, and refuse when nothing is there. Keeping that refusal in the wrapper is what lets a CELL pick accept an EMPTY cell, which a rescue haul's bank is by definition. Rescue, Guard, squad-up and join-squad ride the unit form (candidates from a predicate query, `on_pick` closes over the acting unit); the rescue TILE pick is the cell form's first caller and the only one that passes `flash`.

**A pick may CHAIN another (rescue: body, then the tile to haul it onto), and `_pick_generation` is what makes that survivable** — `_click_picking_target` used to call `exit_current_mode()` unconditionally after its callback, which would tear down a chained pick the instant it opened, with both halves correct in isolation (#103's shape). A `game_state` compare cannot see it: a chained pick leaves the state identical. **Both halves of it read PROJECTED cells (#126 follow-up, 2026-08-08)**: `_unit_cells` marks the overlay with `get_projected_destination()`, and `_click_picking_target` resolves with `unit_at_pointer`, not the raw live `get_unit_at_cell` — it was the last click handler that never got the #107 treatment. Until then a rescue aimed at a shoved body's landing cell **hit nothing at all**: the overlay marked the cell the body was leaving, so the click failed `target_pick_cells.has(cell)` and fell through to `exit_current_mode()` silently, while clicking the vacated cell reached `queue_rescue` and was refused by the (correctly projected) validator, also silently. The two no-plan callers are unaffected either way — `can_squad_up`/`can_join_squad` require an empty queue on both sides, so projected == live for them. *(Corrected 2026-10-20: `squad_up_block_reason` has that clause; `join_squad_block_reason` does not, and the menu's outer gate supplies it for Join.)*

**They also require both sides to be STANDING ([#1004](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1004), 2026-09-17)** -- the shared formation gate (`_formation_basics_ok` then; `SquadManager.formation_block_reason` since #46 made it a REASON, so the Play API's `join` refuses in the game's words), the one gate every formation verb and both directions route through, asked nothing about lifecycle, so a downed body was a legal Squad Up recruit: ejection hands it a FRESH solo squad, and `SquadManager.settle_downed` marks that squad spent only when the unit is standing again, leaving a body order-free and un-acted, which every other clause reads as available.

**The generalizable half: fixing "where is this unit?" at the rule layer is not finished until the PICK layer agrees** — #126 fixed the candidate query and the validator, both suites went green, and the feature did not work, because no test fired a menu pick. `tests/ui/test_target_pick_projection.gd` drives populate → press → click → assert-on-the-queue for exactly that reason; it deliberately does NOT extend to `CHOOSING_MOVE`/`CHOOSING_GROUP_MOVE` (live path-preview IS the feature) or `ATTACK_TARGETING` (directional aiming, not a unit pick) — those stay bespoke on purpose. **Menu system lives in `MainActionMenu` (`Classes/ui/MainActionMenu.gd`), pulled out of `game.gd` 2026-07-22 and completed 2026-07-26** — it holds a `game` back-ref (same pattern as `DevController`/`AIController`: instantiated and `.game = self`'d in `game._ready()`) for everything it reads or dispatches into. It now owns menu **construction** too, so `game.gd` holds no menu knowledge at all: `show_main_menu` is the one door and `build_tree(unit)` is what it hands the widget -- **the WHOLE ring tree, built once at open (#467)**, because a hovered category is ghosted before it is opened and a preview that re-queried could disagree with what commits.

**Rows are built by one builder as of #166 (2026-08-10), `_entry(name, blocked_reason, detail)` — a menu can only grey what it can explain, so the REASON a row is dead belongs to the data layer, never here**: `EquippableData.attack_block_reason(wielder, attack)` is answered per kind (a weapon reports readiness in its family's own `status_text()` words, a rune reports aura via `TransmutationData.channel_block_reason`), and `Unit.is_attack_fireable` is **derived** from it so a greyed row and a refused order can never disagree. The menu's one hardcoded reason string is gone. **An attack row also carries its weapon's LIVE COUNT since [#1045](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1045)** — `EquippableData.attack_gauge(wielder, attack)` answers a `WeaponGauge` (a stock like `4/6`, or a rev TIMER the ring tints), `_with_gauge` attaches it BESIDE `name`, and the ring draws a label only through `ActionMenuController.slice_label_parts`; `status_text()` words its numbers from the same `gauge()`, so the two cannot disagree. Which options are listed-and-greyed rather than hidden is still per-menu POLICY, and only the Transmutation submenu changed: `RuneData.choice_attacks` became the catalogue (every inscription) to match `WeaponInstance.secondary_attacks`, while `selectable_attacks` stays channel-filtered as the "what can actually fire" list the AI and `has_any_fireable_attack` consume — a declared split, commented at the source. *(Corrected 2026-10-20: `Unit.has_any_fireable_attack` has no production caller; only tests call it.)*

**RELOAD stopped being hidden-when-unavailable in [#97](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/97) (2026-09-06, dev ruling) — it LISTS AND GREYS with its reason like an attack, and the reason it could not before is exactly what that ticket had to fix**: `can_reload()` conflated "cannot reload at all" with "already full", so there was no sentence to grey it with. It takes a WIELDER now (the Chemical Spitter's rearm consumes a vial out of the inventory, the first in the game that does) and `reload_block_reason(wielder)` is the rule, `can_reload` derived from it — `can_equip_reason`'s shape.

**`Unit.has_weapon_actions()` therefore opens the KIT slice on `has_reload_verb()`, i.e. on OWNING a rearm rather than on being able to use one**, because a spitter with an empty tank and no matching vial has no other actionable row and a slice gated on availability would hide the one line that explains why; the visible cost, accepted by the dev, is that a full Carbine and a wound Springspear now light the button for a greyed row. Rev and Burrow stay hidden — a declared asymmetry, their refusals being self-evident from the weapon's own `status_text()`. Menu order is still just `ACTION_DATA`'s declaration order — now the CLOCKWISE order **within** a category, which is what preserves a stable sequence in a ring that compacts. `ACTION_DATA` also carries each verb's `group` (Move / the KIT slice / Action / Squad / Inspect / Placement) and `expands` (the ids that are only category headers, contributing their children instead of a row of their own); a verb naming a group the ring has no slice for is a row nobody could reach, which `tests/law/test_glossary_coverage.gd` refuses.

**A category whose ONE child is its own verb COLLAPSES to a terminal slice** — Move holding nothing but Move — and that same rule is the whole of "Inspect is top level": a group of one, holding the verb of its name. Deliberately not "collapse whenever there is one child", because a lone Squad Up names something the word Squad does not. **A SIXTH category joined in [#739](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/739)**, `DEPLOY_GROUP`, which held Undeploy alone -- a group of one holding the verb of its own name, so the collapse rule handed it up as a terminal slice for free. **[#772](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/772) gave it a second verb and therefore a NAME OF ITS OWN**, "Placement": the collapse rule reads the category name against its only child, so a two-verb group named after one of them is a ring labelled Undeploy that contains Undeploy. Worth knowing before adding any verb to a collapsed slice -- the rename is the cost, not the row. The PRE-MISSION ring is not a second tree builder either: `populate` early-returns into `_pre_mission_options` while the phase is open, before any gate that reads `unit.squad`, and everything downstream is unchanged.

## The ring's verbs

**Three verbs LEFT the ring in #467 round 2** and their ids went with them: Execute Orders and Cancel Actions (the queue panel's Execute button and its per-row X, plus right-click's LIFO undo) and End Turn (the corner button, the only door to ending a turn once that row was gone -- OFFERED unless it is an AI faction's turn or a squad's plan is open, #541, which is `active_squad`'s setter's reason to exist; and stood down by #722 for a cinematic pass and #739 for the pre-mission phase -- two questions, one hide: nobody LOOKING and nobody ACTING). The unit's menu is what the UNIT does; the turn is the HUD's business.

**The KIT slice is labelled after what is EQUIPPED** -- "Weapon" or "Rune", never "Attack", because it holds Reload and Burrow and carvings, none of which are attacks (#467 round 3); `MainActionMenu.category_display(group, unit)` is the one answer to what a slice is called, and it is public because three suites had spelled the old label themselves and all three reddened when it moved. **There is no generic Attack row either** — the ring lists every attack by ITS OWN NAME, which needed the MAIN attack listing too, since `WeaponInstance.secondary_attacks()` excludes it and deleting the row alone would have left a plain sword unable to attack at all; a rune's default attack IS one of its carvings, hence the name-dedupe when a group is filled. New-action checklist: the class (with its `actor_block_reason()` override, never `actor_can_perform()`) → one line each in `MAIN_ACTION_TYPES`/`SIDE_CHANNEL_ORDER` → **the player surface** → **its AI stance** in `AIArchetype`'s `MAIN_ACTION_PRIORITY`/`MAIN_ACTION_NEVER` (the law test fails until it lands in exactly one, per archetype) → **its Play API verb** (a `PlaySession` method and a bridge arm) → **its rows in the PARITY LEDGER** ([#1236](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1236), `tests/support/parity_ledger.gd`: every member of every feature list — ring verbs, action types, HUD doors, `ResolvedOutcome` fields, tile states, zone kinds and the rest — declares a Play and an AI stance, covered / never / gap, and `tests/law/test_play_parity.gd` + `test_ai_parity.gd` red on a member with none and on a claim the code no longer backs; any feature, not only a verb, owes its rows) → a `SquadPlanValidator` clause only if the action has plan-context (adjacency/occupancy) rules, mirroring `_revalidate_rescues`/`_revalidate_captures`.

**Queueing a no-argument verb goes through `game.queue_simple_action(unit, type)`** (2026-07-28): `queue_rally`/`queue_reload`/`queue_rev`/`queue_burrow` were four byte-identical copies and are now one path with a `_make_simple_action` factory — a `match`, not a class dictionary, because class references aren't const-foldable and a dict lookup loses static typing. Verbs taking a real argument (rescue takes a unit, capture takes a cell) deliberately stay separate; forcing them through one signature just moves the branching into a parameter bag. **The player surface forks by kind since the 2026-07-24 Weapon Action refactor:** a *general* verb (rescue/guard/…) still takes a top-level `MainActionMenu` slot — menu-id const + `ACTION_DATA` entry + gate clause in `populate()` + dispatch arm in `_dispatch()` (which `on_pressed` wraps, so the #471 camera return cannot be skipped by an arm's early return); a *weapon-specific* one (Rev/Reload/Burrow, Overwatch since #413, and any future family signature) instead adds a branch in `MainActionMenu._weapon_children`, and an inert `can_<verb>()` default on `EquippableData` (overridden on the family's `WeaponInstance` subclass), a thin `can_<verb>_weapon()` delegator on `Unit`, and one `or can_<verb>_weapon()` in `Unit.has_weapon_actions()` — since #467 it lands one ring out, inside the KIT slice (labelled Weapon or Rune), no matter how many signatures exist. Either way the queued `ActionType` is unchanged, so registry/resolver/queue/AI wiring is identical. Registry completeness is pinned by `tests/law/test_action_registry.gd` (every `ActionType` must land in exactly one of the fixed pipeline or `SIDE_CHANNEL_ORDER`) and `tests/squad/test_action_queue_display.gd` (queue sections follow registry order regardless of queue insertion order).

**A DERIVED type joins the fixed pipeline, not `SIDE_CHANNEL_ORDER`** — `COUNTER_ATTACK`, since #419 `TILE_HIT`, and since #922 `SINK`: nobody queues it, so it needs no menu surface, no AI stance and no `actor_can_perform`, and the registry law's `FIXED_PIPELINE` const is where it is declared. **The panel's section order is `MOVE → ATTACK → side-channels → REACTION → END OF TURN`**, that last one closing the panel because it fires after every order in the queue (which is the pass's clock) — including for a unit that never moved, which is why it is a section rather than a row indented under MOVE. Its rows are the squad's members plus, since [#1353](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1353), every other unit of the acting faction the plan touches or deposits under (`PlanResolver._end_of_turn_subjects`; dev, 2026-10-21), because the burn takes the whole faction. AoE = a **volley**: one `AttackAction` per victim, all sharing a `volley` array; secondaries set `is_secondary_hit` and skip the lunge animation.

**A volley is a RefCounted cycle** (each member holds the array that holds it), and Godot has no cycle collector. So until [#1359](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1359) (2026-10-21) every resolve leaked its derived hits: +8 objects per `resolve_plan` and +17 per hover `preview_aim`, measured, and linear. **`ResolvedPlan` now breaks every volley it holds on its own `NOTIFICATION_PREDELETE`.** Its death is the one point every drop and replacement passes through: the manager's cache, a hover's forecast, an AI hypothetical, `executing_plan`. A `release()` instead would be owed at some twenty-five call sites. Every volley builder (the aim, counter, watch-shot and payload sites) lands its members in `attacks`, `counters` or `watch_shots`, so the hook reaches all of them. `Squad`'s own breakers only ever meet queued actions, which carry no volley since #15.

**The rule it leaves: anything that keeps a derived row past its plan and reads `volley` must hold the plan.** The queue panel is the one such holder today. It re-renders from cached entries without re-resolving (a volley toggle, a restyle) while every hover replaces the cache, so `ActionQueueDisplayEntry.plan` holds the plan. Every other reader runs with its plan in hand: the resolver, `BeatSheet`, `StrikeMarks2D.from_plan`, and `ArcLightning`/`UnitMirror` under `executing_plan`. Pinned by `tests/squad/test_volley_lifetime.gd`.

## Execution order

Execution order: moves (parallel) → attacks (sequential — combo order matters for future elemental) → **reactions** (sequential) → rescues (sequential). **A reaction is a COUNTER, and a healer takes none ([#1135](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1135), 2026-09-28 — dev: *"healing on counters is too strong and doesn't make logical sense as well"*).** `SquadManager.calculate_reactions_for_squad` (ex-`calculate_counterattacks_for_squad`) walks the defending party ONCE and picks each reactor's counter target from the attacking party (C2/C3/C5). It forked on the source's `AttackData.heals` from [#148](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/148) (2026-08-07) until that repeal, a healing reactor turning inward to patch its own side; `AttackData.can_ever_counter` now refuses a heal outright, and `squad-system.md` C8–C10 is the record.

**What may TRIGGER that walk is C5's other half, and it went unenforced until [#767](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/767) (2026-09-05): a reaction answers a HOSTILE hit** (`RulesService.can_target` on the attack's actor and its victim, asked before the once-per-party ledger). A heal is an ordinary `AttackAction` whose target is an ally, so a queued heal used to make the healer's own squad the defending party — invisible for a damaging squadmate, whom the aim half refuses anyway, and a doubled heal for a healing one. Overwatch already gated this (`PlanResolver._watch_fires_on`, the trigger predicate `_watch_triggered_by` searches with) and Guard passes heals through, so the counter path was the only one of the three missing it; the gate is faction-shaped rather than heal-shaped by dev ruling, so healing an ENEMY still draws their reaction.

**The repeal cost that gate its only witness** — its one observable leak was the reactive heal, since a damaging reactor is refused by `choose_counter_target`'s own hostility check whatever the trigger — so it stays as the rule (and keeps a friendly hit off the C4 ledger) while no case can see it; `tests/squad/test_reaction_trigger.gd` keeps the trigger cases that can still fail. The stage `resolution-pipeline.md` defers is still owed to a player-authored "act after counters" (a *stored* order that defers cannot live in a derived-only list). Units downed mid-pass are queued and ejected from their squads **after** the whole pass (`OrderExecutor._process_downed_pending`), never mid-`execute` (restructuring squads mid-resolution was buggy).

**Both executors run the SAME state code since #46**: an attack's board half is `AttackAction`'s `open_playback` → `land` → `remove` → `settle`, which `execute()` takes between its awaits and `play_session._apply_attack` calls back to back, a shoved body's slide (game) vs teleport (headless) being the one declared per-host difference; the walk phase is one moment list both play, `ResolvedPlan.walk_moments`, in which a walk's SOAKING (#884) is a moment of its own at the step the resolve soaked it whenever a walk-phase shot lands on that walker (it went on at the walk's END until then, so a shock watch over a ford left the crosser wet where the preview said dry); and the pass end is `SquadManager.settle_downed` per downed unit, then `enforce_contact`. A felled unit's board overlays (its ghost, path arrow and icons) are cleared inside SquadManager's own detach, `_remove_from_squad_and_revalidate`, which a death and `settle_downed` both reach, so every host clears them through one door (#46: the headless host never did, and every later enemy turn threw a script error over the freed unit's ghost). `tests/flow/test_execute_two_hosts.gd` runs one board through both and compares every unit.

**AND A FELLED UNIT ACTS NO MORE ([#1005](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1005), 2026-09-17): R7 liveness was a COUNTER-only rule for thirteen months, so a unit the pass downed still swung, still walked, still armed a watch and still completed a rescue** -- the reported shape being a priest downed by an overwatch shot in the MOVE phase executing its queued self-heal in the ATTACK phase. `PlanResolver.actor_is_live(actor, hypo)` is the one question (read through `projected_lifecycle`, so a unit absent from the hypo falls back to its LIVE lifecycle rather than being assumed live -- the counter-only version could afford that and an authored order cannot); `_counter_actor_live` is one caller. Four surfaces, four spellings of *this did not happen*: the attack walk marks `skipped`, `resolve_move` stamps a halt at index 0, GUARD/OVERWATCH append no ward and no watch, and the side-channel tail reads `BaseAction.resolved_actor_felled` -- a stamp written AFTER `resolve_counters`, because the tail runs last and a counter can still fell a rescuer once every queue slot has gone by.

**Two traps that shaped it: a felled actor's volley is EXPANDED and then marked, never dropped** (the whiff clause looks for a derived attack with a target, so an unexpanded aim reddens the plan -- refusing Execute for a human and conceding an AI squad's whole TURN), **and `is_inert()` is kept apart from `is_refused()`** -- an inert row is legal, stays queued and dims, where folding it into `is_valid` would refuse Execute over the pass's own consequences. A downed unit being HEALED is untouched and legal ([#1002](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1002)); what is refused is the body ACTING.

**AND A HIT ON A BODY THE PASS ALREADY KILLED WHIFFS, AND STILL SPENDS ([#1350](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1350), 2026-10-22; dev, 2026-10-21: *"It should whiff and still spend, and the preview should reflect that"*).** Until then the resolver and execution disagreed about whether such a swing happens. The resolver never asked the pass's lifecycle, so it resolved a full hit and stamped the vial burn and the tank charge, which the queue row showed. Execution asked the LIVE board, and `open_playback` refused a freed target, so nothing was spent. A dead LEAD in an AoE took the whole volley's spend and its `volley_struck` with it.

The resolver decides it now. `_resolve_one` asks `projected_lifecycle(target, hypo) == DEAD` first and stamps `ResolvedOutcome.whiffed`. The stamp carries no damage, state, shove or rung, and no HP reading. It does carry the spend stamps, taken by the same `_source_base_damage` call a hit uses.

`actor_is_live` was the wrong question for a target. A DOWNED body is an ordinary target, there to be finished. Only a body the pass KILLED is gone.

A whiff is not `skipped`. The swing happens, so `open_playback` opens it on the stamp alone and `land` keeps its hands off the body. The lead still lunges, publishes the one impact and settles its spends, in both hosts. `land`'s freed-node test is a guard and decides nothing.

The derived hit keeps its target, so the AIM stays green. A whiff the pass made is legal, while an aim at a cell that is already empty is still refused at the gate.

Its readers follow the stamp:
- a Guard does not step in front of it (`_guard_for`);
- it drops no payload (`_drops_of`, ruling 45);
- it provokes no counter (`calculate_reactions_for_squad`);
- it shows no threat intent (`AIController._intent_for`);
- the queue row wears a "Whiff" chip and keeps its Vial/Tank chips, undimmed;
- the camera frames the swinger, or for a volley the victims it does reach (`BeatSheet.subject`, dev 2026-10-22: *"show the unit swinging at nothing"*).

That last one is not a nicety. The beat's subject is scheduled before the pass, so it was the corpse, and `pan_to` on a freed unit is a script error that ended the game host's pass outright. The test reproduces it with the resolver's stamp removed.

**The two hosts disagreed before this, and a Godot quirk hid it.** By the time the game host plays the second swing, a frame has passed and the body is FREED, and a freed reference compares `== null`. So the old `target != null` refusal skipped itself, and the swing played like a #47 cell attack and spent. The headless host's body was only queued for deletion, so it refused and spent nothing. The stamp is what makes them one answer.

A path swing stops at the body and whiffs. It does not reach through to the unit behind.

**The Crisis corollary (dev, 2026-10-22: each row shows what its hit does — the Crisis entry, then the kill, then whiffs).** `LethalityRules.predict` used to flag every later hit on a dead Crisis unit KILLED when it alone would have killed from pass-start HP (the 2026-06-26 call). Those hits are whiffs now, and the dead answer NONE before the Crisis branch is asked. Pinned by `tests/squad/test_whiff_on_a_felled_target.gd`, and in both hosts by `tests/flow/test_execute_two_hosts.gd`.

**The queue is filled by `game.spawn_unit` connecting `Unit.went_downed` → `OrderExecutor.on_unit_downed`, and that one line was missing until 2026-07-29** — the only `went_downed.connect` in the repo was `play/play_session.gd`'s, so the headless API ejected and the game never did. Both consumers of `_downed_pending` were therefore dead code in the game: downed units stayed squad members (so their tiles stayed walkable to squadmates — `compute_move_range` only excludes cells held by *non*-squadmates, since squads rotate through each other), a downed leader kept the squad and its cohesion leash, and **the Crisis prompt could not fire at all**.

**That wiring moved down one level in [#738](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/738) (2026-09-05) and the reason is worth keeping**: `spawn_unit` was the single door for every board-entry path (scenario load, dev spawner, TestBoard), which is why the connection belonged there beside the `unit_died` one — and the moment a SECOND way to build a unit appeared (`game.spawn_reserve_unit`, the off-board roster), one door became two and a per-door copy of the wiring would have been the identical thirteen-month hole. `game._build_unit` is the shared half now: it creates and wires and deliberately does NOT parent, because `spawn_unit` gates *after* building and frees on a refusal. Pinned by `tests/squad/test_downed_ejection.gd`. The generalizable warning: *a feature can be fully built, correct at both ends, and tested at both ends, and still never run because nobody tested the wire* — and a second live implementation of the same seam (the Play API's) is what let it hide.

## The actions/ folder

`actions/` — the player-order verbs: `BaseAction`→`Move`/`Attack`(→`CounterAttack`)/`Rescue`/`Reload`/`Rev`/`Burrow`, plus the DERIVED `TileHitAction` (#419 — a tile's own end-of-turn damage, and since #508 the soak the gas over it gives, which since #1331 can scald on the same row; one unit's rows are composed by `TileHitAction.end_of_turn`, which threads each damage row's HP into the next; never queued, like `CounterAttackAction`) and `SinkAction` ([#922](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/922) — a unit the pass's own deposits took the footing from, ice melting under it, going into the water; resolved by `PlanResolver.settle_sinks` and played when the deposits land, or at pass end for a melt only a counter or tail shot made); `OrderExecutor` (the one path a queued plan resolves through — player, menu, and AI all land in `execute_orders`; owns the deferred `_downed_pending` ejection and the Crisis offer, plus `_end_squad_turn`, the single definition of "this squad's turn is over"; **a board swap ends a pass in flight since [#661](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/661)**: `clear_board` calls its `abandon_pass()` and bumps `ScenarioManager.board_generation`, which every coroutine awaiting across a pass or a turn checks after each await, so an interrupted pass neither leaves `executing_plan` set nor plays on the board that replaced it -- `visual-clarity.md` → *A board swap ends the pass in flight*); `actions/resolution/` = the resolution pipeline (`PlanResolver`, `ResolvedPlan`/`ResolvedOutcome`/`ResolvedCellEffect`). *(Corrected 2026-10-20: the Crisis offer was deleted by #158.)*

**THE FIRST PER-ATTACK EVENT is [#887](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/887)'s `AttackAction.impact` → `OrderExecutor.volley_struck`**, and until it the diorama learned about a blow only by watching HP fall (`UnitMirror.report_impact`), which knows a number moved and nothing about what moved it. Emitted at the PAYLOAD moment (the lunge's peak since #480, so a bolt arrives when the blow does) and ABOVE the target block, so a #47 cell attack publishes too — every shock rune touches the MAP, so a shot at open water hits nobody and lights a whole river. ONE PER VOLLEY, gated on the action beside the readiness/vial/watch spends rather than at the subscriber, and `_execute_action_sequence` is the one connect site because every attack in the game passes through it (aims, counters, the walk's triggered shots). `battle3d` is the subscriber; the effect is `ArcLightning`.

**The cell-effect stage has a GROUND half and an AIR half since #1335 (2026-10-22).** `PlanResolver._resolve_cell_effects` runs a terrain reaction on a struck cell in one of two ways. A ground reaction (`_resolve_cell_effect_at`, the AI's `_map_pays` asks it too) answers only a map-hitting attack, as Targets has always said. A reaction marked `TerrainReaction.in_the_air` (`_resolve_air_at`) answers every struck cell whatever Targets says, because it happens over the cell, not in it. Both fold into one `ResolvedCellEffect` per cell through `_fire_into`. The first air reaction is `Disperse.tres` (AIR clears every gas, `ResolvedCellEffect.gas_cleared`, folded FIRST by `GasField.apply` and `projected_packed_at`, ghosted as a `{cell, clears_gas}` deposit). It fires only where the live cell holds gas, the tile gates' board-as-it-stands rule. The second is `Ozone.tres` (#1335 again: SHOCK leaves thin ozone). The air half also walks the cells a shock's CURRENT ran through, both ends of each stamped `AttackAction.arc_links` link (`_current_cells`), where the ground half and the attack's own gas do not reach. `tests/terrain/test_gas_in_play.gd` drives that through the real volley builder, falsified by emptying `_current_cells`.

**Since #1332 (2026-10-22) a hit that SHOVES blows the gas it clears, and the gas is read as the PASS leaves it.** Three changes to that stage:
- **The read.** `_resolve_cell_effects` takes `prior`, the plan's cell effects so far, and reads each struck cell's gas through `GasField.projected_packed_at` over them. That is exactly what the batch apply (`OrderExecutor._apply_cell_effects`) will have folded before this hit's own effects land, since both walk `plan.cell_effects` in append order and nothing inserts into it. Dispersal's gate reads the same value (`_resolve_air_at`'s `gas_here`), replacing the board-as-it-stands read above. Fizzle, empowerment and `air_at` keep the board as it stands.
- **The push.** `_gas_landing(action, cell, board)` walks `_source_knockback(action)` tiles along `_shove_direction(action, cell)` (the unit shove's own direction, now one function both read) through `GasSpread.can_cross`, resting on the last tile reached. A landing other than the cell itself becomes an ORDINARY deposit there (`gas_added`, `cause` the hit), so a push needs no field of its own and every reader of the channel already folds it. A cell effect can now sit on a tile the attack did not strike.
- **One effect per tile per hit.** Every struck tile's gas is read before any lands; arrivals are added after the loop, into the landing tile's own effect when the hit struck it, so a tile's own gas leaves before what is blown onto it lands and a cloud across a struck line shifts intact.
