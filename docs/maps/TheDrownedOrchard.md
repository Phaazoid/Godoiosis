# The Drowned Orchard

**CLAUDE-AUTHORED, unreviewed.** Board: `Scenarios/missions/TheDrownedOrchard.tres`. Built
2026-11-10 on `main` at v0.340.1. Medium (20 x 13), Rout in 24 rounds, Basics roster with a cap of
5. The weather is drawn at the start: Clear, Rain or Snow, a third each. Ten enemies in three
groups.

## The idea

It is the first board that draws its weather, and the first on the Basics roster with its whole
stash open. The stash holds a Fire Rune, an Electric Rune with its diamond, a Freeze rune and a
Wind Rune, and Isaac can carry all of them. The pre-mission screen shows the sky before anything
is packed. So the board's first decision is made before round 1: read the sky, then pack.

A channel of deep water splits the board. The player starts on the west bank. Everything that
attacks comes across the water, each group a different way, and the last group does not come at
all: it has to be gone to.

- **Both banks are grass.** One fire on either bank burns all of it. The channel is the firebreak.
- **The ford** is two rows of shallow water. Wading it costs two a tile.
- **The orchard** (rows 0 to 8 of the east bank) is the Pressmen's post. They do not leave it.
- **The channel is four wide.** One Freeze lays three tiles of ice, and the fourth is shallow: a
  bridge anywhere the player likes.

## The encounters

### 1. The Ferrymen (three strong solos who ignore the terrain)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Lock** | Three Water Walkers. They cross the channel on round 1 and hit on round 2, before anything else has moved. Two carry Springspears: 9 to 11 a hit, and the Spring goes through two units standing in a line. A Ferryman on 1 HP with one arm put a Spring through Sara and Noemie for 10 each. |
| **Tell** | They are the only enemies who move on round 1, and they stop on the far side's shallows in a column, one to a row: rows 3, 5 and 7. |
| **Key (board)** | They come straight down the rows they are standing on. An Overwatch lane on that row fires first: 11 or 12 to a 14 HP body. In the Snow run two lanes on the right rows left the three of them on 2, 3 and 6 HP after their charge, and all three were down on round 3. |
| **Key (verbs)** | Do not stand in a line. In Rain they are Wet when they arrive and a Freeze does 13. They end round 1 standing in open water: a Zap from two tiles away previewed as reaching two of them through it (12 each in Rain). I did not cast it. |
| **Window** | Round 2 (the lanes) and round 3 (the answer). |
| **Cost of brute force** | Lanes on the wrong rows, as in my Rain run: 31 damage and two limbs on round 2. |

### 2. The Bailiff's Men (a strong squad, the hazard present)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Lock** | A Bailiff in Riveted Mail (24 HP, DEF 6) and two cutters. They wade the ford and come out on round 5. A carbine does 5 or 6 to the Bailiff. |
| **Tell** | They spend three rounds in the water, in a clump, each one Wet from its first step. |
| **Key (board)** | The ford. One Zap from the tall grass, two tiles from the nearest of them, runs through the water into all three: 11 or 12 each, 36 damage and three right arms from one cast. Mail does not stop it. |
| **Key (verbs)** | Freeze on a Wet body: 11 to 13 a head down a row of three. Stay one tile out of their reach on the round they come out, and the first strike is yours. |
| **Window** | Rounds 3 to 5, while they wade. |
| **Cost of brute force** | Meeting them on the bank with weapons: the Clear run did that and ended the fight with Noemie down and Ross on 2 HP. |

A dry caster can stand beside the water and not be part of the chain. A Wet one cannot: in Rain the
same cast from the same tile listed Isaac among its own victims, and his counter a round later
hit two enemies, an ally and himself.

### 3. The Pressmen (a hazard squad in an orchard)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Lock** | A Press Master with Fireball, two Press Hands with maces (22 HP), and a Shot on Overwatch. They stand on grass, among trees, and hold the orchard. The Shot's lane covers the nearest bank. The Press Master's Fireball melts ice. |
| **Tell** | A fire caster standing in dry grass. The Shot's lane is drawn on the board. |
| **Key (board)** | Fire. One Fireball from the ford lights the east bank, and the channel keeps it off yours. The fire walks north a tile a round. The Pressmen stand in it: 5 from the flames and 2 or 3 from the steam, every round. In the Clear run it killed all four with nobody near them. |
| **Key (verbs)** | A Freeze bridge puts the fire where you want it, six rounds sooner. Once the steam has soaked them they are Wet and standing shoulder to shoulder. |
| **Window** | From round 9. The clock is 24. |
| **Cost of brute force** | See the two Snow runs below. The bridge you froze is where the Press Master kills you: his Fireball melted the tile under Isaac and the Shot finished him in the water. And a squad that is burning comes out to meet anyone who walks up to watch. |

## What the weather changes

| Sky | What it does here | What I packed |
|---|---|---|
| Clear | nothing | Fire Rune. Freeze does 6 to a dry body |
| Rain | every unit is Wet from round 1. Freeze does 13. A Zap chains through any two bodies that touch, the caster's included | Electric Rune and diamond, Fire Rune |
| Snow | fire carvings are weaker (the Press Master did 2 to 4 a hit, to Wet targets). Nobody is Wet until they wade or stand in steam | all three |

**Rain does not put out a fire.** A Fireball on wet grass in the Rain spread exactly as it does in
the Clear. I had built the board expecting the Rain to close the fire key. It does not; it opens
the shock key beside it.

*(Since [#1660](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/1660), 2026-11-11, it does: the rain
strikes every tile's ground with water at each round's end, so a fire lit in the Rain is steam by
the next round, and the Snow freezes the shallows from the end of round 3. The runs below were
played before that and have not been played again.)*

## How it was built and checked

- Written by `ScenarioManager.save_scenario` (authored) from a scratch script, with the board's
  own weather Clear and the three-way draw in `weather_odds`. A second load-and-save writes the
  same bytes.
- Check board: no BLOCKS and no DEGRADES.
- The twelve suite files that read every mission were run one file at a time with this board on
  disk, and all twelve pass (114 cases).
- Each weather was played on a scratch copy with that weather locked. The copies are deleted.
- **Not checked:** the board has never been opened in the windowed game. I have not seen the
  draw on the pre-mission screen, or the trees.

## What I am unsure of

- **I have not won this board cleanly on its final build.** Run 5 won with three dead. Run 2 won
  with none, on an earlier build with no clock, by lighting the fire and waiting. On this build
  that patient line should finish around round 20 of 24. I did not play it again to prove it.
- **The safe answer to the orchard is to light it and wait.** That is thirteen End Turns in the
  Clear run. The clock stops a player healing to full first; it does not make the waiting
  interesting. The dangerous answers (the bridge, walking up behind the fire) are faster and both
  cost me units.
- **The weather changes less than I wanted.** Rain is a different fight. Snow is nearly the Clear
  with a weaker Press Master. A drawn set that mattered more would need rain that damps fire, or
  snow that freezes the shallows.
- **Isaac is the whole plan.** Every key on the board is a carving and one unit carries them.
  When he lost an arm in the Rain run he lost the Fire Rune with it. Sophia and Marco are in the
  reserve; I did not try the board without him.
- **The Ferrymen moved four tiles on round 1 and six on round 2.** The stop in the shallows is
  what makes them readable. I do not know why they stop there.
- **Trees block a carbine.** Twice a shot at two tiles was refused with a tree beside the line.
  The orchard is therefore better cover for the Pressmen than I planned.

## Waiting on alchemy that is not built

- **Raised earth.** A wall on the west bank across the ford's mouth, at (8,9) and (8,10), would
  hold the Bailiff's Men in the water for one more round.
- **Rain that damps a fire, snow that freezes shallows.** Either would make the draw decide which
  key exists, not only which is strongest.
- **A water carving that lays a pool.** The Ferrymen would lose their road if the shallows they
  stop in could be frozen from the bank; today Freeze reaches three tiles and they stand six away.
