## The architecture (implemented — [LOCKED shape])

**Template + instance** (revised 2026-07-16, #59) — a weapon is **policy + geometry**, split the same way as the rest of combat (see `../../CLAUDE.md`). Policy splits again into a shared template and a per-item instance (the same id-reference shape jobs uses), and — as of 2026-07-19 (#72) — the ATTACK itself splits out from the template into its own authored content, mirroring the rune/carving system:

- **`AttackData` = the shared attack skeleton** (`extends Resource`) — identity, geometry, and combat flags common to anything a unit can fire: `display_name`, `power`, `attack_pattern`, `can_counter`, `hits_allies`, `hits_self`, `targets`, and since [#258](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/258) the vertical block: `vertical_rule` (`MELEE` — same step or a facing half step; `RANGED` otherwise), `up_tolerance`/`down_tolerance` (`-1` = unlimited — a lob's climb ceiling is its up-tolerance, a gun stays `-1`), and `arc_clearance` (how high the shot's sight line arcs over its flat course; 0 = flat, no unlimited sentinel; see [verticality.md](verticality.md) → *Targeting* and *Line of sight*). Damage math deliberately stays OFF this base (carvings scale off the wielder's aura; a weapon attack scales off its OWN `scaling_blend` plus the fitted mods of whatever fires it (#485) — there's no shared formula to share).
- **`WeaponAttackData` extends `AttackData`** — one selectable weapon attack, adding `elemental_damage_type` (a carving's elements derive from its sigils instead) and, since [#485](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/485) (2026-08-25), **`scaling_blend`** — the percentage weights across STR/DEX/PER/CON that its damage scales off. **It MOVED here off `WeaponData`, which no longer has the field at all**: a blend per family meant every attack a weapon owned scaled identically, so there was never a reason to prefer one over another. Per attack, a mod that grants an attack brings its own scaling with it, and *a family's blend simply IS its main attack's* — no fallback survives on the template, because a second copy there would be a second answer to what an attack scales off (Law #4). The weights are **normalised at read time**, so a total other than 100 is legal arithmetic and a lie on screen; `Stats.BLEND_TOTAL` is the convention, the Attack Editor's sliders cannot author anything else, and `AttackLint` flags the rest. `TransmutationData` (the rune carving) is the sibling subclass. Authored as `.tres` under `WeaponAttackCatalog`'s dirs: the general pool (`Resources/WeaponAttacks/`) plus a curated set — exactly one main attack per family/prototype in `MainAttacks/` (+ `MainAttacks/Prototypes/`).
- **`WeaponData` = the family TEMPLATE** — a family base (`ChainSword.tres`) or a named prototype (`TheJaw.tres`): `main_attack` (the one REQUIRED attack — what counters and the default aim use, a curated resource the family points at, never a bare array slot) + `extra_attacks` (additional stock attacks, no count cap — mod spaces do the gating), `weapon_type`, `is_prototype` (identity and save folder only since [#486](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/486) — it has no mechanical reader left), **`mod_spaces`** (one capacity per mod space, authored per template), `two_handed`, the prosthetic-only `built_in_stat`, and the `weight` it inherits from `Item` (its own `base_weight` retired into that on 2026-07-27, when weight moved to the base class so anything inventory-able could carry mass — [stats.md](stats.md)). NOT equippable (`extends Item` directly) and never duplicated — every weapon built on a template reads it live, so editing the template (or its main attack) updates every weapon at once.
- **`WeaponInstance` = what a unit actually carries** (`extends EquippableData`) — a direct `template` ref + this item's own mod spaces (`spaces`, one inner array of `WeaponModData` per space, sized by the template's `mod_spaces` — three named fields until [#486](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/486), which is what had capped every weapon at three however many its template authored) + the prosthetic-only `limb_kind` (moved off `WeaponData` 2026-07-19 — a shared template value couldn't let two prosthetics built on the same family be an arm and a leg at once, so it had to become per-instance) + the effective-weapon computation. `base_damage`/`get_elements`/`hits_map` all take an optional `attack: WeaponAttackData` override (null = the template's main) — a counter or an unselected attack falls back to main for free through this default. Copy one via `copy_equippable()`, never `duplicate(true)` (that would fork the shared template).
- **Per-attack readiness (BUILT 2026-07-22, [#73](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/73); third flag added 2026-07-28, [#108](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/108)):** `WeaponAttackData` carries `requires_readiness`/`consumes_readiness`/`builds_readiness` (all default `false` — every pre-existing attack is unaffected), the wind-up/recovery economy the multi-attack model was built to support. The three answer *gated / spends / banks*, and they are the **only** thing a family may consult: the Kinetic Mace had inferred its charge-spender from `knockback > 0` instead, so authoring a readiness flag on that family did nothing and any future knockback attack would have silently rewired its economy (design law #4). `builds_readiness` is what made the shortcut tempting — the original pair could say "needs one and spends one" but had no way to say "banks one", which is precisely the mace's Smash. **The lesson generalizes: a seam that can't express what content needs will grow a private second one.** The state can't live on the shared template (battle-scoped) or on `Unit` (a unit can carry more than one of the same weapon, and each must track independently — the bug an earlier design pass shipped and had to walk back), so it lives on `WeaponInstance` itself via a **per-family subclass seam**: `WeaponInstance.make()` dispatches on `weapon_type` to build the right concrete class (`SpringspearWeaponInstance` for `SPRINGSPEAR` today; plain `WeaponInstance` for every other family), and the base class declares the readiness surface as no-op virtuals (`is_attack_fireable`/`consume_readiness_for`) so families that don't use it are untouched. *(2026-07-27: the argument-free self-ability verbs — `can_reload`/`reload`/`reload_label`, `can_rev`/`rev`/`tick_rev`, `can_burrow` — moved up again to `EquippableData`, so `Unit` delegates to the equipped slot without casting to `WeaponInstance` first. The two methods above stayed put because their `WeaponAttackData` parameter can't widen without breaking every family override.)* `Unit` gained thin delegators (`is_attack_fireable`/`can_reload_weapon`/`reload_weapon`) reading whatever's equipped — no readiness state of its own. A new main action restores readiness: it shipped as `SpringLoadAction`/`ActionType.SPRING_LOAD`, and **went generic in #84 as `ReloadAction`/`ActionType.RELOAD`** the moment a second family (Carbine) wanted the same order — the body had only ever called the generic `Unit.can_reload_weapon()`/`reload_weapon()` seam, so the promotion was a rename. Per-family wording lives on one virtual, `WeaponInstance.reload_label()` ("Spring Load" for Springspear, "Reload" for everyone else); the queue, the AI tables, and the Play API all see one verb. **Every family now has its own subclass (BUILT 2026-07-22, [#82](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/82)):** six then-pure pass-throughs (`ChainswordWeaponInstance`/`DrillWeaponInstance`/`CarbineWeaponInstance`/`KineticMaceWeaponInstance`/`ChemicalSpitterWeaponInstance`/`ProstheticWeaponInstance`) join `SpringspearWeaponInstance`, and `_instance_for()`'s fallback is gone — an unmapped `weapon_type` makes `make()` `push_error` and return `null` instead of silently handing back a generic weapon. **`ChainswordWeaponInstance` gained its first real state — Rev — in [#84](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/84) (2026-07-23; family table below), proving the seam carries signature mechanics, not just readiness.**
- **In-battle selection:** `Unit.get_fired_attack()` (the live pick if the player chose one, else first-channelable-carving / main-attack) answers *"what would this unit fire if it declared right now"* — read while AIMING, and by `AttackAction.declare()` to stamp the order. Reach and ally-splash deliberately do **not** read it: since [#102](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/102) `Reach.*` and `RulesService.gather_attack_victims` take the `AttackData` as a parameter, so a queued order's geometry is derived from its own frozen stamp and a counter's from `get_counter_attack()`. One question, one answer (design law #4) — before that, a pick left over from a previous aim silently re-shaped stored blasts. `Unit.get_counter_attack()` is a deliberate exception — a rune counters with whatever it would currently fire, but a **weapon counter ALWAYS uses main**, ignoring any live pick (overwatch-style alt-attack countering is future scope, [#73](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/73)). The chosen `AttackData` is stamped onto the queued `AttackAction.fired_attack` at declare time (Law #2 provenance — the resolver reads the frozen snapshot, never re-derives).
- **`AttackPattern` = geometry** — pure cell math (selectable vs affected cells), unaffected by any of this. Terrain/LoS filtering lives at the aim gate, not the pattern: **`arc_clearance: int`** is BUILT (#258 rework, 2026-08-20 — renamed from the never-built `arcs_over_obstacles` bool by #218; a flat shot is clearance 0, so shots and lobs are one authored number rather than two categories), evaluated by `Reach.sight_trace` — one trajectory the gate checks and the in-game laser sight line draws — alongside the vertical rule (STEP/tolerances), all inside `Reach.vertical_aim_ok`. The pattern stays board-blind either way; see [verticality.md](verticality.md).
- Content lives under `Resources/Weapons/`: family bases in `MainVarieties/` (dynamically scanned — `WeaponCatalog.get_family_bases()`, no more hardcoded `TYPES` dict), prototypes in `Prototypes/`, and saved NAMED fitted instances (a specific mod loadout + a custom name — the "get attached to your weapon" feature) in `WeaponVariants/`. **All seven families + SEVEN prototypes now have real identity (`display_name`, `weapon_type`) and a NAMED main attack** — only Chemical Spitter's is still a stub awaiting real content (parked behind the materia session), and all fourteen are instantiable end-to-end via the Item Editor today. The six prototypes beside The Jaw arrived with [#111](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/111) (2026-08-26) — Bow, Sweeper, POINT, Shock Rod, Water Staff, Firespitter — and they arrived from an odd place: they had been EMBEDDED inside four pre-#59 scenarios as forked copies of family templates, each carrying its own private main attack, which is exactly the sync break the template/instance split exists to prevent (a family retuned here simply never reached them). Two more forks turned out to be plain Chainswords wearing pet names and were repointed at the shared template rather than promoted. `tests/flow/test_scenario_load_integrity.gd` now refuses a scenario that embeds a template at all — asked of the resource PATH, since Godot gives an embedded sub-resource `"<file>.tres::<id>"` rather than an empty one.

> **Enum debt — CLOSED ([#7](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/7)):** `weapon_type` is `WeaponData.WeaponType` (`NONE / CHAINSWORD / DRILL / SPRINGSPEAR / CARBINE / KINETIC_MACE / CHEMICAL_SPITTER / PROSTHETIC` — matches the seven families below exactly) and `elemental_damage_type` (now on `WeaponAttackData`, per-attack since #72) is `Elemental.Element`. Both migrated off `String`. The family list below is the canonical `weapon_type` vocabulary.

**Dev tooling (#72, 2026-07-19):** the Item Editor's type dropdown reads `WeaponCatalog.get_templates()` (the dynamic scan), so any of the eight established templates can be picked, fitted with mods, and saved as a named instance — closing the original "we can't create weapon instances" complaint. The Attack Editor tab gained two more modes alongside carving authoring, toggled at the top of one consolidated tab (a second tab felt wasteful once built): **Weapon Attack** (pool authoring — new/load/name/save, mirrors carving authoring exactly since both extend `AttackData`) and **Weapon Families** (edits an established family's/prototype's `main_attack` in place, live and un-duplicated — deliberately heavier-handed, since it changes that family everywhere; no "new" or "save as", just load-a-family → edit → overwrite). `WeaponAttackData`'s `requires_readiness`/`consumes_readiness` (#73) — and `builds_readiness` (#108) after them — are plain `@export bool`s, so both modes picked them up as checkboxes for free, on both occasions, with no tool code.

**Authoring a PROTOTYPE ([#486](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/486), 2026-08-25):** the Item Editor gained a fourth kind — a blank `WeaponData` in the type dropdown beside the blank mod, saving into `Prototypes/`. It picks the family (auto-filling that family base's `main_attack` as a **live shared ref**, so an un-swapped prototype follows its family's retunes), edits `mod_spaces`, and draws the physique reflectively. **It never authors an attack file**: swapping the main picks something the Attack Editor already wrote, which is what keeps attack authoring in one place. **The scope split against Weapon Families is declared, not accidental** — that mode already wrote `WeaponData` files (its Update writes two, the main's and the family's, since #473 gave it `extra_attacks`), so a second panel touching the same files is a Law #4 hazard unless the questions differ: this mode owns what a template **is** (family, physique, spaces), that one owns what it **swings** (tuning the main, and extras). Both edit the catalog object **live**, which is the mechanism rather than the convention — two panels holding copies of one file would let whichever saved last silently discard the other's edits. This is also why the Item Editor's read-only rule does not cover it: that rule forbids editing a shared family *through one carried item's form*, and a prototype here is edited as itself. `WeaponTemplateLint` (`weapons/`, `AttackLint`'s sibling) refuses the save on **BLOCKS** — today only an unset `weapon_type`, which makes `WeaponInstance.make()` return null so the file lists everywhere and produces nothing carryable — and reports **DEGRADES** without refusing (a missing main, matching what Weapon Families already saves happily; a space with capacity below 1). It is swept over every shipped template in CI.

**Mod authoring (#74, 2026-08-23):** the Item Editor takes a third kind — a `WeaponModData`, saved to `Resources/WeaponMods/` — and it lives there rather than in a tab of its own because that panel is already where a mod gets *fitted*, so authoring one and trying it are one screen. It is the first thing to draw a mod at all, which surfaced that `DevWidgets.build_resource_editor` has arms for int/float/bool/string/object and **none for an Array or a Dictionary**: the two grant lists and the two stat dictionaries therefore get bespoke UI, and `scaling_nudge` gained its first editor since the field was written. `DevWidgets.add_stat_dict` is the shared sparse-dictionary row where **zero means absent** — storing a 0 would grow a saved `.tres` one key per stat nobody touched.

**The scaling editor, and the family field it forced (#74 second pass, 2026-08-25).** `scaling_nudge` is now **`scaling_change`**: the storage is unchanged (a `+/-` percentage-point shift, stacked additively), but you author **ABSOLUTE** percentages on the same sliders `WeaponAttackData` uses and what is stored is the **difference from the family main attack's blend**. The dev's spec: *"STR is at 55? That means STR scaling is 55%. That's it."* **A delta rather than an absolute is load-bearing since [#485](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/485) put the blend on the attack** — an absolute would force every attack the weapon fires onto identical numbers, flattening exactly the per-attack variety that ticket exists to create. **`WeaponModData.family` is what the shift is measured against**, and its restriction is DERIVED rather than authored (*"only mods with stat bumps are forced to be weapon specific"*): a non-empty `scaling_change` REQUIRES a family, refused at the save by `save_block_reason` and swept in CI; a family set on any mod is a fitting lock either way, which is what the bank's `[CS]`/`[CB]` tags always meant. **Drift is kept and made visible** (dev call): the panel names the family's own mix above the sliders, and a stored shift follows its family when that main is retuned. **No family, no sliders** — a shift measured against nothing is not authorable at all. Fitting gained a reason channel with it: **`WeaponInstance.fit_block_reason` is the one answer and `can_fit` is DERIVED from it** ([#166](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/166)'s shape), **family asked before capacity** because a wrong family can never be fixed on this weapon while a full space can — which is why the fitting picker HIDES what the family refuses and merely refuses, with the reason, what capacity does. The sliders also **notch to `Stats.BLEND_STEP` (5)** now, remainder included, so every weight they author lands where a slider can show it.

**What a mod does to an ATTACK (#529 + #530, 2026-08-26).** The two halves #74 left were one question — *which attacks does this mod's effect reach, and what may it change about them?* — so they shipped as one seam and **#530 closed as BUILT** rather than as a second pass. **`WeaponModData.applies_to`** is the selector (`EVERY_ATTACK`, which is the storage's own zero *and* exactly the pre-#530 behaviour, so no authored `.tres` changed meaning by gaining the field; or `MAIN_ATTACK`), and it governs **every** effect the mod carries — power, element, scaling shift and both overrides — rather than a target per field, because "what does this mod reach" is one question (law #4). `WeaponInstance._mods_for` is the one place it is interpreted. **GRANTS deliberately stay outside it:** which attacks a mod CHANGES and which it ADDS are different questions, so a `MAIN_ATTACK` mod still hands over whatever it grants, it just does not buff it.

**`replaces_main` swaps the main outright** — *"the standard attack BECOMES this"*, which is how half the mod bank is written. **The MAIN only, and it is a SLOT rather than a named attack** — the distinction the first write-up of this got wrong. A mod can already name a specific attack by resource reference (`granted_attacks` does exactly that, and `Line Sniper` ships one), so identity was never the obstacle; #529's own fork list claimed it was and the canon repeated it until the dev asked. The real argument is that the two readings diverge where it counts: a slot means *whatever this weapon's main is*, which survives a prototype authoring its own main and is how every bank entry is phrased, while a named attack means *swap it if you find it* — and **a miss is SILENT**, so a mod authored against a family main would do nothing at all on a prototype that replaced it. What the named form buys is reaching an EXTRA (swapping Springspear's Spring); nothing in the bank wants that yet. **`WeaponInstance.effective_main` is the one answer**, and the *three* direct `template.main_attack` readers now route through it — #529's own text named only the counter, and `secondary_attacks` was the sharper one: filtered by the OLD main, a replacement was fired by Attack **and** listed in the submenu beside it, so the same swing appeared twice. `available_attacks` stopped calling `template.attacks()` for the same reason — that answers what the TEMPLATE carries, which is right for the Attack Editor and `AttackLint` and wrong where the main is a fact about this INSTANCE. A second replacer is refused at the **fit**, through #74's reason channel, asked between family and capacity: fixable, but not by freeing capacity.

**The EDIT vocabulary is two fields, and its boundary is the rule worth carrying: an override may only touch what is read AFTER the attack is chosen.** `knockback_delta` stacks like `power_delta` and clamps at 0 (a negative shove is no shove, not a pull); `hits_allies_override` is tri-state, and **OFF beats ON whatever space each mod sits in** — order-independent on purpose, since rearranging fitted mods must not change what an attack does (law #1), and a safety device a later mod could silently cancel is worse than none. Anything that changes how an attack is **AIMED** arrives as a whole replacement instead: `Reach._pattern_of` takes only the attack and its caller `is_directional_attack` has no unit, so threading a wielder through would reach the targeting click handler and the hover preview, to serve two designs that are honestly whole attacks anyway. Cost, accepted: **Widened Cleave Head** and **Extended Piston** author a `.tres` rather than nudging a field. Both overrides have exactly one runtime reader each (`PlanResolver._source_knockback`, `RulesService.is_attack_victim`), each asked of the ACTOR rather than the attack; **law #2 needed no work**, because `BeatSheet` previews a shove from the resolver's own output rather than from the attack field.

**Three of #529's own premises were stale** (#228's shape again, counted from the bank rather than the ticket). **Bayonet Lug** — *"the gun GAINS the 1-tile melee standard attack"* — is additive and has shipped since #74's `granted_attacks`; **Payload Doser** applies a tile state, not an attack change; and **"Pummel", which both this file and the mod bank use, is in no `.gd` or `.tres`** — the Kinetic Mace's main is **`Smash`**. The real demand was 2 replacements, 4 field edits and 2 for #530, which is why editing was built rather than deferred as the issue's own framing implied.

**Scaling authoring (#485, 2026-08-25):** the blend is edited in the **Attack Editor**, in both Weapon Attack and Weapon Families mode — which is the surface the move was made for, since a family's blend is its main attack's. Four **steal-to-balance sliders** (STR/DEX/PER/CON only — `Stats.SCALING_STATS`, which no code declared before): drag one up and the others give up the difference in proportion, so the total is *always* `Stats.BLEND_TOTAL` and an invalid blend is unrepresentable rather than policed. That is what makes the dev's rule literal — *"STR is at 55? That means STR scaling is 55%"* — instead of a claim the panel has to enforce. **Lowering a stat that holds everything is refused**: the points have nowhere to go, and an even split would author a mix nobody asked for, so the gesture is always *drag the stat you want up* — which is also what avoids the deadlock a plain total-is-full guard would create, since every weapon starts at exactly 100. `AttackLint` gained the sum as a **DEGRADES** rule for the two doors the sliders do not own (a hand-edited `.tres`, content predating them), which in turn forced its one consumer to honour both tiers rather than treat every finding as fatal. And the attack's **menu tooltip** now itemises where its number came from — `4 power + 3 from STR 60%, DEX 40%` — on `ArmorData.mechanical_text`'s precedent, reading the same `effective_blend` the damage does.

**An authored attack can now REACH a unit ([#473](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/473), 2026-08-22).** Until then `WeaponAttackCatalog`'s library had exactly ONE reader in the project and it was the Attack Editor's own load dropdown, so a saved `WeaponAttackData` was **write-only content**: it could be authored, named and saved, and no unit could ever swing it. The door is `extra_attacks`, and it belongs to the **family** — which is why it landed in the renamed **Weapon Families** mode (ex-*Weapon Primary Attacks* / `FAMILY_MAINS`, no longer mains-only) rather than the Item Editor. That was the cheaper-looking option and it is wrong: `extra_attacks` lives on the shared `WeaponData`, and `ItemEditorTool` deliberately shows a template **read-only** because the old reflection path *"silently let you mutate the shared family .tres through any one item's editor"* — attaching an extra there is exactly that mutation. Consequence of the right home: that mode's Update writes **two files** under one confirm, since a family references its main by `ext_resource` and saving one saves nothing of the other. Weapon Attack mode also reports which families carry the loaded attack, so the gap is visible rather than silent.

**Two authoring faults are silent, so they get a lint.** `AttackLint` (`Classes/weapons/`, [BoardLint](missions.md)'s shape) answers *can this attack ever be fired?* — asked by the editor before it writes a file and again in CI over every shipped attack. The verdict comes from **`Reach`, never from a pattern's own numbers**: the rule is *"an attack must be able to select at least one cell"*, and `min_range` above `max_range` is only one way to break it. Going through `Reach` also borrows the bare-fists fallback, so a pattern-less attack reads as fireable rather than being flagged, and a pattern class added later answers without touching the lint. The fault it exists for is genuinely invisible — an impossible range pair simply selects nothing, so the attack stops showing any range in game with no error anywhere, which is how the dev emptied the Carbine's main and a fresh library attack in one sitting. Its sibling half is **field tooltips on the reflective editor**: `Max Range` and `Min Range` were two adjacent lookalike spinboxes with a checkbox between them and no text on either, and the text now lives in an overridable static `property_tips()` beside the `@export` it describes (a function, not a const table, because GDScript refuses to let a subclass declare a member its parent already has).

## Cross-cutting principles ([WORKSHOP])

- **No accuracy, no crit % (Law #1).** The wiki is full of "crit chance / accuracy / dodge" — all dead. The named replacement: a **charge system / mid-battle decisions** plus the elemental **combinatrix** ([elemental-system.md](elemental-system.md)) as the deterministic stand-in for "big hits." Every "% chance to X" below is reframed as a deterministic trigger (a charge, a state, a position) or cut.
- **Scaling is a stat blend.** The wiki says each weapon scales from a mix of **Speed / Skill / Strength**. *Drift half-resolved (2026-07-05 audit correction):* `Stats.Stat` **already carries `DEX` and `PER`** (appended post-stats-session — this doc's old "no Spd/Skill" note was stale), so `scaling_stat` can point at any input stat today; Speed is dead as a stat (DEX owns the role, [stats.md](stats.md)). Still **[OPEN]**: single-stat vs *blend* scaling (a blend would live at the resolution layer), and actually authoring DEX/PER-scaled weapon content.
- **Weapon triangle — CUT (2026-07-06).** With accuracy dead and blocking dispatched (parts/jobs/gear), the triangle has no job left, and the strategy layers it exists to add in FE are already supplied (alternate attacks, elements, squads). Weapon-type advantages must **emerge** from existing gameplay or the element system — never a global matchup rule. The parts system may author matchup *content* (anti-family mods) freely. Revisit only if a meaningful home appears. *Co-dev co-signed 2026-07-11 (full agreement, struck ahead of its Stop 4 slot): the triangle is a tool to lean on later **if a gap naturally arises** that it makes sense to fill — never something to shoehorn in at any stage.*
- **Build / weight gate (captured idea).** From a dev note (FE Engage's "build"): a weapon is usable **without penalty** until a unit's build/weight threshold, penalized past it — a deterministic mobility/weight curve, not a hard lock. **[captured, unplaced]**
- **The 5th-tier power spike — FOLDED into the parts system (2026-07-06):** the spike *is* the keystone-module class (size-3, space-3-only payoffs), not a separate upgrade tree.

## The parts system — weapon customization **[RATIFIED DIRECTION — grilled 2026-07-06; numbers playtest-tunable]**

The dev's long-held frame, stated aloud 2026-07-06 (it predates most of these docs and evolved alongside the alchemy system): **weapons are the mechanist's runes.** Both combat styles reward digging deep into customization — alchemy via sigil/flourish experimentation, weapons via **physically modifying the weapon with parts**. No Kirby-64 discovery requirement on the weapon side; the depth is in the build, not the surprise.

- **Parts, not sliders.** Mods are named steampunk components — swap the external cogs for *galvanized cogs* (adds SHOCK damage), fit a *supercharge* option to the steam generator (unlocks an alternate fire mode). 99% steampunk technobabble; every mod **visually distinctive** on the weapon.
- **Mods cost authored resources** (the scrap/materia curve, [progression.md](progression.md)) — customization is an economy decision.
- **Modified weapons are harder to wield.** Higher **weapon proficiency** is required to handle heavily-modified weapons, or to access their deeper attacks — proficiency is the *license*, parts are the *hardware*. (Rides the same tier-unlock lane that already carries Revved.)
- **Weapons are multi-attack — data model + in-battle selection BUILT 2026-07-19 (#72):** like runes carrying multiple transcribed arrays, a weapon offers several attacks (`main_attack` + `extra_attacks`) chosen at order time via the same pick-menu seam runes use. **The worked example that motivated it is itself BUILT (2026-07-22, #73):** Springspear now carries **Stab** (main — a plain single-cell poke) and **Spring** (extra — a 2-tile-forward AoE hitting allies and foes alike); firing Spring leaves the spear unsprung until a **Spring Load** main action restores it. Whether the sprung state blocks the whole weapon or just re-Spring is one authored boolean (`Stab.requires_readiness`) — the issue-as-written default locks the whole weapon; flipping it to `false` leaves Stab usable while sprung. Full model in the architecture section above. Wind-up/recovery economies are the deterministic "big hit" replacement (the charge system above); mechanically generalized to any family, only Springspear authors it today.
- **Weapon-side abilities live HERE, not in jobs (dev-decided 2026-07-06).** The family of "abilities" that are really weapon behaviors — **Revved**, Drill's **Burrow**, weapon-tied defensive/block abilities, a PER-flavored overwatch on a sniper-type carbine — belong to this system. The **jobs layer** owns unit-side abilities ([grill-queue.md](grill-queue.md)); armor/non-weapon items may carry abilities too — still **unformalized**: the CON + defensive-gear grill already happened (2026-07-06, built as [#55](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/55)) but only settled DEF *scaling*, not an armor-side ability question. **2026-08-20 refinement ([standing-reactions.md](standing-reactions.md)): Guard became a leaning-basic CORE action — what stays kit-side is its brace bonus, grantable from any source; Overwatch capability is an `AttackData`-base flag either a weapon attack or a carving can author.**

### Ratified model (2026-07-06 grill; co-dev ratified 2026-07-14 — no adjustments, triangle-cut co-sign 2026-07-11)

**Core BUILT 2026-07-16 (#59):** spaces/capacities, sized `WeaponModData`, proficiency-gated activation (`UnitInstance.weapon_proficiency`, defaults to all-spaces-active so old content is unaffected), the effective-weapon computation, module weight feeding the unit Weight readout, and one working prototype (`TheJaw.tres`) are all live — see `../../CLAUDE.md`'s Known-debt entry for the full build note, including the template/instance split this session also had to add. **Item 6 (prosthetic integrated weapons), the fitting/proficiency/effective-weapon test suite, item 7 (a real fitting UI), and `WeaponData.LimbKind` are all BUILT 2026-07-17** — full state in the same CLAUDE.md entry, including a real pre-existing `space_capacities()` bug the new tests caught (that method is GONE as of [#486](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/486) — it existed only to fork on `is_prototype`, and callers read `mod_spaces` directly now) and a real hazard the old reflection-based Item Editor had (silently letting a dev edit a shared template through any one item's form). Nothing named in this session's original scope remains open on #59. **`limb_kind` moved from the template to `WeaponInstance` (2026-07-19, side-task fix):** the dev caught that a template-shared value couldn't produce an arm prosthetic and a leg prosthetic off the same family at once — `install_prosthetic(slot, item: WeaponInstance)` now gates on the instance's `limb_kind` (still resolves `item.template` into the fitting so `built_in_stat` keeps reading live), and the Item Editor grew a "Limb Kind" dropdown shown only for `PROSTHETIC`-family instances.

- **Base form:** every standard weapon works unmodified with its family's main attack (melee: one hit in front, one range; guns: authored range bands).
- **Three spaces, simple→complex:** a standard weapon has mod spaces of capacity **1 / 2 / 3** *(placeholders)* — `WeaponData.SPACE_CAPACITIES`, which is the **default** rather than the rule: since [#486](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/486) both the count and each capacity are **authored per template** (`mod_spaces`), with no cap on the count. **Modules have sizes 1–3**; a space holds any modules totaling ≤ its capacity — one keystone in the big space, *or* several small mods instead (RE4-inventory fitting is the UX fantasy; the model is capacity). Mirrors the rune two-knob doctrine (3 = the max sigil combination).
- **Proficiency N (per family) activates spaces 1..N.** A low-proficiency wielder uses a tricked-out weapon at reduced capability — never locked out. **The default is `UnitInstance.UNREDUCED` — every space, whatever the count.** It was spelled as the number 3 until [#486](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/486), which works only while three is the maximum any weapon can have: with spaces authored, a fixed number silently strands the fourth space of a wider template, and an absent key has always MEANT *no reduction* rather than *three*. Space *placement* is a build decision: what sits in space 1, anyone can use; space-3 payoffs are the specialist's.
- **Swappable loadout:** the module is the purchase (authored economy); configuration is free between missions, never mid-mission — the exact mirror of flourishes redrawn in materia.
- **Modules carry Weight** — brass has mass; heavy builds push Weight thresholds (pushability, the MOV step, swim).
- **The scaling contract holds ([stats.md](stats.md)):** scaling modules *nudge* (~10% blend shifts); full re-points are prototype/archetype territory. **As of [#74](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/74) (2026-08-25) that is a CONVENTION the dev keeps, not a rule the code enforces** — his call: *"I know the current idea behind the nudges is that mods only change these values a little bit, but the players will never see this, only me. If there are going to be exceptions, I'll be the one authoring them."* The sliders can author any mix; the ~10% band is what the bank is written to.
- **Prototypes — the archetype clause made content:** a few **named, prebuilt weapons per family** (the wiki `Weapon List` flavor bank finds its home here — the dev's pre-authored designs) that each do something **unachievable by modding the standard frame** — in exchange for an **authored space trade**, classically a single small size-1 one. Predetermined power traded against customization. **The trade became the designer's, per prototype, in [#486](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/486)** (dev: *"I could see a prototype weapon that is weaker than normal but allows extra mod slots"*) — which widens this clause rather than repealing it. The forced `[1]` was a hardcoded fork on `is_prototype`, and a prototype may now trade the other way — weaker, but roomier — so long as it is still trading. ⚠ Balance watch. This *is* stats.md's "archetype weapons break the scaling bounds."
- Riders: techniques (Revved) stay **proficiency-unlocked**, possibly item-specific (A5); alt-fire attacks don't counter unless authored to (BUILT as the counter-always-main rule, #72); data model = `WeaponModData` `.tres` composed onto `WeaponData` via the `WeaponVariants` pipeline; patterns stay pure geometry. **Mods ADD attacks as of [#74](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/74) (2026-08-23)** — `WeaponModData.granted_attacks`, composed in `WeaponInstance.available_attacks`, which reads its OWN fitted mods because an attack belongs to the weapon that fires it. That same field pass gave a mod `granted_abilities` and `stat_modifiers` (the wielder's stats, at the gear stage — never the limb stage `built_in_stat` feeds). **REPLACING and EDITING a stock attack are BUILT ([#529](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/529) + [#530](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/530), 2026-08-26) -- see the paragraph above; the family field below is BUILT too.** Module bank: [weapon-mod-ideas.md](weapon-mod-ideas.md).

Family-restricted mods are confirmed as the intended norm — already the bank's convention ([weapon-mod-ideas.md](weapon-mod-ideas.md) family tags), and CODE-ENFORCED since [#74](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/74) (2026-08-25): `WeaponModData.family` is a real field, `WeaponInstance.fit_block_reason` refuses an off-family fit with its reason, and the fitting picker does not offer one at all. **Chainsword Rev BUILT 2026-07-23** (DEF-pierce; DEF was made a real `PlanResolver` mitigation stage to give it something to bite) — the first family mechanic off this epic. **Kinetic Mace Pummel BUILT 2026-07-23** (charge→Blowback, which forced a real displacement stage). **Drill Burrow BUILT 2026-07-24** (creates Cover terrain, which filled in the Cover half of the DEF stage Rev had left stubbed — each mechanic has paid for a piece of shared substrate). Mid-epic, Burrow's arrival also triggered a **menu refactor** (dev call): rather than every signature taking its own top-level action-menu slot, Attack now always fires the weapon's default attack and a single **Weapon Action** entry opens a submenu holding the family's secondary attacks *and* its self-abilities (Blowback / Spring Load / Rev / Burrow). Menu-layer only — the queued `ActionType`s are unchanged, so the resolver, queue panel, and AI were untouched. The same grouping for ability actions (Intimidate, rune carvings) is [#88](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/88). **Carbine magazine BUILT 2026-07-25** (family row above) — its substrate payments were the **generic `ReloadAction`** (Spring Load promoted once a second family wanted it) and a **fireability term on `Unit.attack_source_can_counter()`**, which closed a latent hole predating the Carbine: a *sprung Springspear* had kept countering with Stab, an attack `requires_readiness` already barred it from choosing. **[#84 CLOSED 2026-07-25](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/84) with 6 of 7 families done** — Chemical Spitter alone remains, deliberately deferred behind the materia pass and re-filed as [#97](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/97) rather than held open in this one.
