GUARD IS BUILT ([#414](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/414), 2026-08-21); the rest is not.** Build issues: [#412](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/412) (queue order becomes real — the prerequisite) → [#413](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/413) (Overwatch, blocked by #412) + **#414 (Guard, deliberately independent — landed first, exactly as the dependency note said it could)**. All three sit on the **Demo** milestone.

**Canon checked through #450 (2026-08-26).**

**Why these exist (the throughline, dev 2026-08-19):** push the player from *"does my queued plan work"* toward genuinely predicting what the ENEMY PHASE will do — more of the puzzle living in anticipating reactive/triggered events, not just resolving an already-known queue. A reactive defense (Guard) and a reactive attack (Overwatch) are the two halves of one idea, which is why they share a grammar and a doc. Secondary motives, both real: **Overwatch is Carbine identity** ("finally give Carbines a real feel"), and **Guard is heritage** — an adaptation of the guarding feature from the GameMaker-era demo (the pre-squad parallel battle system; most of that build died, guarding was always loved).

Both mechanics are **standing policies, never prompts** ([jobs.md](jobs.md) chassis rule 3, exception-free since the BREAK repeal) — they auto-fire, deterministically, previewed. Prior canon this session confirmed rather than invented: [philosophy.md](philosophy.md) Axiom 4 already blesses "telegraphed-but-undirected overwatch"; [weapon-mod-ideas.md](weapon-mod-ideas.md) #26 (Watchman's Sear) already authored fires-once; [weapons.md](weapons.md)'s 2026-07-06 boundary already placed both weapon-side.

## The shared grammar

One lifetime rule for every standing reaction, teachable as a sentence: **a standing reaction arms when declared, absorbs exactly one trigger, and otherwise lapses when its owner's next turn begins.**

- **Declared as a main action; arms at its slot in the resolution order.** Not at end of pass — in-sequence arming is what lets queue order sequence a same-pass combo (arm the watch, then shove them into it) or dodge a self-trigger (fire your splashy AoE *before* your Guard arms). R6's order-is-the-lever doctrine, paying rent.
- **Absorbs exactly one trigger, then spent.**
- **Untriggered, it lapses when the owner's faction's next turn begins** — "the enemy phase ends" generalized to N factions (TurnManager cycles any number of teams); it covers every hostile faction's phase in between. Consequences: no fire-and-forget turret builds (a watch that outlives its owner's attention is a different mechanic — a mine — and a deliberate non-goal), and during your own pass the only live standing reactions are ones armed *this* pass.
- **The anchor rule.** Overwatch: the owner leaving its anchor cell drops the watch, however that happens (shove, rescue carry, lifecycle) — the footprint is frozen geometry aimed from that cell. Guard: the *pair* separating beyond the Guard's range drops it (see Guard below — deliberately range-shaped, not cell-shaped).

**The listening grammar** — three verses, one per reaction kind, and they never collide because each listens to a different thing:

> **Counters listen to authored attacks. Watches listen to entries. Guards listen to hits on their ward.**

Corollaries, each load-bearing:
- **A derived attack is never counter-bait.** A triggered overwatch shot draws no counter (its victim's retaliation is the rest of their own turn); a counter draws no counter — one derivation level, ever, matching [squad-system.md](squad-system.md) C1–C10 untouched.
- **Watch-to-watch chains are legal.** A watch's shot with knockback throwing the crosser into a second watch triggers it — that is the entry rule applied honestly, not a counter chain. Deterministic overwatch pinball, fully previewed; every link is authored content (knockback on an overwatch-capable attack), so degenerate chains get fixed in content, not rules.
- **A substituted hit is never Guard-bait** — no substitution chains. **ON HOLD, not buried** (dev, 2026-08-20): there may be silly edge cases where a tank conga is *wanted*; the door stays open, nothing ships through it yet.

## Queue order becomes real ([#412](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/412))

The structural fork the Overwatch scratchpad entry left open, resolved: **cut (b)**. Move orders resolve in queued order — the order they sit in the queue is the order the resolver walks them — and the player may **drag-reorder** queued moves. Units are still *shown* moving together; simultaneity survives as presentation. This extends **R6** ([resolution-pipeline.md](resolution-pipeline.md)): attacks already resolve sequentially in queued order as the declared skill expression, and moves now join them. The queue is the clock for every mid-move event — a watch trigger is the first customer; future on-entry mechanics (traps, fire-on-entry) inherit the same clock.

What deliberately does not change: **whole-plan validation** (destination conflicts and formation rotations are order-independent questions — two squadmates swapping cells stays one legal simultaneous fact; transient ally co-occupancy during ordered playback is the rotation fiction, already legal since allies never block); **Law #2 machinery** (a reorder is a plan edit riding the synchronous resolve→repaint chain); **Group Move as one authoring gesture** (the solver's member order becomes the *default* row order, individually reorderable after — member order is now mechanically real, no longer cosmetics).

The rejected cuts, for the record: **(a)** spatial precedence ("nearest crosser along the line eats it") was a proxy for time that could contradict the watched story and didn't generalize to tile watches; **(c)** lockstep walk-time (earliest path-step entering the footprint eats the shot) was offered and declined — the dev wants explicit order as the mechanic, the bigger design space. If (b) ever needs revisiting, whatever replaces it must *replace* the clock, never sit beside it as an override (Law #4).

## Overwatch ([#413](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/413))

**A queued attack that doesn't fire on declaration — it stands watch and fires when an enemy enters the watched cells during another faction's phase.** Not a surprise, ever: the footprint is telegraphed on the board, and a queued move that would cross a standing watch shows the shot it triggers in the queue immediately.

- **The watch is a pre-aimed shot.** Declaring = aiming an attack through the **normal targeting flow** — same menu, same facing pick, same overlay. The watched footprint = that aim's affected cells, frozen at declare time with the attack itself (the `fired_attack` stamp pattern, Law #2 provenance). *"Tile or line" is not a new choice*: a directional pattern watches its whole spread for the chosen facing, a point-targeted attack watches the aimed cell — the existing targeting-mode split, verbatim. The sniper fantasy ([weapons.md](weapons.md): "PER-flavored overwatch on a sniper-type carbine") is just a longer authored pattern. A watch cannot reach cells the attack couldn't hit — deliberate; a trap divorced from weapon reach is a different mechanic.
- **Capability is an authored flag on the `AttackData` BASE** — so a carbine's `WeaponAttackData` and a transmutation carving carry it through one mechanism, like `heals` and `deals_no_damage` before it. Whether base carbines have it stock or a mod grants it (Watchman's Sear's original job) is a content call at build time; the flag authors both.
- **Fires once, then spent, spending one magazine round.** Watchman's Sear's own "first enemy entering the line takes the shot," now canon. Fires-at-every-crosser is future keystone-mod space ("suppressing fire"), not v1 — one shot is what makes *who crosses first* the whole game, bounds the ordering problem to one event, and rhymes with Guard's one-hit consumption.
- **Trigger: an ACTIVE enemy of the watcher enters any watched cell, however it got there.** Walking or displacement — **shove combos are intended design** (mace them into your squadmate's line; queue order decides whether the combo connects, since the watch arms at its slot). Riders, each deliberate: allies never trigger a watch, but a triggered shot **is** the attack, full stop — ally splash lands if the attack has it, previewed, your fault; arming does not fire on units already standing in the footprint (the trigger is *entry*); moving between two watched cells is an entry (no safe repositioning inside the line), stepping out is not; **downed bodies don't trigger** ("probably" — held open pending AI self-preservation exploration; the accepted cut is that you can't spend a watch by throwing a corpse through it); **non-movement entries don't trigger in v1** — rescue placement, future spawns — with one recorded future exception the dev wants for comedy: *a rescue delivering its patient into the line of fire, triggering the shot*.
- **Basic shots don't interrupt the move.** Damage lands at the crossing moment (the crosser's later actions resolve at post-shot HP, and a second watch down the same path only triggers if they're still standing); the crosser continues to its destination. A crosser the shot **downs** stops at the crossing cell — lifecycle, not a new rule, and `LethalityRules.predict()` already answers it in preview. **Stopping power is an authored per-attack flag** beside the readiness flags (default off; mods grant it; whether it should be *default* is a playtest question answered by flipping content, never code — dev, 2026-08-20). **Involuntary displacement ends a move** — you can't resume a path you were thrown off, so a knockback overwatch shot is stopping power in disguise. What a halted-but-alive unit's remaining queued orders do is **parked with the stopping-shot content pass** — basic overwatch never raises it.
- **Queue representation: an annotation on the crossing move's own row** ("triggers Overwatch — takes 12 from Carbine (E)"), stacking when one route crosses several watches. A **deliberate divergence** from how counters display (they have their own section): chosen because #412's whole payoff is dragging a move row and watching who eats the shot change — the feedback must live on the row being dragged. The watcher's own declaration is an ordinary main-action row.
- **Board display: always visible, both directions** — your watches to the enemy, theirs to you (Axiom 4's telegraph; the *victim* stays undirected). Its own visual vocabulary: crosshair/flash-shaped threat marks, **not** a filled-square range layer (dev: "they shouldn't just be filled in squares"). Exact look is build-time work behind knobs. **Parity is mandatory** — this is gameplay information; it reaches both the 2D and 3D views, no [#292](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/292) asymmetry.

## Guard ([#414](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/414)) — **BUILT 2026-08-21**

**A main action: pick a unit in range and become its bodyguard — the next hit that would land on them lands on you instead.** Named **Guard**, not "Defend," to stay distinct from DEF the stat (Glossary entries at build time keep the two legible side by side); it **absorbs** [jobs.md](jobs.md)'s unbuilt "Guard redirects" follow-up — one mechanic, many sources, rather than a jobs-side guard and a weapon-side defend answering the same question twice (Law #4).

- **Access — the working model (a lean, playtest-subject; the one deliberately-unresolved fork on Guard):** Guard is a **basic main action everyone has**. What kit grants — weapons (the floated Aegis-class drill), jobs, armor, carvings, source-agnostic — is the **brace bonus**: bonus DEF applied to the absorbed instance; bare Guard substitutes at +0. Every other ruling in this doc is gate-independent, which is what lets this fork stay open through playtest without destabilizing anything. Implementation note for whenever it's picked up: the ability union (`add_live`, innate→jobs→gear) is the only existing multi-source "does the kit grant X" seam — the incumbent, per Law #4; weapons-as-ability-source is the one blocked decision it touches, and it opens when the first weapon-granted bonus ships, not before.
- **Total victim substitution.** The blocked hit resolves against the blocker as if they were the target — the blocker's DEF and armor **plus the brace bonus**, and the *entire* payload: damage, elemental states, knockback, all of it, resolved from the blocker's own cell. The ward takes nothing, ever. It's a per-hit hook in the resolver (Iron Will's shape — instantaneous within one resolution, not a `StatEffect`), so every pipeline stage just runs with a different victim and the preview shows the blocker's real projected HP and lethality rung. Why substitution: it's what carves the tank role — bodyguarding with your armored unit *works* because the tank's mitigation applies; bodyguarding with a second medic is a sacrifice. Bleed-through-to-the-ward was considered and **dropped, not deferred** — the brace bonus is the one block-quality knob.
- **The pair is unit-and-range, not cells** (dev correction, superseding the scratchpad entry's own "must stay in the cell" disarm): the Guard targets a specific **unit** and holds while that unit is **within the Guard's range** of the blocker — range authored on the granting content, default 1. An AoE wind blast shoving both back a cell together keeps the ward; separation beyond range drops it, however it happens. Range is checked at each hit's resolution moment, so mid-pass displacements between sequential hits move the answer, previewed. (The rejected literal reading — "protect whoever stands on the square" — would let an enemy shoved onto the vacated cell inherit your tank; unit-and-range kills it for free.)
- **Catches ANY damaging hit on the ward** — authored attack, counter, overwatch shot, hostile or friendly — except an already-substituted hit (chains on hold, above). Heals and `deals_no_damage` utility pass through (nobody wants the tank intercepting the medic's heal). Friendly-fire coverage is player agency, not a foot-gun: the Guard arms at its queue slot, so sequence your splashy attack before it.
- **"Can't defend while moving" (dev ruling, blessed as structural design):** no Guard is ever live during its own faction's move phase — last turn's lapsed at turn start, this turn's arms after moves. So a mover crossing a watch is never Guard-covered: **Overwatch owns punishing voluntary movement, Guard owns the stationary window**, and the overlap exists exactly where the enemy manufactures it — an enemy shoving your ward through a line on *their* phase fires a shot your live Guard CAN block.
- **AoE ruling — one blast, one moment.** Within a single attack's volley, standing-reaction liveness (range + lifecycle) reads **at the volley's start**; the damage packets then thread HP as usual. A blast covering blocker and ward double-bills the blocker — his own share at normal mitigation plus the ward's at the brace bonus — and if the sum downs him, **he goes down having blocked**. Chosen over sequential-sibling threading because that would let the medic's life hinge on volley *gather order* — an order the player neither authors nor sees, the exact opposite of #412's order-is-a-player-lever doctrine. This narrows [resolution-pipeline.md](resolution-pipeline.md)'s deferred volley fork for reaction liveness only; tile-state threading within a volley stays deferred.
- **Stacked Guards are legal** — two blockers may ward the same unit; the earlier-queued Guard absorbs the first hit. Queue-order vocabulary settling precedence, no refusal rule.
- **The counterplay set, all authored, all previewed:** a pierce flag on the attack (`AttackData`, the scratchpad's own idea); shove the *blocker* out of range (note a knockback hit on a Guard shoves the blocker — the disarm is a combo target); shove the *ward*; or **AoE the cluster** and double-bill the tank. Bodyguarding forces clustering; area damage is its natural predator.
- **Presentation:** the ward link rides the **ground marker channel** ([#346](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/346)'s rule — ground = the interaction): a shield icon under the defendee with a connecting mark from the defender, one clear mark under the restraint doctrine, both views or declared on #292. **BUILT as specified ([#450](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/450), 2026-08-26)** — the shield is the defendee's alone, and the connector is the project's own path-arrow trail run one step from the defender (dev call: *the same arrows we use for everything else*), tinted by `OverlayManager.GUARD_LINK_MODULATE`, which ships neutral white so the Game tab's picker is the whole colour. The arrow atlas has no tile for a diagonal or a gap, so the link is drawn only across one orthogonal step; `GUARD_BASE_RANGE` is 1, and a pair a shove has dragged apart keeps its shield and loses its arrow. **The queue row wears the same shield as the board** — one texture, one answer to what Guard looks like. The **slice-one block moment reuses the existing attack lunge** — defender lunges toward defendee at the block; the *visually loud* jump-in-front the scratchpad wanted is saved for battle-zoom animations (noted on [#358](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/358)'s sprite-FX stack — no bespoke animation machinery ahead of it).

### How it was built (#414, 2026-08-21) — and the rulings the build had to make

**Substitution is a VICTIM REWRITE inside the resolve pass, not a new stage.** `SquadManager.resolve_plan` already expands every stored aim into *derived* `AttackAction`s each pass (the #15 rule), so the resolver rewrites the derived hit's `target` to the blocker and stamps `AttackAction.blocked_for` with who it was aimed at. That is this doc's own sentence — *"every pipeline stage just runs with a different victim"* — made literal: DEF mitigation, elemental immunity, Iron Will, the knockback direction and landing, the elevation stamp, the lethality rung, execution's playback, the headless twin and the queue row all inherit the substitution with no second copy of anything. The player's stored aim is untouched.

**Liveness is ONE query fed by two projections of the same fact** (`ResolvedPlan.guards`, in arm order): wards armed in an EARLIER pass, copied off the units oldest-first, plus Guards queued in THIS plan, appended by `resolve_plan`'s own queue walk when it reaches their slot. That append point *is* "arms at its queue slot" — an attack expanded earlier simply never sees the entry, with no index bookkeeping and no second authority. The relationship is `MoveAction` ↔ `movement.cell` through `get_projected_destination()`, not a duplicate seam. The pass mutates COPIES; the live ward is spent by execution.

**A Guard that ate its owner's own splash arms ALREADY SPENT.** Side-channel verbs execute after the attack phase, so without this the queue would preview a used Guard and execution would hand back a live one. The resolver stamps `GuardAction.resolved_spent` each pass (the R8 "write the outcome onto the action" pattern, for an order with no `ResolvedOutcome` of its own) and the queue row says so.

Three rulings the doc did not spell out, decided at build time and open to argument:

- **The blocker's party counters, not the ward's.** `calculate_reactions_for_squad` reads the hit's victim, which is now the blocker — total substitution applied honestly. It only differs from the old behaviour when blocker and ward are in different squads.
- **A Guard never shields anyone from its own blocker's swing.** Only reachable through an AoE counter (one main action per turn means a unit can never both Guard and attack), and absorbing your own attack on someone else's behalf is incoherent rather than funny.
- **Wards are allies, alive, self excluded; range is manhattan distance with no line-of-sight and no height gate** — Guard is a stance, not an attack. A DOWNED ally stays a legal ward on purpose: a body waiting on a rescue is exactly what someone might stand over.

Shipped beside the mechanic: `AttackData.pierces_guard` (the authored counterplay flag), `Abilities.Id.BRACE` + `BRACE_DEF_BONUS` riding the ability union and authored onto **Bulwark Plate**, `GUARD_BASE_RANGE`, a ground-channel ward marker (real shields as of 2026-08-21, cut from `ProjectUtumno_full` row 38 — the dev's picks; #450 then made it **shield-plus-link** as the design always asked, so col 35 is the one surviving cut and col 30 was deleted with the row's second shield), and the live ward in the save snapshot as an entry-index re-link. **The access fork stays open**: Guard ships basic-for-everyone, and every ruling above is gate-independent, so playtest can still flip it.

## AI — deferred whole (dev, 2026-08-20)

Both new action types ship declared **`NEVER` in every archetype table** (the `tests/law/test_ai_action_coverage.gd` partition demands a stance; NEVER is legal, absence is red). The entire AI side waits for the next big AI pass and is filed on [#117](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/117) as one standing-reactions entry: watch-aware pathing (footprints join fire as hazards moves should prefer to avoid), crossing-order smarts under #412 (feed the tank first), a Guard builder, defended-target deprioritization (the scratchpad's threat-range idea — a *preference, never a ban*, [#57](https://github.com/Phaazoid/Godoiosis/issues/57)'s downed-deprioritization shape), and a Sentry watch stance (idle at post, watching the approach — the natural first builder). Known, accepted cost: until that pass, enemies play neither mechanic, so playtest reads on *facing* them wait.
